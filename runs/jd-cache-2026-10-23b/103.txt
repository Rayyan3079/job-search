Job Description
 What you´ll bring 
 Student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences
 Preferred fields of study: computer science or related fields
 Programming languages: proficiency in an object-oriented language (e.g. Java); knowledge of ABAP is a plus
 Language skills: fluency in German and English
 Structured approach to problem-solving
 Openness to using AI tools as part of your daily workflow
 Availability to join the team in the office in Walldorf on a regular basis
 What You´ll Build
 Are you a student looking to gain hands-on experience in a production cloud environment? Join our team and help shape the tools we rely on every day.
Our team's mission is to bring git-based development processes to customers of ABAP Cloud offerings. This spans a broad range of topics and technologies — from a backend service developed and operated following DevOps practices, to contributions to continuous integration tools, to end-user-facing applications. On top of that, our work extends to Sovereign Cloud environments — which shapes how we approach development, operations, and tooling.
 Your Responsibilities Include
 Develop features across frontend and backend — used in production by internal operators and external customers
 Work closely with experienced developers, including participation in code reviews and team discussions
 Present your work to stakeholders
 Create end-user documentation
 Challenge existing solutions and propose alternative approaches when appropriate
 Where you belong
 You will join the Repository Management team, a diverse team of six developers with different cultural backgrounds. We value questioning existing habits, discussing different solutions, and being curious about the unknown.
Additional Information
 Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages.
 This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
