The Blade Sourcing team is responsible for supporting the strategic procurement of materials and external services for the Nordex Group. Working closely with an international and cross-functional team, the department drives data-based decision-making, reporting activities, and procurement performance management. As the Working Student Blade Sourcing, you will support reporting, analysis, and data management activities while contributing to projects with direct visibility to management.
 YOUR TASKS 
 Support the preparation of monthly management reports and ensure the accuracy and consistency of reporting data 
 Perform variance analyses as part of the monthly reporting process for procurement management 
 Create ad-hoc analyses and data evaluations to support decision-making processes 
 Assist in the development and continuous improvement of Microsoft Excel tools and BI reports 
 Support the maintenance, validation, and updating of master data within relevant systems 
 YOUR PROFILE 
 You are currently enrolled in a degree program in Business Administration, Business Informatics, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable field at a German university 
 You have very good written and spoken English skills (minimum B2) ; German language skills are considered an advantage 
 You possess strong analytical skills and a quick understanding of complex data and business relationships 
 You have advanced Microsoft Excel skills and are confident using the Microsoft Office suite 
 Knowledge of SQL and Power BI is beneficial 
 You work independently, in a structured manner, and with a strong focus on achieving results 
 You are highly motivated and eager to contribute within an international team
