Who We Are 
 AI is changing how software gets built. Code production is becoming a commodity. The focus is shifting from writing code to specifying, orchestrating, verifying, and governing change, and the toolchain is the new constraint.
 We are at the center of this shift. We build Develocity, a toolchain observability and intelligence platform used by some of the world’s leading software organizations: Netflix, Airbnb, Spotify, SAP, major global banks, and hundreds more. Develocity helps software teams achieve delivery excellence through deep observability, build and test acceleration, and AI-powered intelligence across the entire toolchain, with support for Gradle Build Tool, Apache Maven, sbt, npm, Python, and Bazel.
 We are an AI-native company. AI is not a feature we are bolting on, it is central to how we work, how we think about our product, and where we are heading. Develocity encodes a decade of build and test expertise into a context engineering layer: the ground truth AI agents need to change code safely, and the governance to attribute and gate that change at machine speed. Trust, evidence, and explainability are at the core of everything we build.
 We have partnered with the Apache Software Foundation, the Commonhaus Foundation, the Micronaut Foundation, and other OSS projects such as Spring, Quarkus, Kotlin, JUnit, AndroidX, and many more to bring the values of Develocity also to the OSS Community.

 Who You Are 
 You are studying for a master’s degree and want to see how a remote-first technology company really runs day to day. You like variety: one day might be a customer’s supplier questionnaire, the next a recurring task you’re automating with Claude. You enjoy working out how a process works and then making it better.
 You work independently and in a structured way, and you’re comfortable asking questions and working with Security, Legal and Finance to get something done. You care about the details, because supplier registrations and internal records need to be right. You’re curious about AI and automation tools, and you want to use them on real work instead of just reading about them.
 If you meet most of the qualifications below and the role interests you, apply. Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter more to us than a perfect match.
 Responsibilities 
 We are looking for a working student to support our Operations and Financial Management department. The focus of this role is supporting the supplier registrations requested by our customers and helping us build and improve AI-supported workflows across the department. 
 Your responsibilities include, among others:
 Supporting the registration of Develocity as a supplier in our customers’ procurement portals, including completing recurring questionnaires and gathering supporting evidence, in coordination with the Security, Legal, and Finance teams.
 Learning our supplier registration process in depth, helping to implement improvements, and keeping the guides and training material up to date.
 Building, testing, and improving AI-supported workflows with Claude, as well as with common automation tools, to reduce recurring manual work.
 Helping to introduce new software tools, including writing short guides and answering questions from the teams.
 Assisting our Business Administration team with recurring administrative tasks and keeping our internal records and systems up to date.
 Supporting our Finance teams from time to time.
 Taking on other tasks across the department as they come up, which lets you get to know several areas over time.
 Minimum qualifications 
 Enrolment in a master’s program, for example in business administration, business informatics, financial controlling, or a comparable field.
 Basic understanding of business administration, attention to detail, and analytical thinking.
 Flexibility and an open mind, as the role combines very different tasks from several teams.
 An interest in improving current systems and processes, and you enjoy working with automation and AI tools such as Claude Code.
 An independent, focused, and structured way of working, along with a collaborative mindset and good communication skills.
 German at C2 or native level, English at B2 level or higher.
