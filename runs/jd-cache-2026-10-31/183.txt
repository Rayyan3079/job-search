Job Description
 Are you in your first year of your Master's degree and looking for a working student role where you can contribute to real production systems, learn from experienced engineers, and explore the intersection of backend development, data engineering and AI? Then this could be the right role for you.
 About The Team
 The Product Creation & Assignment team at idealo builds the systems behind the automated expansion of our product catalogue. Products are the comparable entities users see on our platform — pages where offers from different shops are grouped, enriched and made comparable. We build the systems that identify and create new products from various data sources, and assign incoming shop offers to the right existing or newly created products.
We work on a modern, cloud-native stack combining JVM-based backend services with data-intensive AWS workflows: Java, Kotlin, Spring Boot, Kafka, MongoDB and EKS, as well as Python-based pipelines using MWAA, Lambda, Step Functions, DynamoDB, Glue Spark and S3. We integrate ML models and GenAI components into our production systems and actively use AI tools such as Claude Code in our daily development workflow.
 About Your New Role
 As a working student, you will contribute to meaningful work alongside a senior engineering team. Depending on your background and interests, your work may include:
 Supporting the development and improvement of backend services and data workflows for automated catalogue expansion. 
 Working on product data quality — helping improve matching logic, data completeness and pipeline reliability. 
 Exploring how ML models and GenAI components (such as LLM-based matchers) can be integrated into production systems. 
 Contributing to tooling, observability and automation that makes the team more effective. 
 Participating in code reviews, pairing sessions and team rituals — learning how a production-grade engineering team operates. 
 Job Requirements
 Skills & Requirements
 Currently enrolled in a Master's programme in Computer Science, Data Science, Software Engineering or a related field — ideally in your first year. 
 Solid programming foundation, ideally in Java, Kotlin or Python. 
 Some experience with backend development, data pipelines or cloud services — through coursework, personal projects or a prior internship. 
 Curiosity about data-intensive systems, ML integration or AI-assisted development. 
 Good communication skills and comfortable working in English and German.
