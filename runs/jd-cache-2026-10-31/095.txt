Job Description: AI/ML Trainer
 Experience: 1–2 Years
 Work Location: Bhubaneswar, Odisha
 Work Mode: Onsite
 Employment Type: Full-Time
 Role Overview
 We are seeking a skilled and enthusiastic AI/ML Trainer with 1–2 years of experience in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, model development, and/or technical training. The ideal candidate should have strong programming skills, practical knowledge of machine learning algorithms, and the ability to deliver structured, hands-on, industry-oriented training.
 The candidate will be responsible for conducting technical sessions, mentoring learners, guiding them through real-world AI/ML projects, and developing their practical understanding of data preprocessing, model building, evaluation, and deployment.
 Key Responsibilities
 - Conduct structured training sessions on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and related technologies.
 - Deliver hands-on sessions on Python programming, data analysis, and machine learning algorithms.
 - Explain supervised and unsupervised learning concepts with practical examples.
 - Guide trainees in data collection, preprocessing, feature engineering, model training, and evaluation.
 - Demonstrate the use of AI/ML libraries and frameworks through practical exercises.
 - Develop training materials, coding assignments, assessments, and project-based learning modules.
 - Mentor trainees in developing real-world AI/ML applications and end-to-end projects.
 - Evaluate trainee performance through coding assessments, assignments, and project reviews.
 - Introduce learners to model optimization, deployment fundamentals, and industry use cases.
 - Stay updated with emerging AI/ML technologies, tools, frameworks, and industry practices.
 Required Technical Skills
 - Strong proficiency in Python programming.
 - Understanding of mathematics and statistics fundamentals relevant to machine learning.
 - Practical knowledge of NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib.
 - Understanding of supervised and unsupervised machine learning algorithms.
 - Familiarity with Scikit-learn for model development and evaluation.
 - Knowledge of data preprocessing, feature engineering, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).
 - Understanding of model evaluation metrics, overfitting, underfitting, and cross-validation.
 - Familiarity with Jupyter Notebook and Google Colab.
 - Basic knowledge of SQL and data handling.
 - Understanding of deep learning concepts and frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch is an advantage.
 - Familiarity with AI application development, model deployment, and Git/GitHub is preferred.
 Eligibility Criteria
 - 1–2 years of relevant experience in AI/ML development, data science, or technical training.
 - Hands-on experience with Python and machine learning projects.
 - Ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly and deliver practical training sessions.
 - Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and presentation skills.
 - Ability to mentor students and guide them through project-based learning.
 - Willingness to work onsite at Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
 Preferred Profile: Candidates with experience in AI/ML project development, academic or corporate training, data science projects, deep learning, or AI application development will be preferred. Relevant certifications and a demonstrable project portfolio will be an added advantage.
