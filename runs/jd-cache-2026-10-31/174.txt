We are hiring an Intern (m/f/d) – AI-Driven Optimization to join our Generative 3D MODSIM R&D team.
In this role, you will help shape the next generation of Generative Design technology within Dassault Systèmes. You will bridge cutting-edge AI techniques with finite element simulation and multiphysics optimization, driving innovation that redefines how engineers design the products of tomorrow.
 Starting date for the internship is 01st of April 2027 in Karlsruhe.
 Role Description And Responsibilities
 Research and develop AI/ML multi-fidelity approaches for non-parametric optimization processes (e.g., enabling the generation of highly detailed structures based on coarser finite element meshes).
 Participate in the development and implementation of structural optimization algorithms.
 Prototype and test implementation of algorithmic routines in a C++ and Python environment.
 Perform modeling within the context of the Finite Element Method (FEM).
 Investigate, evaluate, and test cutting-edge methods in the fast-moving field of AI and Machine Learning.
 Optionally, combine your internship with a subsequent Master's Thesis on advanced Generative Design topics.
 Qualifications
 Currently pursuing a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Aerospace, Computational Engineering, or a related field.
 First practical or theoretical experience with CAE (Computer-Aided Engineering) and FEM.
 Initial experience with AI, Machine Learning, or related data science technologies is preferred.
 Solid understanding of structural mechanics, mathematics, and numerical methods.
 Programming skills in Python and/or C++ are advantageous.
 Strong fascination for simulation-based optimization and generative engineering.
 Self-motivated, goal-oriented mindset with high team spirit.
 Very good communication skills in English and German (both written and spoken).
 What’s In It For You
 Deep insights and practical hands-on training with industry-leading CAE solutions, including 3DEXPERIENCE MODSIM, Tosca Structure, Abaqus, fe-safe, and CATIA.
 Flexible working hours.
 Collaborative environment working alongside highly qualified, dedicated, and diverse engineering teams.
 Clear opportunities for professional development, including access to our 3DEXPERIENCE University and LinkedIn Learning programs.
 Additional benefits & perks (e.g., Urban Sports Club membership discounts, fresh fruit, drinks, and coffee at the office).
