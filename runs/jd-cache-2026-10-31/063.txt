Job Description
 What you bring
 You're a student ready to contribute alongside your studies. You're curious, adapt quickly, and stay resilient as priorities evolve. You think creatively, communicate clearly, and collaborate well across teams. You're building your foundations in business and AI — and you take initiative to apply them. You bring emotional intelligence and cultural awareness to every interaction.
 Currently enrolled student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences, with a preferred focus in computer science, informatics, mathematics, science, or related fields.
 Programming & Technical Skills: Solid understanding of software engineering fundamentals, complemented by hands-on experience in web development, API integration, and programming languages including JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Python, and HTML/CSS.
 AI & Advanced Technologies: Interest or initial experience in Generative AI, large language models, AI agents, and multi-agent systems, where familiarity with LLM tuning, prompting, evaluation, or RAG-based applications is a plus.
 Additional IT & Professional Experience: Related IT, software development, and/or SAP experience is preferred.
 Soft Skills & Working Style: Independent, structured, proactive, and self-starting way of working, paired with strong communication, teamwork skills, curiosity, and a willingness to learn new technologies quickly as a fast learner and team player.
 Languages: Fluent in English, with German language skills preferred.
 Working from the Walldorf office is required.
 What You´ll Build
 Develop and enhance web applications, internal tools, and AI-powered solutions. Implement features using JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, Python, HTML/CSS, and APIs. Build and integrate LLM-based applications and AI agents. Work on agent-to-agent communication and multi-agent workflows. Support LLM tuning, prompt optimization, evaluation, and model performance improvement.
 Integrate external services, APIs, and AI models into existing applications
 Support testing, debugging, and quality assurance
 Work with Git and modern software development practices
 Contribute to dashboards, analytics, and reporting solutions such as Power BI
 Translate technical requirements into practical solutions
 Take ownership of smaller development tasks and collaborate with developers and project teams
 Role Setup
 Part-time position alongside studies. Intended for a student who already has practical technical experience.
 Opportunity to work on real software and AI use cases
 Strong focus on hands-on development, experimentation, and ownership
 Where you belong
 The role is in the SAP COO board area, Corporate Processes & IT (CPIT) and the unit AI, Data & Platform (AIPD). Our vision is to bring the Autonomous Enterprise to life at SAP and act as Customer Zero to SAP.
SAP’s Intelligent Data Management (IDM) unit owns SAP’s enterprise data management and runs product management for master data, and within IDM, one team drives various strategic topics, especially leveraging AI for automation.
They are a team of Project Management and Subject Matter Experts with knowledge and expertise on Intelligent Data Management Strategy Programs and Projects across AIPD, CPIT and SAP. Their core tasks include project management, stakeholder management and connected communications.
