Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Business Informatics, Computer Science, Business Administration, or Engineering — ideally with a digital focus — and want to explore how AI transforms enterprise business.
 Currently enrolled as a Master's student at a German university, with a completed Bachelor's degree
 A solid grounding in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Business Administration, or Engineering
 Familiarity with GenAI concepts and tools such as Claude Code, Agentic AI, or RAG
 Proficiency in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and programming experience, ideally in Python
 A customer-oriented mindset with strong analytical and creative problem-solving skills
 Fluency in German and English is required for this role.
 Working from an SAP office in Germany is required
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems — whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools — including agentic AI — to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work at the intersection of AI and enterprise architecture, contributing to GenAI use cases that create real value for SAP clients.
 Collaborate with GenAI experts to develop and explore AI-driven use cases for enterprise clients
 Engage with clients, management consultants, and business leaders across industries and business units
 Plan and deliver work packages in platform development, digital projects, and customer adoption scenarios
 Research and share knowledge on GenAI trends and digital business developments
 Apply Design Thinking and agile methods to structure and solve business problems
 Prepare and present results to stakeholders across different functions
 Where you belong
 Business Transformation & Architecture (BT&A) is SAP's consulting unit for enterprise digital transformation. You'll work alongside consultants and architects applying GenAI, Agentic AI, and cloud technologies to real client challenges. This is a hands-on role — you'll contribute to real engagements from day one. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen — a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.
 Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages.
 This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
