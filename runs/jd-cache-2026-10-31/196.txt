Beschreibung We are currently looking for a Working Student to join the Product Specialist Team! The position will be based in our Frankfurt office. About the Job AllianzGI is recognised globally as a leading active fund manager. As part of the Product Specialist team based in Frankfurt, you will support communication between Portfolio Managers and the wider business, including colleagues in Distribution, Marketing, and Client Reporting. This role offers an opportunity to learn about a range of equities-based investment strategies, as well as the investment cases for many leading public companies in Europe and abroad. You will support the entire process of originating and creating product information for our funds. We are looking for candidates keen to develop their writing, content and marketing skills within the financial industry, while building their knowledge of key financial concepts. The role provides exposure to activities such as writing fund commentaries, compiling promotional material, analysing competitor research, and using data to craft relevant investment stories. This is a collaborative environment where attention to detail, curiosity, and a willingness to learn are highly valued. What you do Support the Product Specialists in the end-to-end process of originating product information, with a focus on investment processes, performance explanations, portfolio holdings, and the illustration of strategy and single stock investment cases Prepare and ensure consistency of presentation materials for Product Specialists and Fund Managers for marketing events, client meetings, and consultant meetings Support the writing and articulation of portfolio-related commentaries for regular client reporting and marketing materials, ensuring timely delivery Collaborate with the broader Product Specialist team and Distribution Teams globally to support the representation of focus products across relevant marketing formats Interact regularly with Portfolio Managers to ensure the accurate communication of investment-related messages in marketing materials Assist the Product Specialist team with the dissemination of product information to internal and external clients Perform and interpret qualitative and quantitative analyses, including performance attribution analysis, risk analysis, peer group analysis, and other related assessments Support the development,
