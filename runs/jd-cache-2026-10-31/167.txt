Welcome at PIMCO Prime Real Estate
 We are looking for a working student (m/f/d) for our modern and innovative location Frankfurt. Our European Sustainability team supports the further development and implementation of the sustainability strategy across our European real estate portfolio. As a working student (m/f/d), you will contribute to hands-on ESG projects and support the team in preparing, analyzing, and presenting sustainability and building-related data in a clear and meaningful way. In doing so, you will gain valuable insights into the intersection of real estate, sustainability, regulatory requirements, and data-driven decision-making.
 What You Will Do
 You support the development and implementation of asset-specific sustainability initiatives, including decarbonization measures, energy efficiency improvements, and green building certifications.
 You actively contribute to and help shape European ESG initiatives and internal training programs, while collaborating and communicating with Asset Management teams, investors, joint venture partners, tenants, and external service providers.
 You support the collection, structuring, validation, and analysis of ESG, energy, and building-related data, helping to identify and address data gaps and quality issues.
 You contribute to the development of Power BI reports and management dashboards, enabling transparent monitoring of key sustainability metrics and performance trends.
 You research regulatory developments as well as relevant market and technology trends in sustainable real estate and prepare the findings in a clear and audience-appropriate manner.
 What You Bring
 You are enrolled in a relevant degree program, such as Sustainability Management, Real Estate Management, Business Administration, Data Science, Data Analytics, Civil Engineering, or Architecture.
 You are passionate about contributing to the sustainable transformation of the real estate industry and are interested in how ESG data can support operational and strategic decision-making. Ideally, you have already gained initial experience in real estate management, data science, or sustainability-related fields.
 You have strong Excel skills and enjoy working with large datasets in a structured and analytical manner. You are able to interpret complex information, identify patterns and trends, and present results clearly through tables, visualizations, and presentations. Initial experience with Power BI, data modeling, or data visualization is considered an advantage.
 You possess excellent written and spoken German and English language skills. Knowledge of French is a plus.
