The Opening:
 When a client changes its operating model, the decisions we help make end up as settings in SAP: who approves a purchase, which cost center pays for it, how a sale is booked. Wert Group is adding a Junior SAP Consultant to carry those decisions into the system together with the client's IT team. You can do the job from home anywhere in Germany, full-time. You will work with our operating model consultants and an experienced SAP consultant who reviews your configuration before it goes to the client.
 Management consulting: strategy, organization design, operating models, governance.
 In Practice: Write down how finance, purchasing and sales work today in workshops with client staff.
 Map the new processes to SAP S/4HANA and draft the functional specifications.
 Set up and test configuration in FI, CO or MM with a senior SAP consultant.
 Prepare test cases, run user acceptance tests and log defects.
 Train key users and write short process guides.
 Skills & Experience: A degree in business informatics, business administration or a related field.
 First hands-on experience with SAP, from an internship, a working student job or a university project.
 Knowledge of business processes, e.g. purchase to pay or order to cash.
 What we offer: laptop and home office equipment; a monthly home office allowance; training budget each year, certification exams paid; company pension (bAV); flexible hours; and 30 days of paid holiday.
 Bonus: SAP S/4HANA or SAP Fiori
 An SAP certification, for example in FI or MM
 Jira or another ticket tool
