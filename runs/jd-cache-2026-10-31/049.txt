Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You are a student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences in one of the preferred fields of study: User Experience Design, Interaction Design, Interface Design, Human-Computer interaction, Media Informatics or a related field (e.g., Communication Design)
 Computer skills: Figma, Claude Code and other AI tools
 Language skills: Proficient in English, both written and verbal
 Soft skills: Good communication and collaboration skills, cultural awareness, proactiveness and initiative, doer mentality
 Working from Walldorf office is required 1 -2 per week
 What You'll Build
 You'll support the UX team across the full design process.
 Design end-to-end user journeys, interaction flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes — using AI tools to move faster and focus your energy where human judgment matters most
 Contribute to conversational AI and agentic UX — one of the most exciting areas in enterprise design right now
 Support user research and usability testing, analyse findings, and turn insights into concrete design improvements
 Work closely with product managers and engineers
 Where you belong
 You'll join the SAP HANA Cloud UX team — a small, diverse, and distributed group of product and content designers who collaborate closely across geographies. We share ideas openly, challenge each other constructively, and celebrate wins together.
This isn't a hand-off role. You'll shape real product experiences end-to-end, working closely with product managers and engineers along the way.
Curious, eager to learn, not afraid to ask questions? You'll fit right in.
Additional Information
 Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages.
 This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
