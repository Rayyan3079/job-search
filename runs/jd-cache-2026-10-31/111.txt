 The Role
 As a Working Student in Software Engineering, you will join one of our Scrum teams and contribute to the development of our enterprise software products. Working students at ATOSS are treated as regular members of their teams. You will participate in team routines, work on relevant product topics, and contribute code that creates value for our customers. At the same time, your tasks and level of responsibility will be aligned with your experience, availability, and academic commitments.
You will work alongside experienced software engineers and receive guidance while gradually taking on more independent tasks.
 Why this role is exciting, impactful and challenging
 This role goes beyond working on isolated student assignments. As a regular member of the team, you will contribute to a mature, mission-critical enterprise system with complex business logic, long-lived codebases, and high standards for correctness and stability.
Your work will support processes that affect the daily work and incomes of hundreds of thousands of employees. This makes quality and reliability essential—and gives you the opportunity to create real, lasting value at scale from the beginning.
