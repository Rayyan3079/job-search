At 5Plus (part of Pentadoc Group), we build production-grade AI systems that solve real operational problems for our customers. 
As a Working Student - AI Engineering , you will help design, build, and deploy AI applications - from LLM-powered workflows (e.g., intelligent document processing) to agentic systems that integrate deeply into our customers' business processes. 
You'll work hands-on alongside experienced engineers and take on real responsibility from day one. This role is hybrid (remote + Würzburg) and fits flexibly around your studies. 
 Your daily business 
 Build and ship features for AI systems, from prototype to production
 Develop LLM-powered applications (incl. RAG, tool usage, validations) with a focus on reliability and cost-efficiency
 Contribute to agentic systems (e.g., planning, tool orchestration, multi-step workflows) and their integrations into customers' systems
 Set up evaluations – test datasets, metrics, benchmarks, and monitoring
 Help optimize systems across latency, cost, and quality
 Work with customers and the team to turn real-world problems into concrete technical solutions
 Your profile 
 Enrolled in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, or a related field
 Solid Python skills
 First hands-on experience with LLMs or ML tooling - from university, side projects, or internships
 Curiosity about what makes LLM applications reliable, safe, and scalable
 Ability to work independently and take ownership of your tasks
 Some bonus points to score 
 Experience with agentic architectures (planning, memory, multi-agent coordination)
 Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, etc.)
 Proficient in German (written and spoken)
 Own projects on GitHub, open-source contributions, or technical writing
 Some of your benefits at 5Plus 
 Real ownership: Work on AI solutions that go live quickly
 Learn from a small, experienced team with fast decision-making
 Flexible working hours that fit your study schedule, and freedom to choose your workplace
 Learning budget and support for conferences
 Option to write your Bachelor's or Master's thesis with us
