We are CARIAD, the automotive software company of the Volkswagen Group. Our teams build automotive software platforms and digital customer functions for iconic brands like Audi, Volkswagen, and Porsche – supporting the Volkswagen Group in becoming the leading automotive technology company. With CARIDIANS in Germany, the USA, China, Estonia, and India, we are transforming automotive mobility for everyone.
Join us and be part of this exciting journey!
 YOUR TEAM
 For the team Market Intelligence as part of the Product Strategy department we are looking for working students or interns in the area of market & technology intelligence. The Market Intelligence team is working cross-functionally in CARIAD on topics like ADAS, Cloud, Connectivity, Infotainment, Vehicle Motion & Body functions fostering a unified understanding of the competition and market. We works on continuously identifying & evaluating market & technology developments and their impact on the product & technology strategy. We are a team of curious people with a strong analytical & future mindset – embedded into a worldwide network of the VW Group.
 What You Will Do
 Identify and analyze key & emerging technologies, market developments and upcoming features within the competition or across other industries with the help of our tools, digital platform and input from experts & partners 
 Research and evaluate key market & technologic developments, scout leading industry events and prepare management briefings to support strategic decision making 
 Strengthen & enhance our market & technology intelligence and its corresponding processes 
 Explore, test and implement the possibilities of artificial intelligence to improve our intelligence activities 
 Derive potential implications by screening & scouting in the tech industry and market activities for the strategy, innovation and the technology stack of CARIAD 
 Continuously manage and improve our digital intelligence, product & technology strategy platform 
 Collaborate with other departments on intelligence projects by presenting the results into the organization 
 Who You Are
 Enrolled bachelor or master student in a technical or business degree with a focus on Technology Management, Digitalization, Business Informatics, Engineering, Computer Science, Innovation or similar field 
 Good to very good academic grades 
 Familiarity with competitor analysis, technology scouting, strategy management or similar 
 Experiences in utilization or adoption of different AI tools 
 Project, internship or working student experience in the tech, software, consulting or automotive industry would be an advantage 
 Strong communication skills in English & German necessary 
 Proactive mindset, curiosity for technology & innovative topics 
 Well-structured, independent work
