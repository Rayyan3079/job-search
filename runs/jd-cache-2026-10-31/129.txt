Your mission
As a Working Student in Retail Operations, you'll support our Retail Operations Manager in the day-to-day work that gets our products from production to retail shelves. It's hands-on exposure to supply chain, logistics, and retail partnerships in a fast-scaling startup.
Key responsibilities
 Order processing support: Help check and process daily EDI and email orders in SAP, and prepare delivery notes and transfer orders.
 Logistics coordination: Track deliveries with logistics partners, update stock data, and help prepare and follow up on partner meetings.
 Master data maintenance: Keep product, packaging, and specification data up to date for internal teams and retail partners.
 Retail partner communication: Support routine communication with retail partners and dispatchers, such as REWE and COOP.
 Issue resolution: Help investigate delivery issues and complaints and escalate them to the right people.
 Reporting: Prepare simple overviews and reports on orders, stock, and deliveries.
 Your profile
 Requirements
 Enrollment: Currently enrolled at a university in Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Food Technology, or a related field.
 Availability: 15–20 hours per week during the semester.
 Technical skills: Confident with Excel. SAP or ERP experience is a plus.
 Working style: Organised and detail-oriented, and you enjoy solving practical problems.
 Languages: Fluent in German and English.
