About MentionX 
 MentionX is an AI and search intelligence platform that helps brands get discovered and recommended across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and traditional search.
 It goes beyond tracking. MentionX starts with your ICP (ideal customer profile) and buyer personas, then maps the real questions those buyers ask AI and search engines. It shows where you appear, where competitors win, and why. AI visibility, organic rankings, competitor intelligence, citations and brand sentiment all sit in one dashboard. Our Search Twin feature compares buyer intent in AI answers with traditional search results, so you can see exactly where you're missing out.
 Then MentionX helps you act on it, with content and optimisation plans built for each persona that improve how AI engines understand, cite and recommend your brand.
 MentionX was founded in Berlin and is built for marketing teams, SEO professionals, agencies and growing businesses.
 The role 
 Millions of people now ask AI what to buy, and it names only a handful of brands. As an AI Search Intern, you'll work out why some brands win and others aren't seen at all.
 Analyse AI answers and search results to map how brands are discovered.
 Research keywords and buyer intent across AI engines and Google.
 Build visibility reports, dashboards and case studies for clients.
 Run experiments on how content and optimisation change AI visibility.
 Turn your findings into clear recommendations and published research, with you credited.
 Why this internship is different 
 🌍 Work from anywhere. You can work fully remotely from wherever you are.
 ⏰ Truly flexible hours. We judge you on what you deliver, not on when you're online.
 🤝 Work side by side with the founder. Learn directly from a growth marketer with 13 years in performance and growth.
 🚀 Master GEO and performance marketing. You'll learn the newest skill in marketing alongside the one that has driven growth for the past decade.
 🌐 Work with SaaS companies around the world. You'll do real work for real brands, not practice exercises.

 You'll fit if
