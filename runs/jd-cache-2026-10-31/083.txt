Key Responsibilities
 Dashboard Development: Design and build comprehensive business dashboards in Power BI to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and business metrics, enabling data-driven decision-making across the organization.
 Data Extraction, Transformation, and Loading (ETL): Utilize Talend to extract, transform, and load data from multiple sources, ensuring high-quality data is available for reporting and analysis.
 SQL Data Management: Perform advanced SQL queries to extract, clean, and manipulate data for reporting and analysis, ensuring data integrity and accuracy.
 Reporting and Analysis: Generate regular and ad-hoc reports using SQL, Power BI, and Excel, presenting findings and insights to business stakeholders.
 Excel and PowerPoint Proficiency: Develop complex Excel reports and present findings effectively through professional PowerPoint presentations tailored to different audiences.
 Cross-functional Collaboration: Work closely with business units and stakeholders to understand data needs, provide insights, and offer recommendations based on analysis.
 Communication Skills: Conduct effective, fluent communication in English, both written and verbal, to present complex data insights to non-technical stakeholders.
 Continuous Improvement: Stay current with best practices in data analytics, suggesting improvements in data collection, processing, and visualization techniques.
 Qualifications
 Education: Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Business, or a related field.
 Experience: experience in a data analysis, business intelligence, or similar role.

 ⁠Power BI: Proficient in Power BI for creating interactive, visually appealing dashboards and reports to support business insights.
