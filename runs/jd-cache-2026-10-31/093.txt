 About The Role
 The Digital and Information Services Branch plays a critical role in enabling Sunshine Coast Council to deliver innovative, efficient and customer-focused services. As part of the Business Transformation & Performance Directorate, the branch provides agile technology and information solutions that support informed decision-making, continuous improvement and sustainable service delivery across the organisation.
As a Business Intelligence Analyst, you will lead the development of data models, analytics solutions and interactive reporting that empower evidence-based decision-making across Council. Working closely with stakeholders from diverse business areas, you'll translate complex business requirements into meaningful insights, drive best-practice business intelligence governance and help shape the future of Council's enterprise reporting and analytics capability.
This position is based at Sunshine Coast City Hall, in the heart of Maroochydore CBD. This state of the art and sustainable building is designed to achieve an innovative and collaborative workspace.
 Key Responsibilities
 Lead the development, maintenance and enhancement of enterprise data models and analytics solutions.
 Deliver insightful Power BI dashboards, reports and visualisations to support business outcomes.
 Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business needs into technical solutions.
 Provide governance, technical advice and continuous improvement recommendations for Council's business intelligence platform.
 About You
 You hold a degree in Information Technology or a related discipline and bring substantial experience delivering business intelligence and reporting solutions within a large, complex organisation.
 You are an expert in Power BI, with proven capability designing sophisticated dashboards, reports and visualisations that transform data into meaningful business insights.
 You have strong technical expertise in SQL, ETL processes, data modelling and data warehousing principles, enabling you to build robust and scalable analytics solutions.
 You are a collaborative communicator who can confidently engage stakeholders, understand business challenges and deliver solutions that create value across the organisation.
