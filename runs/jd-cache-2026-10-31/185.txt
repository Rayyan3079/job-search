 What you`ll do 
 You'll empower Product Standard Owners and development teams to build secure, compliant software at scale—bridging technical depth with strategic impact. From designing evidence, schemas and enhancing policy workflows to providing CI/CD integration guidance, you'll shape how thousands of SAP developers experience compliance.
This role offers the unique opportunity to influence both the technical architecture of our Policy as Code platform and the organizational processes that drive adoption across SAP. If you're energized by building tools that scale and making compliance seamless—this is your role.
 The Role
 Support Product Standard Owners in creating, adopting, and maintaining central policies that enforce corporate requirements
 Design and document evidence schemas and policy evaluation workflows
 Create measurements for evidence that can be generated and uploaded from SAP’s CI/CD pipeline library Piper or other workflows
 Evaluate evidence using the Open Policy Agent and Rego
 Provide guidance on policy execution in CI/CD pipelines and evidence collection automation
 Bridge technical policy implementation with business requirements through documentation, training, and direct collaboration
 Promote Policy as Code to increase adoption and effectiveness across the organization
 What You Bring
 Rego & OPA - Familiarity is a plus; we'll support your growth in policy-as-code
 Go - Beginner level is fine
 JSON & JSON Schema - Good understanding required
 Communication skills - Clear and collaborative, fluent in written and verbal English, German language is a big plus 
 Proactive & self-organized - Takes ownership and works independently
 Git & GitHub - Comfortable with version control workflows
 CI/CD fundamentals - Familiarity with GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar tools
 Working from Walldorf office 2 times per week is required, if you are not living in commuting distance your application can not be considered

 Where you belong 
 The Cumulus team builds and operates Cumulus, SAP's internal compliance data store for CI/CD pipelines. The platform enables teams across SAP to verify that their software deliveries meet corporate requirements before going live. Cumulus processes audit-relevant artifacts—test results, scan reports, SBOMs—and offers OPA-based policy evaluations to aggregate compliance status. The team works closely with Product Standard Owners, Security Experts, and development teams across SAP.
Cumulus is part of Hyperspace – SAPs internal development platform that provisions pipelines, manages credentials, orchestrates compliance workflows and much more.
