 Job Description

 You help with software and hardware installation and repair
 You support the administration and configuration of Google Meets Devices in all conference rooms
 You independently and autonomously supervise the backup and repair of hardware of retired employees
 You take on initial project responsibility 
 You are responsible for maintaining documentation

 You are studying for a Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, information technology or a comparable course of study
 You have a strong affinity for IT and a keen interest in technical solutions (ideally already have some experience in IT support)
 You have a structured, independent and responsible working style
 You have excellent written and spoken English skills, German is a plus
