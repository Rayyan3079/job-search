Your mission
If you're studying supply chain, logistics, or business and want real hands-on experience alongside your studies, this is your chance to see the full supply chain of a scaling food company from the inside. As a Working Student in Supply Chain, you'll support our Retail Operations, Planning, and Procurement teams and help keep daily operations running smoothly.
Key responsibilities
 Retail operations: Support the processing of EDI and email orders in SAP, prepare delivery notes and transfer orders, and help coordinate deliveries with logistics partners.
 Supply chain planning: Help maintain stock, production, and inventory data, and support the Supply Chain Planner with planning overviews.
 Procurement: Prepare and follow up on purchase orders, check supplier confirmations, and track deliveries of ingredients and packaging.
 Master data: Keep product, packaging, and supplier data accurate across our systems.
 Team support: Pick up operational tasks across the team as priorities shift, always in close coordination with the responsible team member.
 Reporting and process improvement: Prepare regular reports on orders, stock, and purchasing, and help document and improve our supply chain processes.
 Your profile
 Requirements
 Studies: Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's programme in Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, Logistics, Industrial Engineering, or a related field, ideally with at least 12 months of study remaining.
 Availability: 16–20 hours per week during the semester, with the option to work more during semester breaks.
 Experience: First practical experience in supply chain, logistics, or procurement (internship, previous student job) is a plus, but not a must.
 Technical skills: Good Excel skills. First exposure to SAP or another ERP is a plus.
 Working style: Organised, reliable, and comfortable switching between tasks and stakeholders.
 Mindset: Proactive, curious, and eager to learn in a fast-paced environment.
 Language proficiency: Fluency in English and German, written and spoken.
