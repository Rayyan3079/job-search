 Tasks
 Your tasks?
 As a Working Student Innovation & Strategy (m/f/d/x), you will support the team in designing and organizing team- and cross-project collaboration and exchange formats.
 You will contribute to the conceptualization, planning, and implementation of strategy and innovation formats.
 You will also be part of the organizing team for an international innovation format and actively support its preparation and implementation.
 Together with your colleagues, you will prepare documents and presentations for management.
 You will support the development of concepts related to strategy and innovation, contributing your own ideas and perspectives.
 In addition, you will support the organization and implementation of workshops, events, and other collaboration and exchange formats.
