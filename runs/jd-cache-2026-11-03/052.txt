Data Analyst - Dublin (Hybrid) 
 Love finding the story in the numbers? This one's for you. 
 We're working with a business in Dublin that's looking for a Data Analyst to join the data team. This is a role with real visibility: you'll partner with people right across the organisation, answer the questions that matter to them and help leaders make better calls. If you're as comfortable writing a query as you are walking a room through the results, you'll fit right in.

 Pull together, clean and interrogate data from a range of sources, with SQL as your main tool
 Design, build and look after dashboards and reporting in Power BI or Tableau
 Partner with colleagues in different functions to get to the heart of what they need to know
 Pick out patterns, risks and opportunities, and share them in a way that lands with any audience, technical or not
 Raise the bar on data quality, reporting processes and documentation
 About you 
 2+ years in an analytics or data-focused position
 Confident, hands-on in SQL
 Practical experience with Power BI, Tableau or a similar BI platform
 Solid Excel ability
 A knack for explaining findings simply and keeping stakeholder requests on track
 Bonus points for Python or R, exposure to cloud data platforms such as Azure, AWS or Snowflake
