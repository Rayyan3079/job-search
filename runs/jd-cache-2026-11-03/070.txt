Discover your exciting role
 Would you like to put the theoretical knowledge you have gained during your undergraduate studies into practice? Do you have an affinity for numbers that you would like to prove? Then become part of our Corporate Controlling Team! We are looking for a committed team member to find new ways of thinking and to shape an innovative vision for the integration of our systems. Together as a team, we support our subsidiaries around the world with performance tracking. You will also be responsible for management reporting, budgeting, forecasting, and cost control. Do these interesting tasks match your interests and skills? Then we look forward to receiving your application.
 Explore Your Tasks And Responsibilities
 You support monthly reporting and the preparation of business analyses.
 You create and maintain controlling dashboards.
 You support the Corporate Controlling team in preparing monthly and annual financial statements as well as management presentations.
 You prepare target/actual comparisons and conduct variance and data analyses.
 You support budgeting and forecasting processes.
 You contribute to the continuous improvement of internal processes.
 You support cross-departmental projects and initiatives.
 Show Your Expertise
 You are studying economics, business administration, or a comparable course of study with a focus on controlling and accounting.
 Ideally, you have gained experience in controlling through internships or working student activities.
 You have very good MS Office skills, especially in Excel and PowerPoint.
 Ideally, you have knowledge of Power BI.
 You are characterized by a high degree of independence, personal responsibility, and the ability to work in a team.
 You have analytical thinking, good conceptual skills, a good understanding of numbers, and a quick grasp of things.
 You have very good written and spoken German and English skills.
