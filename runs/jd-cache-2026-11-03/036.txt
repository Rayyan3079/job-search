Job Details
 Job Description:
 Join Intel's OpenVINO Team and Shape the Future of AI
 Are you passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Software Engineering? Do you want to work on cutting-edge AI technologies that help neural networks run faster and more efficiently on real-world devices?
Join the Neural Network Compression Tools Team within Intel's OpenVINO organization and gain hands-on experience developing technologies used to optimize state-of-the-art AI models.
What You'll Do
As an AI & Deep Learning Intern, you will contribute to the development of the Neural Network Compression Framework (NNCF) , a key technology used to improve the performance and efficiency of deep learning models.
 During Your Internship, You Will
 Explore and evaluate state-of-the-art machine learning and neural network optimization techniques
 Contribute to the development of compression algorithms that improve AI inference performance
 Work with modern deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, and OpenVINO
 Collaborate with experienced AI engineers and researchers
 Learn about the latest neural network architectures and industry-leading AI technologies
 Gain exposure to Intel hardware platforms and AI software ecosystems
 What You'll Gain
 Hands-on experience with real-world AI products and technologies
 Mentorship from experts in Deep Learning and Software Engineering
 Exposure to state-of-the-art AI models, frameworks, and optimization techniques
 An opportunity to contribute to open-source and production-ready software
 Flexible working hours that fit your academic schedule
 Valuable industry experience at one of the world's leading technology companies
 Qualifications
 Minimum Qualifications
 Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in: Computer ScienceMathematicsPhysicsSoftware Engineering or a related field
 Undergraduate student in the 3rd year or above
 Programming experience in Python
 Availability to work at least 20 hours per week
 Preferred Qualifications
 Knowledge of Machine Learning or Deep Learning concepts
 Understanding of Computer Vision or Image Processing
 Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or OpenVINO
 Curiosity, problem-solving mindset, and eagerness to learn
 If you're excited about AI, eager to learn, and ready to make an impact, we'd love to hear from you. Apply and help us build the future of intelligent computing.
