 A Snapshot of Your Day 
 To support the expanding research and development activities within Siemens Energy's Generator Engineering division, specifically focusing on synchronous machine development, this student internship or thesis position offers a unique opportunity to contribute to advanced technical calculations and simulations. Located in Erfurt within the Technical Integration and Performance team, the role directly aids in driving innovative solutions for industrial electrical machines. To address these R&D objectives, this role focuses on executing numerical simulations and analyzing electromagnetic data. These core areas directly support the verification and optimization of electrical machines under realistic operating conditions. Given the focus on advanced engineering simulations, the ideal candidate will be an enrolled student in electrical engineering, mechatronics, physics, or a related field with foundational knowledge of simulation tools. Analytical skills, a proactive approach to solving complex technical problems, and the ability to work collaboratively in a team are essential for success.
 How You’ll Make An Impact
 100% Simulation, Calculation & Experimentation
 Set up and execute numerical simulations (e.g., FEM, CFD) for electrical machines and components
 Perform electromagnetic calculations to evaluate component integrity
 Simulate transient behavior
 Conduct experimental validation
 What You Bring
 Enrolled student in electrical engineering, mechatronics, physics, or a comparable engineering discipline
 German or English at B2 level
