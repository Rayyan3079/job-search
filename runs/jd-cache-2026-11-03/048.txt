 Turn our data into decisions. 
 St John Ambulance NSW is looking for a hands-on Power BI specialist with strong analytical skills to own our reporting environment and help our leaders make better, data-informed decisions. You will build reliable, automated Power BI reports, turn financial and operational data into clear insight, and bring structure and discipline to how data is integrated, automated and governed across the organisation. It is a chance to help shape our data platform as our Microsoft environment evolves, including Microsoft Fabric.
 About the Role 
 Reporting to the Chief Financial Officer, you will be our go-to person for Power BI and data analysis. Our Power BI environment has grown over time to cover a wide range of needs, and some of it relies on manual effort to keep running. A big part of this role is helping us get that right: agreeing with the business what is genuinely needed, automating and documenting what we keep, and recommending where a source system’s own reporting would do the job better.
 You will also work with our system owners and technology team on data quality, system changes and testing. Systems support is part of the role, but Power BI and analysis are at its core.
 What You Will Do 
 Design, build and maintain Power BI reports, dashboards and semantic models used across finance, operations and member services.
 Write DAX, Power Query and SQL to bring together data from multiple systems.
 Build data integration pipelines and dataflows, including in Microsoft Fabric as we adopt it, so data is modelled once and reused.
 Automate data refreshes and routine tasks with Power Query, Fabric and Power Automate, removing manual, spreadsheet-based steps.
 Build validation and reconciliation checks into reports so issues are caught before they reach users.
 Manage our Power BI Service, including workspaces, refresh schedules, access and Row Level Security.
 Analyse financial and operational data, explain what it means in plain language and recommend action.
 Help set and apply data governance standards: definitions, ownership, certified datasets and access controls.
 Keep a register of our reports, review it with the business and retire what is not adding value.
 Document reports and processes so they do not depend on any one person, and train backup users.
 Work with system owners and IT on data quality, system changes and testing, including data migration for our move to Business Central.
 About You 
 To be successful in this role, you will bring:
 Proven commercial experience building and supporting Power BI solutions end to end.
 Advanced DAX and Power Query skills, and sound data modelling (including star schema).
 Solid SQL for querying, preparing and validating data.
 Experience integrating data from multiple systems and automating data preparation.
 Hands-on experience with the Power BI Service, including scheduled refresh, gateways and Row Level Security.
 Strong analytical skills, with experience in financial or operational analysis and advanced Excel.
 Close attention to detail and the habit of checking your numbers against the source.
 The confidence to question requests and recommend the simplest solution that works.
 Clear communication with non-technical people.
 A tertiary qualification in a related field, or equivalent experience.
 Highly regarded:
 Experience with Microsoft Fabric (lakehouses, Dataflows Gen2 or pipelines) or Azure data services.
 Experience with Power Automate.
 Experience with Dynamics 365 Business Central or other ERP data, including ERP migrations.
 Experience with CRM, contact centre, finance system data.
 Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300) certification.
