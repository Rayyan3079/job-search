Are you excited to gain hands-on experience in offshore wind operations? We’re looking for a Working Student to support our Business Area Wind team with operational and project tasks related to offshore wind farms, including DanTysk and Sandbank . This is a unique opportunity to contribute to maintenance planning and project coordination while building practical skills in one of the most innovative industries of our time.
As a Working Student Offshore Operations, you'll work closely with project managers and our O&M Coordinators to ensure smooth operations and effective documentation processes.
 Project Support: Help project managers with coordination, preparation, and follow-up tasks to ensure successful project execution. 
 Support Maintenance Planning: Assist in creating workflow descriptions, material lists, and time schedules for offshore maintenance activities. 
 Documentation Management: Organize, review, and update "As-Built" and project documentation in collaboration with engineers and service leads. 
 Collaboration: Serve as an interface between internal teams (e.g., Engineering, Operations, and Project Management) to ensure clear communication and efficient workflows. 
 Process Optimization: Contribute to improving processes for documentation discovery, tagging, and structuring, ensuring up-to-date technical information is readily accessible. 
 Qualifications
 We’re looking for a proactive and motivated team player with excellent organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills. You’ll thrive in this role if you combine technical expertise with a structured mindset, practical experience, and a passion for learning.
 What You Bring To The Team
 Enrollment: You are currently studying in a technical field like Electrical, Marine, or Mechanical Engineering, Renewable Energy, or a similar discipline. 
 Languages: You are fluent in both German (C1 minimum) and English. 
 IT Proficiency: Strong skills in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel), and ideally, basic knowledge of SharePoint. 
 Detail-Oriented: You excel at structured thinking and bring consistent quality to repetitive tasks. 
 Team Player: You communicate effectively, adapt quickly, and enjoy working in a collaborative environment. 
 Motivation: You are eager to learn, take initiative, and approach challenges proactively. 
 Additional Information
 Location & Conditions 
 This role is based in our Hamburg office with flexible working hours—up to 20 hours/week during semesters and full-time during breaks. A minimum 12-month contract and 40% office presence (ideally Wednesdays or Thursdays) are required. Start date: 01.02.2027 . This setup lets you gain hands-on experience in renewable energy while balancing your studies.
 What's in it for you?
 As a student working with Vattenfall, you’ll have the unique opportunity to contribute to one of the most dynamic and future-oriented industries: offshore wind energy. This is your chance to make a tangible impact on how we operate, maintain, and innovate within renewable energy projects.
 Practical Experience: Dive into real-world tasks like project management, maintenance planning, and documentation processes, gaining valuable insights into offshore wind operations. 
 Meaningful Contributions: Your work will directly influence our operations, helping us optimize workflows and improve performance in a rapidly growing sector. 
 Skill Development: Build expertise in technical workflows, process improvement, and cross-functional collaboration, setting you up for success in your career. 
 Supportive Environment: Join a welcoming and collaborative team that values your input and fosters a culture of mutual learning and growth.
