 Company Description
 What you'll bring
 You're a student of business, IT, or engineering — curious about data, controlling, and how strategic decisions get made.
 Currently enrolled at a university or Fachhochschule, ideally in Business Informatics, Business Administration, or Industrial Engineering
 Strong MS Office skills, particularly in Excel and PowerPoint
 Quick to pick up new concepts and comfortable working across teams
 A customer-oriented mindset — you consider the impact of your work on others
 Interest in AI tools and a willingness to explore how they can support your work
 Fluency in German and English is required for this role
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems — whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools — including agentic AI — to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll support the Premium Engagement Controlling team on work that directly shapes how the business tracks and improves performance. Your analysis will reach senior colleagues and feed into real decisions.
 Analyse strategic KPIs across different aggregation levels to make performance transparent
 Identify and develop concrete measures to improve business results
 Prepare and present findings to colleagues for discussion and review
 Follow up on agreed actions and track their progress
 Explore how SAP's Premium Engagement delivery model works in practice
 Get hands-on exposure to the role of a Technical Quality Manager
 Where you belong
 You'll be part of the Premium Engagement team, working on services that help customers navigate digital transformation. The team blends technical depth with strategic thinking — and you'll be close to both. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen — a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers. It's a space where your work has real weight from day one.
