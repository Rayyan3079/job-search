About The Role
 As an AI Generalist Trainer at Rex.zone, you will evaluate and improve AI systems by reviewing model-generated responses in English and German. You will perform RLHF-style ranking, prompt evaluation, QA evaluation, and validation against policy and annotation guidelines to improve training data quality and model performance.
 Responsibilities
 Review prompts and model outputs in English and German
 Rank and compare multiple model responses using RLHF-aligned criteria
 Write concise, evidence-based rationales that justify evaluation decisions
 Execute QA evaluation for annotation accuracy, consistency, and guidelines compliance
 Label and categorize data, including content safety labeling when required
 Validate edge cases, detect policy violations, and escalate ambiguous items with clear notes
 Track common failure modes and propose rubric improvements
 Maintain throughput while meeting accuracy targets and quality benchmarks
 Basic Qualifications
 Must be based in Germany and able to work remotely from Germany
 Fluent in German and English (reading, writing, comprehension)
 Strong analytical skills and attention to detail
 Comfortable with structured tasks, feedback loops, and quality audits
 Able to explain reasoning clearly and objectively in written rationales
 Preferred Qualifications
 Experience with AI data labeling, LLM evaluation, prompt evaluation, or QA evaluation
 Familiarity with RLHF concepts and ranking tasks
 Knowledge of content safety labeling (toxicity, policy compliance, sensitive content)
 Self-driven and reliable in a remote environment
 FAQ
 Is this role remote?
 Yes. This is a fully remote, full-time role, and you must be based in Germany.
 Do I need AI experience?
 AI experience is helpful but not required; strong analytical skills and consistent guideline-following matter most.
