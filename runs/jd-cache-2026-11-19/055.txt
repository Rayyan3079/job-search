We are establishing SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) as the planning solution within our system landscape and are searching for someone who can take on the responsibilities below.
 Responsibilities
 Analyze, structure, and align business planning requirements (e.g., financial, sales, or workforce planning) in close collaboration with internal business departments
 Design the technical and functional implementation concept for the planning landscape, ensuring integration with the existing SAP system landscape (SAP S/4HANA, SAP Datasphere)
 Design and implement data security, role-specific access, and authorization structures within SAP SAC and its interfaces to SAP S/4HANA
 Build and implement planning layouts and data models, including dimension and key figure modeling and version management for Actuals, Plan, and Forecast scenarios
 Establish and configure data connections between SAP SAC and source systems (SAP S/4HANA via CDS views, SAP Datasphere) to ensure seamless data exchange
 Set up and validate the write-back (retraction) of SAC planning data into SAP S/4HANA, specifically for Plan Data Line Items and Activity Cost Rates, to ensure data consistency
 Connect and harmonize actual financial data from SAP S/4HANA via CDS views for ongoing actual-vs-plan comparisons and rolling forecasts
 Develop intuitive, input-ready stories, tables, and dashboards for business planners and key users
 Create automated planning functions, such as allocation rules, Data Actions, currency translations, and calculations using Advanced Formulas
 Execute and document functional, integration, and performance tests for the planning functionalities
 Resolve bugs systematically through issue and defect tracking during hypercare and deployment
 Create comprehensive technical system documentation, process flows, and user manuals for key users and administrators
 Conduct enablement workshops to empower internal key users to independently manage and administer SAC models
 Requirements
 3-5 years of proven hands-on experience designing, modeling, and implementing planning solutions using SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)
 Deep functional understanding of corporate finance processes, including Integrated Financial Planning, P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow planning, and Cost Center budgeting
 Proficiency in building SAC planning models, such as dimension and key figure modeling and version management
 Expertise in developing complex business and calculation logics using Data Actions and Advanced Formulas
 Proven experience connecting SAP S/4HANA, including data import via CDS views and configuring data retraction (Write-Back / Retraction) to S/4HANA
 Strong communication and moderation skills to translate Finance/Controlling business requirements into precise technical specifications
 Structured, self-driven work ethic with the ability to resolve complex data structure and performance issues
 Fluent English skills (written and spoken) for project documentation and stakeholder alignment
 Nice to have
 Practical experience with the SAP standard package "Integrated Financial Planning for SAP S/4HANA" (specifically Scope Items 1HB / 4RC)
 Experience in data harmonization and provisioning via SAP Datasphere to support complex planning structures
 Initial experience designing or connecting Sales & Operations (S&OP) or demand planning models as a bridge between sales and financial planning
