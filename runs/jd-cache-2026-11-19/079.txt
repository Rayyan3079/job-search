Job Description
 What you'll bring 
 You’re studying economics, humanities, social sciences, or psychology - ideally with a Human Resources focus. You take initiative, stay curious, and thrive on meaningful, continuous learning.
 A strong interest in recruiting within an innovative technology company 
 A hands-on, reliable approach - you take charge and keep going, even when things get challenging 
 Strong communication and networking skills for outreach and collaboration 
 Creativity and curiosity - always looking for smarter ways to attract talent 
 Customer Orientation - keeping the candidate experience at the center of everything you do 
 Fluent business-level English (written and spoken). German is a plus 
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems - whatever stage of your career you’re at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools - including agentic AI - to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You’ll take part in real recruiting work from day one, supporting the Talent Discovery team across the full candidate journey.
 Identify suitable candidates on social media and reach out directly 
 Review incoming applications and conduct initial pre-selection interviews 
 Help organise selection days and support the application process 
 Bring your ideas on how to attract the best early talent 
 Support the preparation and delivery of campus events and workshops 
 Support the team with day-to-day administrative tasks across the recruiting process 
 Where you belong 
 You’ll belong to a supportive Talent Acquisition community that collaborates across regions and business areas to hire great people for SAP. In a truly inclusive culture, we don’t just make room for different perspectives - we seek them out, because better questions lead to better answers. Here, your voice matters and your ideas are heard.
We value clarity, empathy, and teamwork, while giving you the autonomy to make a real impact.
 Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships).
