 Your mission as a Working Student Customer Success DACH (m/f/d)
 We’re looking for a Working Student to support our Customer Success team in DACH. In this role, you will work closely with our Customer Success Managers, helping ensure our customers get the insights they need to succeed in their business.
You'll be part of a team that is shaping the future of market research and consumer insights, while gaining valuable hands-on experience and building a strong foundation for a future career in Customer Success, Account Management, or Sales at one of Europe's fastest-growing companies.
 What you’ll be doing
 You will support the monitoring and optimization of data flows between HubSpot and internal tools and workspaces (e.g. ClickUp and PandaDoc) to ensure smooth data synchronization
 You will prepare slides and other materials to support client communication
 You will develop content and formats to educate and engage customers, such as newsletters, use cases, and benchmarks
 You will support day-to-day operational tasks, including planning and organizing monthly meetings and managing digital workspaces for team collaboration
 You will thrive in this role if
 You have a strong analytical mindset and combine data-driven problem-solving with a structured, diligent, and proactive get-things-done approach
 You are highly proficient with Excel (including formulas and pivot tables) and PowerPoint (particularly when visualizing data and metrics)
 You have strong verbal and written communication skills in both German (C1) and English
 You are currently enrolled at a German university, with at least 12 months remaining in your studies
 Ideally, you have gained some experience in a fast-paced environment, such as a startup, tech company, or consulting firm
 Ideally, you have experience with SalesForce, HubSpot, Tableau, or similar marketing, sales automation, or analytics tools
 What’s in it for you?
 Subsidised Urban Sports Club or ClassPass membership based on location
