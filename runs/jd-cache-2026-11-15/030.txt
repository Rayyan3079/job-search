 About The Role
 Leveraging the latest advancements in Agentic AI and Generative AI, you will work with a team of experienced Application Engineers to develop, test, and validate next-generation AI workflows for semiconductor design . These AI agents are intended to automate complex engineering tasks, accelerate design convergence, and significantly improve the productivity of semiconductor design teams.
This internship offers hands-on experience with state-of-the-art Agentic AI workflows built on top of world-class EDA technologies used by leading semiconductor companies.
 What You Will Do
 Investigate the application of Generative AI and Agentic AI technologies in semiconductor digital implementation workflows
 Design, implement, test, and validate AI-driven workflows that automate engineering tasks and improve design productivity
 Collaborate with Application Engineers to evaluate AI agent performance on real customer design challenges
 Develop benchmarks and validation methodologies to measure the effectiveness and productivity gains of AI-driven workflows
 Job Qualifications
 MSc/MEng student in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or Microelectronics
 Knowledge of digital IC design methodologies and semiconductor design flows
 Strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills
 Excellent communication skills (fluent in English)
