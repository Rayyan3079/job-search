Your Role
 As a Working Student in our Data & AI department, you will support the team across a range of projects and help bring Black Semiconductor’s AI-first vision to life.
Working closely with a dedicated mentor, you will gain hands-on experience in data engineering, data science, and AI technologies. You will support the development and testing of data and machine-learning platforms, contribute to ontologies and knowledge graphs, and assist with agentic platforms and AI tools.
 What You’ll Do
 Support the setup, configuration and testing of data and machine-learning platforms
 Help create, structure and maintain ontologies and knowledge graphs
 Assist with configuring agentic platforms, LLM tools and other AI technologies
 Perform data preparation, analysis and data-pipeline tasks under guidance
 Support research and other administrative activities related to Data & AI projects
 Prepare clear documentation so that your work and findings can be understood and reused by others
 Record meeting notes and help keep project information structured and up to date
 Participate in stand-ups and team meetings, sharing your progress, questions and blockers openly
 Collaborate with your mentor and other team members while developing your technical skills
 What You Bring
 Current enrolment in a bachelor’s or master’s programme in computer science, data science, physics, electrical engineering, mathematics, business informatics or another quantitative field
 An interest in artificial intelligence, data technologies, deep tech or the semiconductor industry
 Curiosity, reliability and a strong willingness to learn
 A structured approach to your work and the confidence to ask questions when something is unclear
 Good communication and teamwork skills
 Good English skills; German is an advantage
 Some programming experience with Python or SQL is an advantage
 Familiarity with Jupyter, Git or GitLab is an advantage
 Experience with Microsoft 365, Teams, Miro or PowerPoint
 Familiarity with LLM tools or agent frameworks is an advantage
