We are a leading global digital publisher of individualized content and games. Through quizzes, articles, and Instant Games, we have reached more than 500 million people worldwide and built a community of over 120 million Facebook fans, with content available in more than 40 languages.
Behind this success is a creative, international team that combines family-friendly feel-good content with self-developed technology and a data-driven publishing approach. Together, we create entertaining digital experiences with one clear mission: to make people smile :)
Do you want to explore cutting-edge AI tools, automate workflows, and turn data into actionable insights — all within a dynamic, international team?
 Join us as a Working Student - Operations (m/f/d) in Cologne!
 We offer a flexible hybrid working setup and value in-person collaboration. We'd love for you to join us at our Cologne office once a week to connect and collaborate with the team.
 Your Tasks
 As our Working Student in Operations, you'll dive into data and AI-driven workflows, supporting our team in turning insights into action and driving automation across processes. Your key responsibilities will include:
 Exploring and testing AI tools and LLM-based APIs (e.g., GPT-powered tools) for use cases such as data enrichment, report automation, and insight generation
 Compiling and analyzing data from Google Analytics, Meta tools, and other sources; visualizing insights in Google Data Studio and improving reporting automation
 Supporting and maintaining project monitoring and performance tracking through data cleaning, reporting, and dashboard maintenance
 Preparing ad hoc analyses to support decision-making across teams
 Documenting and sharing findings, workflows, and proofs of concept
 Collaborating with senior analysts and product managers to identify opportunities where AI can improve workflows
