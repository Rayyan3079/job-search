Job Description
 Wireless communication, sensing technologies and RF components continue to increase in complexity with added functionality. At the same time, AI is transforming the way engineers design and test these systems and how we all use them. In this internship, you will be looking at ways to modify traditional workflows in test design and analyzing measurement results using AI technology to improve their outcome significantly.
 In This Role, Your Responsibilities Will Be:
 Literature research
 Building proof-of-concept implementations of selected algorithms and functionality
 Setting up and performing measurements
 Assessing performance and quality of results of the implemented algorithms and functionality
 Demonstrating your work to internal stakeholders
 Documenting your work 
 Who You Are:
 You are passionate about wireless technology and like to learn and work with the latest test and measurement techniques and equipment. You take time to ask questions and define the problem and make learning a priority. You work effectively in a group environment, solve problems, diagnose technical issues and propose reasonable solutions. You prepare content for communication that is impactful and reach out to mentors with questions to help drive progress and completion of goals and tasks.
 For This Role, You Will Need:
 Ongoing academic studies in Electrical Engineering, Information System Technology, Computer Science, or comparable field of studies (from 4th semester)
 Basic programming knowledge (e.g., Python or C/C++)
 Interest in data science and/or AI with a solid understanding of basic statistical methods
 Basic knowledge of RF engineering
 Basic knowledge of measurement systems and how to take meaningful measurements
 Good command of spoken and written English and/or German
 Availability for 4 to 6 months starting in April 2027
 This internship must be a mandatory part of your studies 
 Preferred Qualifications That Set You Apart:
 Experience in the practical use of data analysis tools or methods (in classification & regression, estimation & prediction, data correlation, principal component analysis, …)
 Experience in the practical use of machine learning, deep learning, artificial intelligence tools or methods (AI APIs and toolchains, PyTorch, Tensorflow, …)
 Experience in taking measurements in the field of electronics, ideally RF engineering, including knowledge about typical uses of instrumentation like signal & arbitrary waveform generators, as well as signal, spectrum, & network analyzers
 Good knowledge of programming and RF engineering
 Ability to work independently in a structured way 
 Our Culture & Commitment to You:
 We attach great importance to your development, so we want to make sure that you not only find a challenging job with us, but also a workplace that is supportive and rewarding. You'll receive structured onboarding as well as dedicated support from professional mentors.
 Practical expertise imparted:
 Opportunity to work with the latest measurement techniques and equipment 
 Experience in improving technology day by day
 Get insight into how day-to-day business looks like for an engineer 
 Work in an international R&D team 
 In addition, you can expect a variety of attractive employee benefits:
 Fair compensation
 Flexible working hours
 Team events and free drinks, fruits, and veggies 
 We are committed to providing an inclusive, open, and welcoming workplace environment for people, helping all to make the most of their professional potential.
