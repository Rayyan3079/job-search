Job Description
 What you’ll bring 
 You're a student with a curiosity for digital platforms and how technology drives business outcomes. You adapt quickly, stay resilient as priorities shift, and bring emotional intelligence and cultural awareness to every interaction.
 Currently enrolled at a university or university of applied sciences - preferred fields include Digital Marketing, Business Administration, or Computer Linguistics
 Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint); knowledge of AI tools is a plus
 Strong communication skills and a collaborative approach to working across teams
 An organised, proactive mindset with the ability to prioritise effectively across multiple deliverables
 Technical curiosity and a genuine interest in how digital products and platforms work - paired with a drive for building knowledge and relationships
 Fluency in German and English is required for this role.
 Office presence is required 1x per week
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems, whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools, including agentic AI, to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You’ll Build
 You'll work on real deliverables, not shadow tasks. Your contributions connect directly to the team's goals, and you'll leave with a portfolio of SAP work you can build on.
 Support planning and project management across key areas of sap.com, including non-marketing email campaigns, specific use cases, roadmap planning, and PI planning
 Help organise projects in line with requirements, advising on materials and services as needed
 Build working relationships with stakeholders to deliver a positive client experience
 Coordinate production schedules through early communication with lines of business and development teams
 Develop expertise in digital products and SAP's broader digital landscape
 Provide operational support for the team's day-to-day activities
 Where you belong 
 The SAP Digital Platforms team manages customer-facing digital touchpoints and capabilities across 49 countries. The team covers sap.com, PartnerEdge.sap.com, Support.sap.com, Developer.sap.com, and campaign and microsite environments. In this role, you'll support programme management and a range of digital capabilities, working closely with stakeholders across the business. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen, a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.
Additional Information
 Please apply initially with your resume in table form. In a later stage we will ask you to upload also school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships).
