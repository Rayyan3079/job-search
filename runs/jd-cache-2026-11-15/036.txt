TransferGo is a growing fintech scale-up on a mission to champion financial freedom with care. We strive to provide tailored, more affordable financial services that make people's hard-earned money go further. Now in our 14th year, with close to 400 employees in offices across Europe and the UK, we’re crafting a brilliant, relevant product that makes a difference in people's lives and the well-being of their families.
We’ve come this far by building a talented, diverse workforce on a fair culture and our strong values. Having this strong team of employees, we can serve those who really need our product to make their lives better.
Data team builds and runs TransferGo's data platform and supports business domains with analytics. Analysts work directly with their domains and turn data into pricing and growth decisions.
Your analysis directly shapes pricing and growth decisions in a remittance business operating across many corridors. You work on a modern data platform, close to decision makers, with real ownership of your domain.
 Here’s what you’ll be doing as Data Analyst:
 Extract and prepare data from the data warehouse and internal systems using SQL
 Analyse pricing and growth performance and turn findings into clear business recommendations
 Design, run and evaluate experiments (e.g. A/B tests of price changes and growth initiatives)
 Build and maintain dashboards and recurring reports for Pricing and Growth
 Monitor competitor pricing and market data
 Work with Data Engineers on metric definitions and the quality of data sets
 Use AI tools to speed up analysis and automate repetitive work
 You’ll report to our Head of Data.
 Here’s what we’d love from our new Data Analyst:
 2–4 years of experience as a Data Analyst, working closely with business teams
 Strong SQL skills
 Experience with at least one BI tool (Tableau, Looker, Power BI or similar)
 Good grasp of statistics and experiment design: you can plan an A/B test, read its results and tell a real effect from noise
 Ability to turn a vague business question into a clear analysis and explain the findings to non-technical people
 Hands-on use of AI in daily work, e.g. writing and checking SQL with AI assistants, automating recurring reports, speeding up exploratory analysis
 Fluent English, written and spoken
 And if you can also do this stuff, even better: 
 Python for data analysis
 Web scraping (e.g. collecting competitor or market data)
 Experience in fintech
 Experience in pricing
 Experience with data modelling tools such as dbt
