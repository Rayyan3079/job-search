 About Frigoblock
 FRIGOBLOCK GmbH is currently the largest German manufacturer and the third-largest European supplier of transport refrigeration units. Since its foundation in 1978, FRIGOBLOCK has been developing, manufacturing, and marketing highly efficient, fuel-saving, and environmentally responsible electrically driven transport refrigeration systems. With this technology, FRIGOBLOCK holds a leading position worldwide. Since March 2015, FRIGOBLOCK has been part of Trane Technologies. At its Essen site, the company employs around 170 people and produces up to 2,500 high-performance refrigeration units annually.
