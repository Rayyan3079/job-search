 About The Role
 Our Global Digital & Business Services Unit at the Aschheim / Munich location is looking for a Working Student DTBE (Digital Transformation & Business Enabelment) Management Reporting for a period of at least 12 months and up to 20 hours per week.
 What You Will Do
 Support the monthly closing and quarterly forecasting processes and contribute to regular month-end reporting using Excel and Power BI.
 Manage and coordinate end-to-end master data processes while helping ensure high standards of data quality and governance.
 Execute SAP reports and support ETL processes to ensure the delivery of high-quality and reliable data.
 Contribute to process automation and continuous improvement initiatives using Power Automate, AI Agents and other digital solutions.
 Take ownership of maintaining and continuously improving the team’s SharePoint sites, including access management and authorization administration.
 Who You Are
 Current Bachelor’s or Master’s studies in Computer Science, Engineering, Business Administration or a related field at a German university or university of applied sciences.
 Initial experience with tools such as Excel (especially Power Query), Power BI, Power Automate, SharePoint, SAP or Visual Basic, gained through university, internships, student jobs or other practical experience.
 An analytical and solution-oriented mindset, combined with curiosity and enthusiasm for learning new tools and technologies.
 A collaborative approach to teamwork and strong time management skills to successfully balance work and studies.
 Fluency in written and spoken English.
