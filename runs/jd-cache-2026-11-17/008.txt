Support the digitalization and standardization of Facility Management processes
Assist with the documentation, analysis, and preparation of relevant data

Currently enrolled in a relevant degree program, such as Facility Management, Real Estate Management, Business Administration, Industrial Engineering, Business Informatics, or a comparable field
Strong IT affinity and confidence in working with structured data and digital tools
Very good written and spoken English skills
Strong communication skills and confidence in working with international stakeholders
Structured, reliable, and independent way of working
High level of accuracy and attention to detail
Interest in Facility Management, real estate, and digitalization
