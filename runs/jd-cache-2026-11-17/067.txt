Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying business, information systems, or a related field — and you're drawn to where strategy, data, and execution meet.
 First experience in any management or business operations student role is required — strategy, PMO, or strategy operations is a plus
 A structured mindset — you simplify complexity and create clarity from ambiguity
 Strong ownership: you take initiative and follow through on commitments
 A customer-oriented perspective that shapes how you approach problems and stakeholders
 Confidence communicating with diverse audiences, including senior leadership
 The ability to manage multiple workstreams and stay focused as priorities shift
 Fluency (minimum C1) in English and German is required for this role
 You are currently enrolled and will remain a student for at least one more year
 The role is based in Garching, near Munich, with office presence required some days per week. Alternatively, the location Walldorf would also be possible. We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems — whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools — including agentic AI — to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work inside SAP's Data & AI unit on real priorities that connect directly to SAP's Autonomous Enterprise agenda.
 Turn strategic priorities into clear plans, milestones, and deliverables
 Build controlling dashboards that create a single source of truth for KPIs and progress
 Set up governance cadences that provide transparency and keep execution on track
 Craft executive-ready updates and presentations that make Data & AI topics clear
 Coordinate stakeholder alignment: drive follow-ups and connect the right people at the right time
 Develop internal communications — newsletters and key messages — to keep the team informed and inspired
 Where you belong
 This role puts you at the heart of Global Delivery MEE Data & AI — where strategy becomes execution. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen — a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers. This is a place to build real skills, take ownership from day one, and grow a network that lasts.
