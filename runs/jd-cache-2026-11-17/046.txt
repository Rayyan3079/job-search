Discover your exciting role
 As a Working Student IT (m/f/d) , you will support our employees with their day-to-day IT-related requests and act as a link between users and the relevant IT support teams. You will create and follow up on support tickets, helping to ensure that requests are addressed and resolved promptly. The role primarily focuses on user support and the coordination of IT requests .
 Explore Your Tasks And Responsibilities
 First point of contact for employees regarding day-to-day IT requests and support with basic hardware and software-related questions.
 Creation and follow-up of IT support tickets, including checking that all required information is complete.
 Coordination of communication between users, internal IT teams, and international support teams.
 Support with access-related issues, for example concerning mobile phones, printers, and systems, as well as coordination of required access rights with the responsible IT teams.
 Support with the provision, setup, and commissioning of new IT equipment.
 Monitoring of open issues, such as missing software, access rights, or interfaces to ERP systems, including escalation and follow-up with the responsible teams.
 On-site support at office locations and construction sites in and around Dresden.
 Assistance with the technical equipment and IT setup of new locations.
 Show Your Expertise
 Currently enrolled in a degree program in Information Technology or a related field, with a strong interest in IT topics and digital tools.
 Confident written and verbal communication skills in German and English, combined with an open and approachable manner when interacting with different stakeholders.
 Structured and reliable way of working, with persistence in following up on open issues and the ability to stay organized when handling multiple requests simultaneously.
 Strong service orientation and enthusiasm for teamwork and collaborative problem-solving.
 Willingness to work regularly from the office and occasionally provide support at construction sites in and around Dresden.
