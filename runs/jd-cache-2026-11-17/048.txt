Job Description
 What you’ll bring
 You're a student ready to contribute alongside your studies. You're curious, adapt quickly, and stay resilient as priorities evolve. You think creatively, communicate clearly, and collaborate well across teams. You're building your foundations in business and AI — and you take initiative to apply them. You bring emotional intelligence and cultural awareness to every interaction.
 Enrolled student (f/m/d) at a university or university of applied sciences – pursuing a Master's degree, or in an advanced Bachelor's programme with the intention to continue towards a Master's. Preferred fields of study: Computer Science, Natural Sciences
 Developer skills: AI Day-to-Day Practice, Proficiency in at least one server-side programming language such as Java or Python as well as UI development (Typescript)
 Language skills: fluent in English
 Soft skills: Team player
 You're curious about AI and machine learning, enjoy working independently and taking initiative, and you like solving problems as part of a team. Prior experience with cloud and AI technologies is a plus, but above all we value your willingness to learn.
 What You’ll Build
 You'll work on real deliverables, not shadow tasks. Your contributions connect directly to your team's goals. You'll leave with a portfolio of SAP work you can build on.
We are looking for a working student (f/m/d) who joins us in developing cloud services for automated document processing. Use the solution to reduce manual effort and errors through intelligent matching and automation. Achieve greater accuracy and informed decision-making with AI.
 We are integrating machine learning models including generative AI and our service is already adopted by hundreds of customers.
 Your tasks will include refining backlog items, designing and developing software in Java and presenting your work results in the team.
 You will work closely together with a team of dedicated professionals including developers, machine learning experts, architects and fellow students.
 You will gain hands-on experience with Git, CI processes and the latest software development trends.
 As a fully integrated team member, you will actively participate in our agile setup and make a significant impact
 Where you belong
 As a working student you will be part of SAP Next Gen, a global network of students and early professionals growing together. You'll access mentors, development programmes, and peers building careers alongside you. It's a place to start something real.
In our Document AI team, we turn structured, unstructured or semi-structured documents into reliable, structured data at scale – removing manual, repetitive work so businesses can focus on what matters. We've worked successfully with students for many years and are happy to share our knowledge with you.
Additional Information
Please apply initially with your resume in table form. In a later stage we will ask you to upload also school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships).
This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
