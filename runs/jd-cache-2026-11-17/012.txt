A lot of what German companies ask us to build an application around starts or ends in SAP, whether it is a purchase order, a goods receipt or an invoice. Thread & Logic is hiring an Associate SAP Consultant in Germany for that side of our projects.  Projects you'd join Work out which SAP fields and transactions an application needs to read or update
 Test interfaces between our applications and SAP S/4HANA or ECC in a test system
 Configure small changes in SAP MM or SD together with a senior colleague
 Write test cases and record the results for each release
 Answer user questions about the SAP side of an application after go-live
 To start, you need A degree in business informatics, business administration or computer science, or completed commercial training
 First contact with SAP, in a job, an internship, a university course or SAP training
 A basic understanding of purchasing, sales or accounting processes
 Excel, including filters, pivot tables and XLOOKUP
 Good extras An SAP S/4HANA certification or a course in MM, SD or FI
 Basic SQL or ABAP
 IDocs, OData or REST APIs
 From Thread & Logic 30 days of holiday a year
 Company pension plan with an employer contribution
 Yearly training budget for courses and certifications
 A laptop and home office equipment

 Thread & Logic in short
 Thread & Logic builds custom business applications in Dallas, Texas. We design internal tools, connect them to the systems a team already uses and add AI where it can prepare the next step, while the person using the application makes the decision and the application records it.
