 The opportunity
 At Vantage Towers, we’re on a mission to power Europe’s sustainable digital transformation. As a leading tower company, we’re ushering in an era of technology-driven advances to help connect people, businesses, and internet-enabled devices like never before. We combine the scale, stability and quality of our tower network with the agility, optimism and energy of a start-up.
As a young TowerCo, we have already achieved strong results with a continued focus on accelerating growth and a special emphasis put on sustainability. As part of our team, you’ll work in a dynamic and multicultural environment that embraces open communication, collaboration and teamwork.
If you’re ready to take responsibility and shape the future of telco infrastructure with us, then let’s level up in your career and reach the top, together.
 Your Responsibilities
 Deployment Support & Quality Assurance: You support the deployment team in creating, maintaining, and assuring the quality of project and release documentation, tracking board feedback, and managing document formats.
 Project Tracking & Execution: You assist in executing grid connection projects by tracking documentation, managing digital signatures (DocuSign), and overseeing incoming mail and project closure correspondence.
 Administration & Contract Management: You review goods receipts, support administrative approval processes, prepare contracts for signature, and handle digital filing and archiving.
 Process Automation & AI: You actively support the digitization and automation of administrative processes by utilizing modern tools and AI applications to optimize workflows. 
 Your Profile
 Educational Background: You are currently enrolled in a degree program, ideally in Business Engineering, Business Administration, Telecommunications, Computer Science, or a related field.
 Technical Skills & Tools: You possess initial experience with Microsoft Office (especially Excel, Word, Outlook) and show strong interest or experience in AI-driven tools, data analysis, reporting, and process optimization.
 Soft Skills & Work Ethic: You bring a structured, conscientious working style, excellent organizational skills, strong reliability, and a high level of personal initiative.
 Industry Passion: You have a strong interest in project management as well as telecommunications or infrastructure projects.
 Language Proficiency: You have fluent written and spoken German skills as well as good English skills. 
 What We Offer
 A diverse, multicultural setup based on our values – accountability, respect, teamwork, and trust – and the unique opportunity to shape the organisation
 An attractive salary package depending on your experience and qualifications
 Maximum flexibility: With Full-Flex Office, we support you in combining university and work easily. You can freely decide how you’ll work upon agreement with your manager and even work from other EU countries for up to 20 days per year.
 Extensive learning opportunities and individual support for your professional and personal development with the opportunity to secure permanent employment after graduation
 A buddy who will help you with any questions and support you in your ongoing work life
 On top: a total of 30 days in holidays per year, fitness and health offers,and access to corporate benefits
 Are you ready to create the future of digital telco infrastructure together with us? Then we are happy to receive your CV. Depending on the role, we generally invite you to two-three rounds of interviews before you receive an offer.
We are committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where everyone can thrive. Applications from all individuals, regardless of background, identity, or ability are more than welcome. If you require any reasonable adjustments during the recruitment process, please contact us so we can support you.
