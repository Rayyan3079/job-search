Working Student - Product Delivery (f/m/d) (Frankfurt am Main) - Deutsche Börse AG Working Student - Product Delivery (f/m/d) Company: Deutsche Börse AG Your area of work The Product Delivery unit serves as a central interface between a significant number of internal departments and is responsible for the planning, organization, coordination and tracking of the relevant activities required for the successful introduction and rollout of software releases and the implementation of new processes for Trading Systems of the Deutsche Börse Group. Your responsibilities: You will have an excellent opportunity to take responsibility in strategic projects, gain interesting insights into our company, and the trading platforms of both the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and the Derivatives Exchange Eurex You will be involved in project management and project coordination, for example by preparing and following up on project meetings and helping to prepare and check reports for the project management and stakeholders, including the use of AI-based tools to support analysis, documentation and administrative tasks Additionally, you will activiely contribute to developing the Product Delivery area with new ideas and innovative suggestions for automating internal processes, including the identification and implementation of suitable AI-supported solutions You will co-ordinate activities and cooperate with other internal departments and participation in cross divisional meetings Furthermore, you will create documentation and statistics to support internal processes and coordination, using AI-based tools where appropriate Your profile: You are enrolled during the entire period of activity at a state-recognized university as a regular student and have completed at least 1 semester (Study focus in the field of informatics, mathematics, physics, economics, business studies, humanities or other relevant fields) Your communication and cooperation skills help you to integrate quickly into the well-established team. Your sense of responsibility and your analytical and organizational skills help you to familiarize yourself with new and interesting topics You are proficient with Microsoft Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Sharepoint and ideally complemented with a strong interest in AI and experience with Microsoft Copilot, Power Automate or similar tools for process automation and development of collaborative solutions Furthermore, you are fluent in written and spoken English; German is a plus Start date: 15/01/2027 What we offer International work environment Hybrid work and flexible working hours Meal allowance at our canteen Free access to e-learning platforms For working students: monthly internet allowance and eligibility for EGYM Wellpass participation Dedicated student and intern networking community and events How Does the Process Look Like?
