About Dunkermotoren
 Dunkermotoren, an AMETEK company, is one of the leading manufacturers in the field of drive technology. For more than 75 years, we have been developing and manufacturing high-quality and intelligent complete solutions in the field of precision drive technology at our site in Bonndorf as well as at other international locations. Our portfolio includes brushless and brushed DC motors, linear systems, and AC motors. As a full-service provider, we offer comprehensive solutions in the fields of drive technology, automation, and digitalization.
 Your Position
 To support our data-driven business management, we are looking for a Working Student (m/f/d) Business Intelligence & Data Analytics to support us in developing a company-wide reporting and KPI cockpit based on Microsoft Power BI . The position is designed for 15–20 hours per week, with a start date by agreement .
In this position, you will work closely with the Business Unit Management and various departments and actively contribute to bringing together data from different areas of the company, developing meaningful KPIs, and establishing a consistent reporting structure.
You will gain insights into different business areas such as Sales, Operations, Supply Chain, Service, and Finance and will have the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and actively shape the further development of our reporting landscape.
 Your Responsibilities
 Conceptual design and development of a company-wide Power BI cockpit
 Analysis, preparation, and consolidation of data from various systems such as ERP, CRM, Excel, and databases
 Creation and further development of data models, KPIs, and management dashboards
 Development and automation of reporting solutions
 Close collaboration with the Business Unit Management and various departments
 Identification of improvement opportunities regarding data quality and reporting processes
 Support in further developing a data-driven corporate culture
 Documentation of data sources, KPI definitions, and reporting standards
