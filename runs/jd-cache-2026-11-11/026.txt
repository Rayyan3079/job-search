Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're a student ready to contribute alongside your studies. You're curious, adapt quickly, and stay resilient as priorities evolve. You think creatively, communicate clearly, and collaborate well across teams. You're building your foundations in business and AI, and you take initiative to apply them. You bring emotional intelligence and cultural awareness to every interaction.
You're currently enrolled in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Software Engineering, or a comparable programme.
 Basic knowledge of at least one modern programming language: Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, or Python
 Interest in cloud technologies and modern software development practices
 Initiative, a structured way of working, and a genuine drive to get things done
 Depending on your chosen focus: familiarity with CI/CD, shell scripting, GitHub Actions, or Cloud Foundry, or experience with SAPUI5/OpenUI5, REST APIs, or database querying
 Fluency in both English and German is required for this role
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems, whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools, including agentic AI, to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work on real deliverables, not shadow tasks. Your contributions connect directly to your team's goals. You'll leave with a portfolio of SAP work you can build on.
 Automation Pipeline for Central Deployment
 Integrate SAP's internal Hyperspace tooling as the foundation for the pipeline infrastructure.
 Build and configure GitHub Actions workflows to automatically trigger build, test, and deployment processes
 Implement a complete Dev → Staging → Production deployment cycle with promotion mechanisms and gating logic
 Write smoke tests and additional automated tests to ensure service quality after every deployment
 Usage Report Extension for the Cloud Service
 Develop a SAPUI5 interface that visualises customer usage data in an interactive data table with charts and graphs
 Implement server-side logic to query and process usage data, with data isolation and tenant separation
 Build scheduled background jobs for automated data aggregation and on-demand report generation for individual customers
 Design and implement offline report availability as a downloadable PDF or structured data format
 Where you belong
 As a working student you will be part of SAP Next Gen, a global network of students and early professionals growing together. You'll access mentors, development programmes, and peers building careers alongside you. It's a place to start something real. You'll join the Forms Services team, building a cloud-native SaaS solution on SAP Business Technology Platform. The team is agile and international, with a focus on technical excellence and continuous improvement. The role is partially onsite in Walldorf, with flexible hours and the option to work remotely. You'll have a dedicated mentor and a direct point of contact in the team.
Additional Information
 Please apply initially with your resume in table form. In a later stage we will ask you to upload also school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships).
