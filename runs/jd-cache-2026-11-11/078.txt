 Job Description
 Your role:
 We are looking for our next Intern in the Quantitative Research for a minimum of 6 months based in Frankfurt am Main !
During this internship, you'll support the quantitative investment research by combining rigorous statistical methods with modern machine learning and responsible AI tools to improve research efficiency, portfolio insights, and investment outcomes.
 Key Responsibilities:
 Develop and improve quantitative and factor-based investment strategies for institutional and retail clients globally.
 Investigate tools, techniques, and metrics, including appropriate AI-assisted methods, that can provide deeper portfolio insight and improve client outcomes.
 Prepare, validate, and analyze financial and alternative datasets; use AI-enabled workflows where appropriate while maintaining data quality, reproducibility, and confidentiality.
 Participate in the development and evolution of alpha signals, portfolio construction, and risk-management approaches.
 Perform thorough research and statistical analysis for strategy development and ongoing management, with robust out-of-sample testing and clear documentation.
 Contribute to exploratory projects that apply machine learning or generative AI to quantitative research and workflow improvement, with guidance from senior researchers.
 Ensure all activities are in line with Invesco's Conduct principles and applicable governance for data, models, intellectual property, and responsible AI use.
 You'll need to have:
 Experience working with SQL and cloud-based environments such as AWS is a plus.
 Excellent working knowledge of econometrics and statistics, including time-series analysis.
 Exposure to machine learning or generative AI through coursework, research projects, or previous experience is a plus.
 Experience with financial databases such as Compustat, Worldscope, or Datastream, and risk and optimization tools such as Axioma or Barra, is beneficial.
 Very strong analytical and mathematical skills, including optimization techniques.
 Good coding skills, preferably in Python or R; familiarity with Git and reproducible research practices is desirable.
 Basic understanding of machine-learning concepts and an interest in applying AI techniques to quantitative investment research.
 Familiarity with modern AI tools, including generative AI and coding assistants, and an ability to use them critically and responsibly.
 Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical methods clearly.
 Working towards an advanced degree in a quantitative field such as economics, finance, computer science, data science, applied mathematics, physics, or a related discipline.
 Excellent academic background with demonstrated strength in quantitative coursework or research.
 Evidence of applied problem-solving through coursework, research projects, competitions, or relevant internships is valued.
 Highly motivated and able to collaborate across groups while remaining focused on high-quality research execution.
 Curious and pragmatic about emerging technology, with sound judgment regarding where AI adds value and where human review remains essential.
 Ability to communicate effectively with quantitative researchers, portfolio managers, client portfolio managers, distribution teams, intermediaries, and clients.
 Passion for quantitative, research-driven investment management; results-oriented and a dependable teammate.
