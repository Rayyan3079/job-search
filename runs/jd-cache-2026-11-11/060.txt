As a Working Student in our Reefer Excellence Team you will play an active role in shaping the future of our reefer products, processes and services. Working closely with the Global Reeder Product Excellence Team. This is a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a dynamic, global environment and contribute to strategic projects from day one.
 What You'll Do 
 Support the Reefer Product Excellence team in ongoing and upcoming commercial, operational, technical and digital projects
 Assist in the development of online training modules related to reefer technology and product offerings
 Conduct benchmarking and market research on reefer-related products and industry trends
 Maintain and update internal product documents, reports and strategic roadmaps
 Collaborate on the creation if sales collateral and customer-facing materials
 Contribute to quarterly business reviews and help maintain key product files and assessments 
 What Makes You A Fit 
 Currently enrolled in a full-time study program in logistics, supply chain, business administration, engineering or a related field with availability of approximately 12 months
 Strong organizational, analytical and communication skills
 Motivated, customer-focused and a dependable team player
 Proficient in MS PowerPoint and Excel, with an efficient and structured working style
 Basic understanding of logistics and/or reefer technology is an advantage
 Ability to work both independently and within a global, diverse team environment
 Fluency in written and spoken English; proficiency in Spanish is a plus
 Eagerness to learn and adapt to new tools, technologies and market dynamics 
 What We Offer You 
 Be a part of our Student community: meet other students and exchange ideas during our Student Lunch & Coffee Breaks
 If flexibility is important to you: choose the working hours that best fit your college schedule
 Work with us directly in the heart of Hamburg at the Binnenalster or mobile at home
 Of course, we also take care of your physical well-being: in our company restaurant we offer yummy dishes for small and large appetites
 Take part in our company sports activities such as table tennis, dragon boat racing and yoga. And if you're in the mood for music, why not join our band?
 Attractive training programs and excellent career opportunities complete your experience
 About Us 
 Hapag-Lloyd is one of the world’s leading liner shipping companies, connecting businesses and people across more than 600 ports worldwide . With a fleet of over 300 modern container ships and a vessel capacity of 2.5 million TEU , we keep global trade moving reliably every day.
 Our global network spans 139 countries, 396 offices and a growing portfolio of terminal and infrastructure investments. This scale enables us to deliver consistent, high-quality service across continents and to support our customers in even the most complex supply chains.
 When you join us, you become part of more than 19,600 colleagues working across borders, functions and cultures, to not only deliver quality for our customers, but to create innovation and opportunities across roles, regions and perspectives.
 We believe that every exploration is a chance to grow, and every port is a place to belong. Your journey, our horizon.
