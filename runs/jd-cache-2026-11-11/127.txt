About the role: 
 As a Working Student, you will be a key part of our Product Management Team to work on the entire product life cycle that includes first idea, development, go-to-market and continuous support. You’ll push boundaries of unmanned ground vehicle technologies together with the UGV Product Manager.
 You will be working closely with stakeholders from the R&D, Production, Maintenance, Marketing, Sales and/or Supply Chain departments to bring our products forward - your mission will have a powerful impact.
 What is your Day to Day Mission 
 Ensure progress of product related initiatives
 Develop product documentation
 Gather customer feedback and incorporate it into the product roadmap
 Create operational scenarios, mission workflows, and user journeys
 Analyze user needs and derive operational and product requirements
 Support customer workshops, field trials, and preparation of technical briefings.
 What you bring to the team: 
 You have completed a degree/apprenticeship in the field of engineering or economics.
 You are privately interested in technology, robotics, or unmanned ground systems.
 Due to your open, communicative, friendly, and competent attitude, it is easy for you to be considered an internal expert.
 You are highly self-motivated and drive your area of responsibility independently.
 Communication in English is a matter of course for you.

 About us: 
 Quantum Systems develops advanced autonomous systems for defense and security operations across multiple domains, powered by integrated software and AI. Quantum Systems is at the forefront of AI-powered aerial, ground, naval robotics, developing a family of smart systems that go far beyond traditional drones. Built on a foundation of in-house hardware, software and AI, our systems transform data into critical insights, empowering decision-making across security, defense and public safety, as well as for the commercial, industrial and GIS sectors. Our vertically integrated system with industry leading endurance, enable seamless interoperability and software interconnectivity. On-board AI powers multi-modality sensing, autonomous navigation, anti-jamming, swarm operations and more.
