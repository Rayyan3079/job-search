 Write SQL and dbt models that turn raw tables into reporting tables
 Handle first loads, later changes and resent files so that no record is counted twice
 Add tests that compare model output with the source records
 Document what each table holds and where its data comes from
 What we check for in your CV A degree in computer science, data science, mathematics or a related field, or equivalent training
 SQL with joins, GROUP BY and window functions
 Python for scripts or data processing
 At least one data project, at university, in an internship or at work
 Not required, but useful dbt, Airflow or a similar tool
 Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks or Azure Synapse
 Git
 Working at Grainset 30 vacation days
 Employer-funded company pension
 Training budget for courses and certifications
 Laptop and home office setup
 Flexible hours with a daily overlap with Boston
