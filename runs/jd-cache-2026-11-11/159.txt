 Your Responsibilities
 As a Working Student in the Project Management Office (PMO) , you will assist in driving process improvement initiatives, coordinating stakeholder feedback and ensuring high-quality project documentation.
 You will assist in preparing presentations, meeting materials and project-related content, while contributing to the standardisation and optimisation of PMO processes. By working closely with colleagues from various departments, you will gain valuable hands-on experience in project management, cross-functional collaboration and the practical application of AI-powered tools in a dynamic business environment.
 Process Analysis & Improvement: Support the analysis and documentation of existing PMO processes and contribute to developing recommendations for process optimisation.
 AI-Supported Concept Development: Work with the PMO to develop and improve concepts using AI-powered tools to support PMO activities and boost efficiency.
 Feedback Coordination: Collect, consolidate, structure and prepare feedback from various stakeholders, including project managers, Implementation Services, Sales and Consulting.
 Meeting Preparation: Prepare presentations, agendas and supporting materials for internal and customer-facing meetings.
 Documentation Standardisation: Support the creation and standardisation of project documentation in line with established internal standards and best practice.
 Administrative Support: Assist with organisational and administrative activities within the PMO as required.
