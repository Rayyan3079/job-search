Who are we?
 Endress+Hauser is a global leader in measurement instrumentation, services, and solutions for industrial process engineering. Even with more than 18,000 employees worldwide, we have remained a family-owned company and take pride in our welcoming and supportive work environment. Working with us always combines two dimensions: the technical and the human. The result: greater satisfaction. Every day.
 What is this position about?
 Become part of our development team at our Freiburg office and help shape digital cloud solutions for the future of Product, Asset, and Sustainability Management. You will gain first-hand experience with SAP BTP, modern software architectures, and practical development processes.
 What will you do?
 Contribute on-site to the development of digital solutions for Product, Asset, and Sustainability Management topics
 Gain first-hand experience with the SAP Business Technology Platform (SAP BTP) and modern cloud technologies in an on-site environment
 Take ownership of your own tasks within the development process and support the implementation of new requirements
 Explore new technologies and gain hands-on experience in real-world projects
 Work closely with experienced colleagues and continuously develop your skills
 What makes you a great fit?
 You are studying Computer Science, Business Information Systems, Software Engineering, Data Science, or a comparable IT-related degree program
 You are interested in software development, cloud technologies, and modern platforms
 You work independently, in a structured manner, and have a strong willingness to learn
 You are curious, enjoy trying out new technologies, and are passionate about developing solutions
 Initial experience with a programming language such as Node.js, Python, or similar technologies is an advantage
 Ideally, you are interested in topics such as Cloud Computing, SAP BTP, APIs, Data Integration, or Artificial Intelligence
 You communicate openly, enjoy working as part of a team, and take responsibility for your tasks
 You have good written and spoken German or English skills
 You can dedicate approximately 10 hours of your free time per week to this role
 You live in the Freiburg region
 What can you expect from us?
 Professional and personal mentoring from experienced supervisors
 The opportunity to take ownership and contribute to challenging projects
 A state-of-the-art working environment in an internationally operating family-owned company
 State-of-the-art equipment to help you get started successfully
 A great office environment and, by arrangement, the option to work partly from home
 A variety of company and team events
 A Germany-wide public transport ticket included!
