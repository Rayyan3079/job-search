Mode of Employment: Fixed Term / 15-20 hours / week
Join us in Munich as a Working Student (f/m/d) Advanced Analytics and Artificial Intelligence and help shape the future by turning cutting-edge data and AI concepts into real solutions. Your ideas and curiosity will drive innovation from day one! Ready to dive into cloud tech, machine learning, and impactful teamwork?
Connect, learn, and grow with our Working Student Network – your space to exchange ideas, build meaningful relationships, and navigate your Siemens journey.
 You'll make an impact by
 You actively support the conceptualization and execution of advanced analytics and artificial intelligence projects, bringing your innovative perspectives into the team
 By collaborating closely with colleagues, you extract, validate, and transform data using cloud-native platforms like Snowflake and Azure, ensuring data quality and accessibility
 Leveraging your technical know-how, you assist in developing and maintaining data pipelines in cloud environments and contribute to applying machine learning algorithms with Python and relevant frameworks
 Furthermore, you help create interactive dashboards and reports, translating complex analytics into actionable insights for stakeholders
 Throughout your tasks, you participate in workshops and help prepare training materials on emerging AI/ML technologies, fostering knowledge sharing within the organization
 This Is How You'll Win Us Over
 Education: You are currently successfully studying business informatics, statistics, mathematics, physics, data science, engineering, or a similar field
 Experience and Skills:
 You have practical experience in analytics, data science, software development, or related areas through internships, academic projects, or research
 You demonstrate proficiency in Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn), SQL, and have hands-on experience with cloud data platforms such as Snowflake, Azure, or AWS
 You are familiar with machine learning algorithms, model evaluation and deployment, version control (Git), and collaborative development workflows
 Ways of Working: You are eager to learn, think analytically, and communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders
 Languages: You have excellent English skills (minimum C1 level); German is an advantage
