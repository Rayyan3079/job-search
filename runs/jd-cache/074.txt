 Join us in making a difference
 Our Service Desk team within Content Data Quality & Governance is looking for a detail-oriented working student to join our dynamic environment as a Content Data Engineer. You'll be part of a team that ensures the quality and accuracy of our hotel content data, working with various stakeholders across trivago to maintain high data standards and resolve content-related issues.
If this sounds like an environment you can see yourself enjoying, read on and apply today!
 How you’ll make an impact:
 Analyze & Monitor Data – Use SQL to query, analyze, and validate content data for inconsistencies
 Triage Data Anomalies – Identify, investigate, and escalate content quality issues, collaborating with engineers for resolution
 Support Data Engineering – Assist in improving data quality practices, ensuring clean, structured, and optimized data pipelines
 Optimize Manual Processes – Help automate and refine existing workflows to enhance operational efficiency
 Collaborate with Teams – Work closely with content teams, data engineers, and stakeholders to improve data governance
 What you’ll learn:
 How a data-driven company operates and makes decisions
 Best practices in SQL, data analysis, and quality assurance
 How to support data engineers in optimizing content data pipelines
 Collaboration within a fast-paced, international tech team
 What you'll need to thrive:
 SQL-Savvy – Comfortable writing SQL queries for data analysis and quality checks
 Analytical Thinker – Able to spot data inconsistencies and propose solutions
 Team Player – Excited to work with engineers, analysts, and stakeholders
 Process-Oriented – Passionate about optimizing workflows and reducing manual effort
 Availability – Can commit 20 hours per week for at least one year
 Currently enrolled at a university in Germany, living within a commutable distance to Düsseldorf
 Stand out with:
 Knowledge of Python for data analysis or automation
 Experience with data visualization tools (Looker, Tableau, Power BI, or similar)
 Familiarity with ETL pipelines or data processing frameworks
 Experience in agentic automation (e.g., building AI agents or automated workflows)
 Some preliminary understanding of working with AI tools such as Cursor and Claude
