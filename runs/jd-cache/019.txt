 Where you belong
 Objectives 
 The Business Transformation Network is part of Value Management in SAP’s Business Transformation Management Area.
In today’s dynamic and rapidly changing business landscape, organizations must cultivate a transformation capability to continuously navigate their organization’s future state of readiness while ensuring that strategic initiatives translate into measurable business outcomes and sustainable value realization.
Through connecting companies across industries and regions, the network fosters an open exchange on transformation business challenges, innovative approaches and lessons learned. The overarching aim is to jointly build the transformation capability, accelerate transformation journeys and maximize sustainable business value together.
Key stakeholders on the customer side include C-level executives, upper business management and selected business experts.
 What We Are Doing
 Driving 1:1 Exchanges with Companies & Partners
 Sharing key transformation and business practices from strategy to value.
 Organizing Deep Dives into various transformation topics to build and manage the Transformation Capability and realizing business value
 Engaging in regular touchpoints with network members to check-in on updates and progress.
 Networking via different formats, such as roundtables, masterclasses, working sessions and events. Some of them are organized by us and others we attend as speakers. 
 Managing a Knowledge Hub as the basis for knowledge sharing:
 Identifying and generating transformation stories, content, and presentation materials
 Identifying speakers and connecting experts through the network.
 Benchmarking and consolidating practices.
 Building a strong case for Business Transformation Management story by leveraging Signavio, LeanIX and WalkMe.
 Driving cross activities such as customer office, success measurement and branding:
 Agenda-building, briefing, organizing, executing, moderating and follow-up of session.
 Maintaining a qualitative contact management of customer & sales team data.
 Building standardized communication assets (e.g. Survey, Newsletter).
 Why We Are Doing It
 Help companies to succeed on their E2E transformation journey and build a transformation capability with real business impact.
 Strengthen relationships by building strategic partnerships.
 Predict customer needs, build up pipeline of customers demand, fuel into product development and create options to buy more from SAP.
 Learn from other companies and leverage the feedback internally.
 We share SAP's transformation experience to help the customer understand that transformation is not a project; it is a capability.
