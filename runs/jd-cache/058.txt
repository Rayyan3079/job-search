Join our R&D Innovation Synergies team as a Working Student (x|f|m) and play a key role in advancing the data infrastructure that powers innovative research and development. Working closely with lab scientists and data experts, you will contribute to cutting-edge data engineering initiatives while gaining hands-on experience in data analysis, cloud technologies, and machine learning. Through your work, you will help create the foundation for smarter, data-driven innovation and enable our teams to focus on what matters most: better health for more people.
The position is based in Goettingen, Germany, with a second location option in Bielefeld or Ulm.
As a working student, you benefit in two ways: you gain important work experience and earn some extra money alongside your studies. With a working week of 20 hours, you can continue to concentrate on your studies.
Please apply with your complete application documents in English.
 Grow With Us – Your Responsibilities
 Support permanent employees in day to day tasks on a short term basis
 Help build and maintain robust data pipelines, collaborate with team members to design and maintain data models for analysis and reporting
 Support the Data Science and Innovation team with data preprocessing and preparation, enhancing the quality and usability of data for various projects
 Take over of tasks coming up on short notice, mostly administrative
 Contribute to projects by taking over defined work packages
 What Will Convince Us
 Currently enrolled in a master's degree program in computational biology, Biotechnology, computer science, data science or a related field
 Working knowledge of Python programming is required. Familiarity in libraries such as Pandas and scikit-learn is a plus 
 Willingness to learn new data analytical technologies
 Ability to integrate into teams easily
 Reliability and accuracy
 Willingness to learn and to act flexible when taking over tasks on short notice
 Fluent in English, German an asset
