Join the Data Analytics & AI team at Lufthansa Group Business Services (LGBS) and help us build AI-driven solutions that support real business processes across Lufthansa Group.
Interested? Apply and help us shape the future of AI at Lufthansa Group.
 Tasks
 We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist, Data Scientist or Senior Data Scientist who wants to combine data science with practical software engineering and work with modern AI technologies.
Responsibilities:
 Design, build and evaluate ML models and AI agent solutions for real business use cases
 Work with LLMs, prompting, RAG and agent orchestration
 Develop production-quality Python code using testing, Git and code review practices
 Explore and work with data from SAP, Azure and Databricks
 Use MLflow for experiment tracking and model lifecycle management
 Work with technologies such as Azure AI Foundry and Airflow
 Collaborate with platform engineers to bring AI solutions into production
 Work directly with business stakeholders to understand problems, challenge assumptions and identify the right solution
 Perform all other duties assigned or delegated by supervisors

 Requirements
 What we’re looking for:
 2+ years of experience for Junior/Data Scientist level or 5+ years for Senior level in applied data science or a related area.
 Solid knowledge of statistics, machine learning and model evaluation.
 Strong Python skills and an understanding of software engineering best practices.
 Experience with LLM-based solutions such as prompting, RAG or AI agents.
 Analytical mindset and ability to turn business problems into practical data-driven solutions.
 Strong communication and collaboration skills in an international environment.
 Nice to have:
Experience with Azure AI Foundry, Databricks, SAP Datasphere/BW, MLflow, Airflow, Copilot Studio, Unity Catalog or MLOps.
