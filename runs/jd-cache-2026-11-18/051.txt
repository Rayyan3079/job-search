 Tasks
 Within Mercedes-Benz AG, our department is responsible for the concept, development, and global provision of customer information relating to our vehicles. At the heart of our work is the Mercedes-Benz Owner's Manual, which provides customers with relevant information on vehicle functions and their operation across various digital channels.
 Our team is shaping the Owner's Manual of tomorrow and continuously enhancing existing products, processes, and systems. Our work includes developing new digital usage concepts, data-driven services, and the provision of vehicle information for innovative applications and Artificial Intelligence. From the initial idea through to global rollout, we collaborate closely with Vehicle Development, IT, international markets, and internal as well as external partners.
 Join our team and help shape the digital customer experience at Mercedes-Benz.
 These challenges await you:
 Supporting the development of new digital information products and innovative ways of using Owner's Manual data
 Supporting the provision and quality assurance of data for internal and external applications, particularly AI applications
 Contributing to the analysis and optimisation of existing processes, for example related to how-to videos, languages, and digital publications
 Supporting requirements management and the coordination of various departments, markets, and other stakeholders
 Preparing concepts, analyses, decision-making materials, and presentations
 Contributing to market and competitor analyses as well as identifying new trends and customer needs
 The activity can begin from February 2027.
 Qualifications
 Studies in the field of Media Informatics, Business Informatics, Computational Linguistics, or a comparable course
 Very good written and spoken English skills; additional language skills are an advantage
 Confident use of MS Office (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint)
 Ideally, initial experience with Power BI, Jira, Confluence, or comparable tools
 Strong interest in digital products, technologies, and innovative solutions
 Strong analytical way of thinking as well as a structured and independent way of working
 Very good communication skills, ability to work in a team, and a high level of initiative
 Quick comprehension skills and enthusiasm for contributing own ideas proactively
