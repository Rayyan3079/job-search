RWE Supply & Trading GmbH
 To start as soon as possible, part time (20 hours / week), fixed term (6 months)
 As a Working Student in HR SAP & Finance Administration, you'll support the financial and administrative processes behind our HR activities in Germany and the UK. You'll work extensively with SAP, coordinate with stakeholders across different teans and gradually take ownership of recurring processes, from purchase requisitions through to invoice processing.
 About The Role
 Support day-to-day SAP processes, including purchase requisitions, service entry sheets and invoice-related activities.
 Coordinate with internal stakeholders, suppliers and Accounting to ensure the required information, approvals and documentation are in place.
 Track purchase orders, invoices and outstanding process steps, following up where needed to keep things moving.
 Act as a point of contact for SAP-related administrative requests across our HR teams in Germany and the UK.
 Help maintain accurate records, identify recurring issues and contribute to making administrative processes more efficient.
 Gain insights into different HR functions and, depending on your interests, support additional team activities.
 Job Requirements And Experience
 Currently enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field.
 Basic understanding of financial and accounting processes, particularly purchasing, invoicing and payment workflows.
 Strong affinity for systems and processes, combined with an interest in developing practical SAP skills.
 Structured and detail-oriented approach to work, with the ability to manage multiple requests and follow up on outstanding tasks.
 Strong communication skills in German and English, with confidence in interacting with different internal and external stakeholders.
 Proactive and reliable working style, with a willingness to take ownership of recurring tasks and learn new processes.
 Advantageous, But Not Essential
 Previous experience with SAP or another ERP system through an internship, university project or student job.
 RWE is committed to creating a diverse and inclusive environment – we value your passion, your willingness to learn and your desire to thrive. Even if you don't have all the above skills, need flexible working arrangements or other adjustments not yet listed here, we'd love to hear from you.
 Your Benefits
 Gain hands-on experience in an international company and learn how administrative and financial processes work in a corporate environment.
 Develop practical SAP skills and become familiar with the systems and workflows used in day-to-day business operations.
 Build a strong network across HR and beyond, working closely with different teams and stakeholders in Germany and the UK.
 Get insights into different areas of HR, with opportunities to explore additional topics based on your interests.
 Take on responsibility from an early stage while benefiting from a supportive team that helps you learn and develop your skills.

RWE Supply & Trading is our connection to the world’s energy markets. Headquartered in Essen, Germany, the company operates one of Europe’s largest and most advanced energy trading floors. A diverse team of over 2,300 professionals from around 90 countries trades electricity, gas, commodities, and CO2 emission allowances. With precise market analysis and a strong customer focus, we create innovative energy supply solutions and tailored risk management concepts for industrial clients.
We also ensure the commercial optimisation of RWE’s power plant portfolio and market the electricity generated by RWE. In addition, legally independent RWE gas storage companies operate in Germany, contributing to a secure energy supply.
