Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field. You're drawn to the frontiers of human-AI interaction and want to work on real research problems.
 Solid Python skills, including Jupyter Notebooks, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib
 Practical experience with LLM APIs and modern AI/ML frameworks
 A foundational understanding of speech-to-text, text-to-speech, or real-time speech systems
 Genuine curiosity about how people interact with AI and what makes those experiences work well for them
 A collaborative, detail-oriented approach to research and experimentation
 Fluency in English is required for this role.
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems - whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools - including agentic AI - to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work at the intersection of applied AI research and real-world product development - contributing to projects that shape next-generation enterprise software.
 Build and test voice-enabled AI applications and real-time conversational systems
 Develop dynamically generated user interfaces for AI-driven interactions
 Generate, collect, and curate multimodal datasets and evaluation scenarios
 Design and run experiments to benchmark speech models and interaction architectures
 Analyse results and document findings with academic and industry researchers
 Contribute to a joint research initiative between SAP and the Technical University of Munich (TUM)
 Where you belong
 SAP Research & Innovation partners with leading universities to explore technologies before they reach the market. This role sits within a joint initiative with the Technical University of Munich (TUM), focused on human-AI alignment in multimodal interactions. Day-to-day work centres on experimentation, prototype-building, and systematic evaluation. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen - a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.
