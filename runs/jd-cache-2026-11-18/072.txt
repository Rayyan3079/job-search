 About the Role! 
 FOMO is looking for a Data Analyst to own the reporting data layer behind its payments and cross-border business. Working with Payments, Cross-border, Finance, Compliance and BD, the role turns broad questions such as “how are corridors performing?” into precise, testable metric definitions, and builds the Kimball-style data marts, specs and business glossary behind them. It also validates each mart against source and finance figures, prototypes the first Holistic dashboards, and owns data quality and metric consistency, starting with the Finance Dashboard for Product and Finance.
 Key Responsibilities: 
 Capture reporting needs from business owners and convert them into metric definitions, dimensions, filters and refresh requirements.
 Map each need to its source systems: ledgers, merchant onboarding, KYC/KYT, settlement and CRM.
 Write data mart specs and star-schema models, including facts, dimensions, keys and SCD handling.
 Write source-to-target mappings with column-level logic and sample SQL, plus ERDs and lineage diagrams kept alongside the dbt models.
 Maintain the business glossary (GMV, net revenue, take rate, FX margin, active merchants, settlement TAT) and keep metrics consistent across reports.
 Profile source data in SQL, write acceptance and reconciliation tests (mart vs. ledger and finance reports), and run UAT and sign-off before release.
 Prototype first dashboards and datasets in Holistics.
 Flag data quality issues to source owners and track them to resolution.
 Enable self-service through documentation, training and office hours.
 Requirements: 
 Bachelor’s degree in a relevant or equivalent field.
 3–5 years of experience in data analysis, BI or analytics engineering, including at least one role that involved designing reporting or data mart models.
 Strong SQL skills: window functions, CTEs, complex joins and aggregation at mixed grains. You are comfortable profiling large tables.
 Solid grasp of dimensional modelling (Kimball): facts vs. dimensions, grain, conformed dimensions, SCD Type 1/2, and snapshot vs. transaction facts.
 A track record of writing clear specs, such as mapping documents, metric definitions and data dictionaries, that engineers could build from without rework.
 Able to work with business stakeholders, push back on ambiguous requests, and turn them into testable definitions.
 Experience with at least one BI tool (Holistics, Looker, Tableau, Power BI or Metabase).
 Fluent written and spoken English and Mandarin.
 Experience in payments, fintech or cross-border businesses.
 Hands-on experience with dbt and version-controlled documentation.
