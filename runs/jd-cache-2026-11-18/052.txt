About the role: 
 As an Intern/ Working Student in our Project Management Office within the Product Management Team, you will help shape the processes and standards that enable effective cross-functional collaboration throughout the product lifecycle.
 You will work closely with Product Management and stakeholders from all departments. By taking ownership of defined work packages, you will help establish scalable ways of working and continuously improve how our teams plan, coordinate and execute product-related activities.
 What is your Day to Day Mission: 
 Analyze and document existing cross-functional processes and identify opportunities for improvement.
 Develop and maintain project management standards, templates, guidelines and best practices.
 Support the introduction and continuous improvement of standardized processes across different teams and departments.
 Coordinate with relevant stakeholders and assist in preparing and facilitating workshops and cross-functional meetings.
 Research project management methods and industry best practices and translate them into pragmatic solutions for our organization.
 Contribute your own ideas to improve transparency, collaboration and efficiency across the product organization.
 What you bring to the team: 
 You are pursuing a degree/apprenticeship in the field of engineering or economics. 
 You are able to understand complex topics and translate them into clear processes, standards and documentation.
 You are highly self-motivated and drive your area of responsibility independently. 
 You are perfectly comfortable communicating in English. 
 Your open and confident communication style enables you to collaborate effectively with different teams and stakeholders.
