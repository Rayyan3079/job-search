 At the DLR Institute for AI Security, we research and develop AI-related methods, processes, algorithms, technologies and execution and system environments. Our focus is on secure and standard-compliant AI, cybersecurity in open data and service ecosystems and AI, as well as automation in mobility and logistics.
 What To Expect
 In the Execution Environments and Innovative Methods department, we bring together novel AI methods and challenging execution environments. In particular, we focus on the energy efficient implementation of AI for mobile systems, quantum AI and neuromorphic AI approaches. The department thus provides innovative AI approaches for use in safety-critical applications.
 Your tasks
 Supporting our research work on a pre-defined core topic
 Depending on your background, working independently on literature reviews, the development of software, methods, demonstrators or concepts under the guidance of a specialist member of staff
 Testing the developed methods on use cases
 Your profile
 Currently enrolled in a university degree programme (Bachelor’s / Master’s / University Diploma) in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics or other relevant fields
 Initial experience in one or more of the following areas: machine learning methods, robotics, embedded systems, automation, neuromorphic computing, quantum computing, cryptography, cyber security
 Ability to work as part of a team and experience of collaborative work in academia and/or software development
 Good communication skills, including in English (spoken and written)
 Proficiency in at least one relevant programming language (e.g. Python and/or C/C++)
