 About Your Program 
 You’ll be joining the JD Young (JDY) Internship Program, designed to give early-career talent hands-on exposure to real projects, real responsibilities, and real growth. Over 3–6 months, you’ll work alongside experienced teams, build fundamental skills, and gain insights into how a global business operates.
 What You'll Do
 Data analysis for user growth: Analyse marketing performance across our EU businesses. Find what drives the numbers and turn it into clear recommendations for marketing and operations teams. 
 AI agents and automation: Help build and test AI agents and automated workflows that produce reports, run analyses and support everyday decisions. Make repetitive data work faster and more reliable. 
 Reporting and insights: Prepare and maintain weekly reports and dashboards for marketing leaders, and help present the findings in short, decision-ready formats. 
 What we are looking for
 Currently enrolled in or recently graduated from a degree in data science, computer science, statistics, business analytics or a related field Studies, projects or a strong personal interest in AI (LLMs, AI agents, automation) 
 Analytical mindset: curious, careful with numbers, able to explain findings simply 
 Fluent English; good reading skills in Chinese are a strong plus Interest in e-commerce, marketing or user growth is a plus
