Our goal is to have a solar system on every roof, a storage unit in every house, and an electric car in every garage. Enpal makes this possible with an integrated total solution for decentralized energy—from solar systems and battery storage to wall boxes, smart meters, and heat pumps. At the heart of it all is our AI-powered platform Enpal.One+, which intelligently connects thousands of systems and efficiently optimizes electricity procurement and feed-in on the energy market.
Omniwatt Energy is Enpal’s electricity division, making clean energy accessible to every household—from Germany’s most affordable electricity rates to smart energy solutions that optimally integrate generation, storage, and consumption.
With ambitious growth targets and a rapidly expanding customer base, we are building a team that is actively shaping the German energy market. In doing so, we combine startup agility with the scale of a market leader—across all areas, from operations and technology to finance, customer support, and product development.
 What you’ll do
 You build AI solutions that streamline our customer ticket inflow, from automated resolution to smart routing and agent assistance
 You work with Customer Service and other teams to identify high-impact use cases and translate them into working solutions
 You take ownership end-to-end, from scoping the business case and aligning with stakeholders to hands-on implementation and measuring impact
 You use a modern agentic stack to iterate rapidly, going from first prototype to deployment in a few days
 What you’ll bring
 You are enrolled in or have completed a technical university program such as engineering, mathematics, computer science, or related
 You think in business impact and want ownership of the full problem, not just the technical part
 You have hands-on experience building and evaluating agentic tools; experience with classical ML and statistical modeling is also valuable
 You understand the fundamentals behind modern AI (e.g. attention mechanism) and can weigh technical trade-offs
 You think in systems and understand how components like APIs and databases fit together; you can reason about code, perfect syntax is not required
 You communicate clearly with non-technical colleagues and enjoy working in a business-driven team
 Are you interested even if you don't meet all the requirements? Apply anyway! We look forward to discovering your potential—regardless of whether your experience meets every single requirement. ☀️
