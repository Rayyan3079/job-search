 About The Team
 Revenue Engineering sits within Data & Insights Operations and exists to close the gap between our Data Production & Insights teams and our Revenue teams. We do this in two ways: by bringing product expertise directly into client conversations (for example through strategic pitch support), and by bringing our data and insights teams closer to the customer through programs like Voice of Customer (VoC), where we analyze signals such as customer feedback forms and support tickets submitted by our CSMs. Alongside both, we run a tool that automates our competitor research and content, and the team keeps that tool sharp and its outputs trustworthy.
As a Working Student on this team, you'll get a front-row seat to how a data company turns customer signal and market knowledge into commercial impact, working across all three areas rather than being boxed into just one.
 YOUR JOB
 As Working Student in Revenue Engineering, you help turn customer signals and market knowledge into commercial impact and keep our Data & Insights teams closely connected to our customers.
 Analyze customer feedback forms and support tickets from our Customer Success Managers (CSMs) to find recurring themes, then turn them into structured summaries our Data Production and Insights teams can act on
 Maintain and improve how we track and report on Voice of Customer signals over time
 Support strategic Salesforce case requests by researching market and product angles and turning our data capabilities into clear talking points and visuals for clients
 Review pitch materials for quality before they go to our Sales and Account teams
 Keep our automated competitor research tool accurate by fact-checking its outputs against internal standards, flagging gaps and covering missing topics through targeted manual research
 Build reports, presentations and data visualizations in PowerPoint and Excel, and support data cleaning and quality checks across team deliverables
 Your Skills
 Enrolled student in business administration, economics, data science, communications, social sciences or a related field
 Genuine interest in how customer insights, market research and data drive business decisions
 Confident with Microsoft Office, especially Excel and PowerPoint; experience with Think-Cell is a plus
 Strong written and spoken English; other languages are a plus
 Curious and detail-oriented, able to work independently and reliably, and a team player who enjoys both analytical and client-facing tasks
 Available 15 to 20 hours per week, with a flexible schedule that fits around your studies
