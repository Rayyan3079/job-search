Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Business, Economics, Business Informatics, or Engineering - and you're ready to bring analytical thinking into a global, strategy-focused environment.
 A strong analytical mindset and the ability to break complex problems into clear, structured recommendations
 Advanced proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel, with hands-on experience using AI tools such as MS Copilot, Perplexity, or Claude
 A proactive, self-directed working style — you take ownership and deliver reliably, even under pressure
 Customer orientation and the ability to collaborate effectively in a diverse, international team
 Genuine curiosity and a learning mindset, with the openness to challenge assumptions and explore new approaches
 Fluency in English is required for this role; German is a plus.
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems - whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools - including agentic AI - to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll contribute to live strategic projects alongside a high-performing team of former top-tier consultants and business experts - helping shape SAP's global go-to-market direction.
 Support strategy formulation end-to-end - from identifying pain points to developing actionable recommendations
 Design and conduct analyses that translate complex information into clear, decision-ready insights
 Synthesise findings into structured outputs for senior stakeholders across the organisation
 Work across multiple projects simultaneously, managing your priorities independently and reliably
 Collaborate with stakeholders from across SAP's global organisation to drive meaningful results
 Where you belong
 The Go-To-Market Strategy To Execution team is a high-performing group with backgrounds in management consulting and business transformation. You'll work with global leaders on initiatives that shape SAP's go-to-market success. The work is substantive and your contributions matter from day one. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen - a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.
