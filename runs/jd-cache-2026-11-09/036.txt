Billennium is a global technology company with over 20 years of experience, committed to innovation and empowering businesses. As an employer, we offer a supportive, growth-focused environment where collaboration and creativity thrive. Join us to shape the future of technology together!
 For our Partner, we are looking for an AI Trainer & Enablement Specialist . We are looking for an experienced professional who combines hands-on AI expertise with strong training and enablement skills. The role focuses on helping employees effectively and responsibly use modern AI technologies, build AI agents and automations, and identify practical AI use cases that deliver real business value.
 What you will do: 
 Design and deliver practical AI training sessions, workshops, and coaching tailored to different target groups and levels of experience.
 Enable employees to build and use AI agents, agentic workflows, and AI-powered automations using tools such as Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Studio, Copilot Cowork, and Claude Code.
 Identify, assess, and prioritize AI use cases, supporting their prototyping and implementation in real business scenarios.
 Support AI Cost Control initiatives, including optimization of AI usage, licensing models, token consumption, and resource requirements.
 Drive AI adoption and empowerment through knowledge sharing, best practices, reusable training materials, and responsible AI usage.
 What we're looking for: 
 Proven experience in designing and delivering AI training, workshops, or enablement initiatives for technical and/or business users.
 Strong hands-on knowledge of Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Studio, Copilot Cowork, and Claude Code, particularly in building AI agents and automations.
 Practical experience in developing and prototyping AI solutions, AI agents, and agentic workflows.
 Understanding of AI governance, data protection, information security, AI cost control, and license management in an enterprise environment.
 Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts clearly to audiences with different levels of AI expertise.
