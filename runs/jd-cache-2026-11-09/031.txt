Berlin, Germany, hybrid
As a Data Analyst, you will support business performance across multiple countries by delivering high-quality reports, deep-dive analyses, and actionable insights. You’ll work closely with stakeholders from marketing, product, customer care, finance, as well as management, acting as a trusted partner in translating data into business impact.
 Your Responsibilities
 Design, build, and maintain dashboards and reports for business stakeholders
 Perform in-depth analyses and ad-hoc deep dives to answer complex business questions
 Define and track KPIs, ensuring consistent and meaningful performance measurement
 Translate data into clear insights and recommendations for decision-making
 Manage stakeholder requirements and prioritize analytical work accordingly
 Present results in a clear, structured, and business-friendly way
 Lead data projects independently from concept to deliver
 Your profile
 5+ years of experience in data analytics (marketing or business context is a plus)
 Strong SQL skills and experience working with large datasets
 Proficiency in data visualization tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau) 
 Proficiency with Microsoft Excel 
 Experience with tools such as Databricks and BigQuery 
 Ability to independently structure analyses and drive projects end-to-end
 Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
 Fluency in English
