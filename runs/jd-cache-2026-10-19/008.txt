Working Student – Business Analytics & Intelligent Automation 
 Global Technical Service | Bausch + Lomb
 On-site / work from office | 20 hours per week | Start Date: ASAP | Long-term working student opportunity (minimum 6 months) 
 About The Role
 Global Technical Service is building a more automated, proactive, and AI-supported analytics function — moving beyond static Power BI dashboards toward systems that detect problems on their own, notify the right people, use AI to investigate causes, and trigger the right action, with human oversight where it matters.
As our Working Student, you'll work on-site, 20 hours per week. We're looking for someone who thinks beyond the dashboard and brings real AI expertise: open-minded enough to explore new tools and approaches, and skilled enough to actually build and implement automation that works.
 What you'll do 
 Build and improve Power BI reports, Power Query transformations, and DAX measures using Salesforce/ServiceMax data 
 Design and build real Power Automate workflows that turn data conditions into automated notifications and actions 
 Apply AI (Claude, Claude Code, and similar tools) to analyse data, detect anomalies, and prototype AI-based solutions to real business problems 
 Spot manual, repetitive processes and help design and build the automation that replaces them 
 What you bring 
 A specialised, hands-on background in AI and analytics — we need someone who can genuinely aid our automation work, not someone learning AI from scratch 
 Basic Power BI / data-analysis skills, or clear evidence you pick up new tools fast 
 A builder's instinct: able to apply AI to real automation problems, not just talk about it, and more interested in testing something real than writing a long proposal 
 Comfortable with real, messy operational data and basic data privacy awareness 
 Clear communicator in business-fluent English 
 Able to work on-site, 20 hours per week — this role is not offered remote or hybrid 
 What you'll gain 
 Hands-on ownership of automations and analytics used by Technical Service leadership 
 Direct experience applying AI to real analytics and automation problems, with mentorship from a Business Analyst working across BI, automation, and applied AI 
 A portfolio of real prototypes you helped design, build, and ship 
 Competitive pay and attractive employee benefits, including a fitness allowance 
 Convenient on-site massage services 
 Subsidized, high-quality lunch options through FreshTaste 
 Access to a wide range of employee discounts via our Corporate Benefits platform 
 Interested? We're looking for someone with more curiosity than credentials, and more instinct to build than to just propose. If that's you, we'd like to hear from you.
