Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Industrial Engineering, Business Informatics, Business Administration, or a related field. You're drawn to the point where financial analysis meets strategic decisions.
 A solid grasp of financial modeling: business cases, investment calculations, or Total Cost of Ownership analysis
 Confidence with numbers - identifying patterns and translating complexity into clear recommendations
 The ability to build presentations that communicate complex ideas simply to senior audiences
 A customer-oriented approach: you consider the audience and the decision at hand in every deliverable
 Hands-on experience with AI tools, especially AI-assisted development using Claude Code or comparable coding assistants, is a plus
 Fluency in both German and English (both C1+), written and spoken, is required for this role
 This role is based at SAP Deutschland SE & Co. KG, Hasso-Plattner-Ring 7, 69190 Walldorf, with regular on-site presence expected.
We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems - whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools - including agentic AI - to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll produce real deliverables that reach decision-makers directly - not internal practice runs.
 Build business cases and Total Cost of Ownership analyses for customers evaluating technology investments
 Develop presentations for C-level audiences that turn data and analysis into actionable narratives
 Participate in internal workshops and live customer engagements
 Collaborate with sales teams and SAP partners on value-selling practices, tools, and the SAP value story
 Develop industry-relevant enablement content to support the broader team
 Where you belong
 The Digital Value Advisory Team within SAP Corporate MEE turns technology impact into concrete numbers - business cases, TCO analyses, and value narratives for corporate customers. Your work will span independent project delivery and live customer engagements, with a direct line to boardroom decisions.
When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen - a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.
