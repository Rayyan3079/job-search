 You Are :A seasoned SAP data migration leader who thinks in blueprints and delivers at scale. You have the architectural vision to design enterprise-grade migration strategies and the hands-on depth to back it up. Whether you're defining migration frameworks for a global S/4HANA greenfield, leading complex brownfield conversions, or overseeing multi-wave data cutovers across heterogeneous landscapes, you bring the technical authority and executive presence to drive success. You are a trusted advisor to client leadership and a force multiplier for delivery teams—equally comfortable whiteboarding migration architecture and rolling up your sleeves to solve a stubborn data quality issue.
 The Work:
 Help clients modernize finance and planning by combining SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) with SAP Datasphere. 
 Design and deliver integrated planning, budgeting, forecasting, and reporting solutions that give clients a single, governed view of their business.
 Work across the full project lifecycle, from discovery and solution design through build, testing, deployment, and support. 
 Lead or help lead workstreams, manage client relationships, or run entire programs.
 Travel may be required for this role. The amount of travel will vary from 0 to 100% depending on business need and client requirements.
 Here's What you Need:
 Minimum of 3 years of hands-on experience with SAP Analytics Cloud Planning, including model design, data actions, advanced formulas, and planning workflows.
 Minimum of 1 year of experience with SAP Datasphere, including data modeling, data integration, and its role as the data foundation for SAC.
 Minimum of 2 years of experience executing planning processes through SAC, including data entry, forecasting, allocations, version management, and write-back to source systems.
 Experience with at least 2 full-cycle SAP analytics or planning implementations.
 Minimum of 2 years of experience with financial planning concepts and finance processes, including budgeting, forecasting, consolidation, and reporting.
 Minimum of 1 year of experience with SQL and data modeling principles.
 Minimum of 2 years of experience working with business and technical stakeholders, including translating business requirements into technical solutions.
 Bachelor's degree or equivalent (minimum 12 years' work experience). If Associate’s Degree, must have equivalent minimum 6-year work experience
 Bonus Points If:
 You have a SAP certification in SAP Analytics Cloud (Planning) and/or SAP Datasphere
 You have experience with SAP BW/4HANA, S/4HANA embedded analytics, or SAP BPC
 You have knowledge of SAC scripting (analytic applications), APIs, and custom widgets
 You have experience with SAP Business Data Cloud or other SAP data and AI strategy components
 You have exposure to Agile or hybrid delivery methods
 You have experience in a consulting environment, with industry experience in areas such as consumer goods, utilities, financial services, or life sciences
