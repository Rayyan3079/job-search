 About your role 
 As a Working Student, you will support our software engineering team in software development, maintenance, testing, and technical documentation. This role offers hands-on experience in modern software architecture, AI-supported software development, automation, quality assurance, and enterprise application delivery within a collaborative and innovative engineering environment.
 Supporting our modular transformation initiatives through code analysis and API/interface design
 Assisting with post-release activities, including incident analysis, ticket implementation, and test support
 Contributing to AI-powered knowledge management solutions and support automation tools
 Creating and maintaining automated tests to improve software quality and reduce regression risks
 Supporting technical modernization efforts, including library upgrades and legacy code refactoring
 Developing internal tools and AI-based utilities to automate recurring development and operational tasks
 Maintaining technical documentation, architecture artifacts, API specifications, and process flow diagrams
 About you 
 Eager to grow your knowledge and your career, you’ll fit right in. You’ll succeed because you:
 Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field; currently pursuing a Master's degree is preferred
 Solid understanding of modern software development practices, preferably with Java or other object-oriented programming languages, including Clean Code principles and API design
 Theoretical and practical experience with Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Natural Language Processing (NLP), including areas such as prompt engineering
 Basic understanding of software architecture concepts, including monolithic and microservices-based architectures as well as Domain-Driven Design
 Quality Assurance & Testing: Experience with, or willingness to learn, end-to-end testing frameworks, unit testing and performance testing tools such as JMeter and k6.
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured and self-driven approach to work
 Ability to collaborate effectively across development, testing, and support teams.
 Are comfortable to communicate effectively in German and English language (both written and oral)
