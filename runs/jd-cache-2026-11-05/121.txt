Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Marketing, Economics, or a related field and want to apply analytical thinking to real business challenges in the consumer products industry.
 Strong research skills with the ability to identify trends and translate findings into clear insights
 Confidence in analysing and interpreting data to support planning and decision-making
 A customer-oriented mindset and a collaborative approach to working across teams
 Comfort with preparing and presenting structured reports and recommendations to stakeholders
 A proactive approach to problem-solving with the ability to manage priorities independently
 Fluency in English is required for this role.
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems — whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools — including agentic AI — to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll contribute to SAP's global Consumer Products strategy by turning research and data into insights that drive business plans, client engagement, and revenue growth.
 Conduct market research to identify key trends, opportunities, and competitive dynamics
 Collect and analyse data from multiple sources to deliver insights that support strategic planning
 Contribute to the development and updating of business plans with data-driven recommendations
 Prepare concise reports and presentations with recommendations that influence revenue growth strategies
 Research and prepare content for customer-facing meetings and engagement activities
 Track priority action items across the team and support day-to-day business operations
 Where you belong
 SIX CI - Consumer Products is SAP's global industry unit focused on making SAP the trusted technology partner for the consumer goods sector. You'll work alongside industry experts and consultants who drive strategy, client engagement, and business development across global markets. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen — a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.
 Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages. 
 This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
