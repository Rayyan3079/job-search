 Job Description
 Deep analysis. Smarter systems. Real users. As a Data Engineering Intern, you'll help build the pipelines and data models that give People & Culture reliable data to make better decisions about how we hire, develop and support our teams. You'll own one workstream at a time, learn from experienced data engineers and data scientists, and see your work used by real stakeholders.
People Data Engineering and Science is the team that turns People & Culture data into trusted, usable insight. Data engineers and data scientists work side by side to build the data foundations behind People analytics, from ingestion and modelling to quality and reporting. You'll join this team and learn how production data work is done at scale.
Join the team that champions the employee experience at Delivery Hero. As part of the People & Culture Team, you'll help attract, develop, and retain top talent, creating a workplace where people feel valued, supported, and empowered to grow. With each initiative you support, you'll be building a positive culture that fuels innovation, teamwork, and a sense of belonging across our global organization.
 Build and maintain data pipelines that bring People & Culture data from source systems into our data platform. 
 Write SQL and Python transformations that turn raw data into clean, documented, analytics-ready datasets. 
 Add data quality checks and monitoring so stakeholders can trust the numbers they use. 
 Support data scientists by preparing and validating the datasets behind their analyses and models. 
 Apply data privacy and access standards in all your work, working only with anonymised or approved data. 
 Document your work and share progress and learnings with the team in regular reviews. 
 Qualifications
 Enrolled in, or recently graduated from, a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, Data Science or a related field. 
 Working knowledge of SQL and Python, from coursework, personal projects or previous experience. 
 Understanding of data basics such as relational databases, data modelling and ETL/ELT concepts. 
 Curiosity and care about data accuracy: you check your work and ask when something looks off. 
 Ability to pick up an unfamiliar problem and work out how to move it forward, asking for help when needed. 
 Clear written and spoken English, so you can explain your work to technical and non-technical colleagues. 
 Nice to have:
 Experience with a cloud platform (GCP, AWS or Azure) or a data warehouse such as BigQuery or Snowflake. 
 Familiarity with workflow orchestration tools such as Airflow, or with dbt. 
 Experience with Git and version control in team projects. 
 Interest in People analytics or HR data.
