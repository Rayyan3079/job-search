Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Computer Science or a related field and want to apply AI and automation tools to real business challenges. You're ready to explore what's possible - and leave with work you can build on. You have a genuine passion for solving business challenges through AI and automation.
 Current enrolment at a university or university of applied sciences, ideally in Computer Science or Informatics
 Familiarity with tools such as Claude, Power Automate, or Microsoft Copilot - or a strong drive to learn them
 A proactive approach to designing, building, and refining prototypes independently
 A customer-oriented mindset - you consider what colleagues and stakeholders actually need
 Fluency in German and English is required for this role (written and spoken)
 Working from Walldorf / St. Leon-Rot office is required
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems - whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools - including agentic AI - to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work on real deliverables, not shadow tasks. Your contributions connect directly to the team's AI and automation goals.
 Research, design, and validate automation solutions for the SCI team
 Apply AI tools to practical business scenarios and document outcomes
 Develop prototypes, gather user feedback, and refine use cases iteratively
 Collaborate with colleagues to map processes, gather requirements, and act on feedback
 Align regularly with the SCI AI Lead on use cases, legal considerations, and tools
 Prepare and deliver enablement sessions for team members
 Where you belong
 The Strategic Customer Innovation (SCI) unit works with strategic customers on complex, forward-looking challenges and acts as the voice of the customer for Cloud ERP. We sit within SAP's development organisation. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen - a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers. This is where you start something real.
Additional Information
Please apply initially with your resume in table form. In a later stage we will ask you to upload also school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships).
This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
