Germany / Bayreuth
CYBEX is a company that puts tomorrow’s people at the heart of its brand. Always questioning the status quo and not afraid of challenging the establishment, CYBEX has become not just a leader in child safety but is seen as an innovative lifestyle and fashion brand for parents. We offer car seats, baby carriers, kids furniture and strollers enabling parents to keep their little ones safe in style. After growing rapidly in the last few years, we look for ambitious talent to contribute to CYBEX’s success story.
 Your seat to fill: 
 Support product testing by developing test methods, conducting product tests, and evaluating the results. 
 Contribute to product risk assessments by reviewing products and identifying potential risks from a consumer perspective. 
 Assist the Quality Lead in following up on quality-related tasks within projects, including administrative support. 
 Analyze quality deliverables to identify potential bottlenecks within projects, for example by reviewing resources when project timelines change. 
 Create and maintain master layouts in Excel. 
 Support the creation, adjustment, and execution of quality project reports. 
 Review and maintain quality databases to ensure information is accurate and up to date. 
 Contribute to the development and documentation of quality processes. 
 Your stroll to CYBEX: 
 You are currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Business Administration, or a comparable field. 
 Advanced Microsoft Office skills, particularly in Excel. 
 Experience with Outlook and SharePoint is a plus. 
 Good written and spoken English skills. 
 A detail-oriented and independent working style, combined with motivation and a hands-on mentality. 
 A Class B driving license is a plus. 
 An interest in technical topics and a good technical understanding would be an advantage. 
 Willingness to occasionally travel to our warehouse near Worms for short business trips of 2–4 days. 
 This job is at our office in Bayreuth.
