Our talented Data Engineering team is made up of globally recognized experts - and there’s room for more analytical and ambitious data professionals. If you’re passionate about helping clients make better data-driven decisions to tackle their most complex business issues, let’s talk. Take your skills to a new level and launch a career where you can truly do what matters.
 Come Join Us
 As a member of the Data Engineering team, you’ll have access to the research, knowledge, and tools to create leading-edge solutions across Avanade’s Business Intelligence practice. The role of Data Engineer is perfect for ambitious technologists passionate about working with the latest Microsoft cloud technology and Microsoft experts. Our clients look to us for innovation, which means you’ll have early access to the newest Microsoft technologies so you can master them and stay ahead of the curve.
 Key Responsibilities
 Together we do what matters. 
 Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines and ETL/ELT processes.
 Build modern data solutions using Databricks and cloud technologies.
 Develop and optimize data transformations using Python, PySpark and SQL.
 Work with Delta Lake and lakehouse architectures to process large volumes of structured and unstructured data.
 Implement data ingestion, transformation, integration, and quality processes.
 Collaborate with Data Architects, Data Scientists, BI specialists, and business stakeholders.
 Apply best practices around data quality, performance, governance, security, and maintainability.
 Participate in international projects, communicating with clients and colleagues in English.
 Qualifications
 What we’re looking for
 Professional experience working as a Data Engineer or in a similar role.
 Hands-on experience with Databricks in production environments.
 Strong knowledge of Python / PySpark and SQL.
 Experience designing and implementing ETL/ELT pipelines.
 Knowledge of Delta Lake, Lakehouse architectures, and data modelling.
 Experience with cloud data platforms, ideally Microsoft Azure.
 Familiarity with technologies such as Azure Data Factory, Azure Data Lake Storage, Synapse Analytics or Microsoft Fabric is a plus.
 Understanding of CI/CD, Git and DevOps practices applied to data solutions.
 Strong English communication skills, comfortable working in international environments and participating in technical conversations with clients.
 Nice to have
 Databricks certifications, such as Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate or Professional.
 Microsoft Azure data certifications. 
 Experience with Unity Catalog and data governance.
 Knowledge of streaming technologies and real-time data processing.
 Previous experience in consulting or client-facing environments
