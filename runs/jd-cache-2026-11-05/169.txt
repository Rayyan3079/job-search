Lloyds Bank GmbH and its brands Bank of Scotland and Lloyds Bank have won over 1 million satisfied customers in recent years. And this thanks to convincing products and, above all, our committed colleagues who want to achieve the best for our customers every day with new ideas, challenges and joy. In order to continue writing our success story, we are looking for the right reinforcement for our teams, because all our ambitious goals would be unattainable without teamwork. We also welcome career changers.
 For our office in Berlin Mitte we are looking for a  
 Working Student - Sourcing, Data & Governance (m/f/d)    
 Your tasks
 Clean, validate and maintain supplier, contract and sourcing data across relevant systems and trackers
 Identify missing, duplicate, outdated or inconsistent data and support the correction and completion of records
 Perform regular data quality checks and reconcile information across different data sources
 Support the maintenance of supplier information, including contract details, service information, classifications, subcontractors and responsible stakeholders
 Support the preparation and maintenance of Excel-based reporting, dashboards and management information
 Follow up with internal stakeholders to obtain missing information and ensure records are complete and up to date
 Support the maintenance of sourcing and supplier governance information in JIRA, Confluence and other relevant systems
 Assist with documenting and improving data management processes, controls and working procedures
  Support periodic reviews, sourcing activities and remediation initiatives within the team
 Your knowledge/experience
 Bachelor’s or Master’s studies within Business Administration, Economics, Business Informatics, Data/Business Analytics, Information Management or a similar field
 Strong analytical skills and a structured, detail-oriented way of working
 Good knowledge of Microsoft Excel and confidence working with larger datasets; knowledge of Power BI or Power Query is a plus
 Knowledge or interest in indirect procurement, sourcing, supplier management, data governance or financial services is a plus
 Advanced English language skills; German and/or Dutch language capability is a plus
 Comfortable working with Microsoft applications, particularly Excel and PowerPoint, as well as JIRA and Confluence
  Ability to work independently, identify data inconsistencies and proactively follow up on missing or unclear information

 An international team with colleagues from more than 50 different countries
