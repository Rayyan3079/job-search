Location: Remote, right to work and travel in Europe.
Albatross builds perception models for real-time product discovery on marketplaces, adapting to user intent in-session and creating inspiring shopping experiences by surfacing new and relevant products and sellers. Today, Albatross powers the online experience of 50+ million shoppers monthly and processes 3+ billion user interactions per month across 150+ million products. Winner of the Tech for Retail 2025 Award, Albatross is based in Switzerland and backed by Redalpine, MMC Ventures, and Daphni.
 The Role
 We're looking for a Deployment Strategist who loves digging into data and turning it into results customers can see. You'll own the deployment from the first data drop to launch day, validating data, building the dashboards that tell the story, and chasing down anything that looks off. After a structured onboarding, proper training and support, you'll be ready to:
 Own the customer relationship end-to-end during deployment: timeline, expectations, data, analyses, and reporting
 Gather and validate customer data, chasing stakeholders as needed to get it in on time and in the right format
 Review automated data-quality checks and flag anomalies before they become problems
 Run and report on data-adequacy analyses to confirm a customer's data is ready for deployment
 Manage the deployment pipeline end-to-end (workflows, early training, go-live)
 Build and maintain dashboards tracking deployment health and A/B test results
 Support the commercial team with data and analysis during customer negotiations
 Requirements
 Degree in a quantitative field (Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Statistics, or similar)
 1-3 years of hands-on experience working with real data (e.g. data analyst, BI analyst, data engineer, junior data scientist, or a similarly data-heavy role)
 Strong analytics skills, including handling real-world datasets
 Experience building and reading dashboards
 Comfortable learning fast and growing technical judgment
 Strong client-facing communication skills in English with the ability to explain technical concepts clearly to both engineers and non-technical stakeholders
 Good judgment on when to escalate vs. resolve independently
 Eligible to work in Europe
 Benefits
 Full remote flexibility across Europe
 Yearly gross salary range € 50k - 70k
