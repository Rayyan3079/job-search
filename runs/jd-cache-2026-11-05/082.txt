Make data clear and actionable for teams across Basic-Fit 
 You don’t want to just build dashboards.
You want your reports to be used, trusted, and understood.
You want to see decisions being made because of what you built.
At Basic-Fit, teams across the organisation rely on data to serve millions of members across Europe. The reports you build and own will be used tomorrow by people who need answers today.
 This Is What Your Role Looks Like
 You’ll join the central Data Platform Team as the Data Analyst for our central, cross-functional reports and for teams that don’t have an analyst of their own. You’ll work directly with the business, from the first question to a clear, finished report that people actually use. Over time, you’ll help teams take ownership of their own reports.
 In a Typical Week, You Might
 own and maintain central reports that are not tied to a single team 
 build and maintain reporting for teams without a dedicated analyst 
 investigate data questions and ad-hoc requests from across the business 
 tell the story behind the numbers and present insights in a way that supports better decisions 
 apply data visualisation best practices and standards to keep reports clear and consistent 
 work with Data Platform Engineers to get the right data in place in Databricks for your reports 
 improve existing reports on clarity, performance and usability 
 automate recurring reports and enable self-service 
 use AI to speed up your analysis and reporting work 
 Good ideas don’t stay in slide decks here - they get built.
 The team you’ll work with 
 You’ll be part of a multidisciplinary Data Platform Team with:
 Data Platform Engineers 
 Analysts 
 BI Developers 
 Data Scientists 
 Together, you support almost every department at Basic-Fit, enabling teams to act on reliable, well-governed data.
 Who Are You?
 You’re Curious, Structured, And Good At Understanding What Stakeholders Really Need. You Can Turn Complex Data Into a Clear Story. You Don’t Need To Tick Every Box. You Will Thrive Here If Most Of This Sounds Familiar
 3+ years of experience as a Data Analyst or BI Analyst 
 Hands-on experience with Power BI, including building reports, DAX and data modelling basics 
 Strong SQL skills to explore, validate and understand data 
 Strong data visualisation skills and a good feel for design standards: you turn complex data into clear, consistent, actionable dashboards that follow best practices 
 Experience working directly with business stakeholders and translating their needs into reports 
 Flexibility to quickly get up to speed in different business areas, as you’ll support a variety of teams 
 A mindset of enabling others: you like to document, automate and hand over 
 Attention to detail and data quality: you validate your numbers and spot inconsistencies 
 Excellent communication skills in English 
 Experience with Databricks, Python or Fabric is a plus 
 Experience using AI tools to speed up analysis and reporting is a plus
