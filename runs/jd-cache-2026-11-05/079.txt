Job Summary 
 The Business Intelligence Analyst is responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining critical data pipelines, dashboards, and analytical solutions that support business operations across Sales, Operations, Finance, and Management teams. This role requires expertise in SQL-based data extraction and transformation, with additional capabilities in dashboard development using Power BI to deliver actionable insights to stakeholders.
 Key Responsibilities 
 Data Pipeline Development & Management
 Design, develop, and maintain complex SQL-based data extraction scripts and ETL pipelines to support daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly business reporting requirements
 Create and manage data pipelines for key performance indicators (KPIs) across Sales, Retail Operations, Postpaid services, and Network domains
 Develop and maintain datasets supporting operational dashboards, management reports, and strategic analysis including Master Trackers, Closing reports, and Territory Hierarchy updates
 Ensure data quality, accuracy, and timely delivery of all data feeds meeting T+1 and end-of-month SLA requirements
 Optimize SQL queries for performance and efficiency in handling large-scale data processing
 Dashboard & Visualization Development
 Design and develop interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI to visualize key business metrics for various stakeholders
 Create and maintain dashboards for Postpaid, Retail, Prepaid, FTTH (Home Broadband), Trade operations, and Network KPIs
 Develop specialized reporting solutions including Champion Club tracking, DSE (Direct Sales Executive) performance, Partner Cost & P&L, and Sales incentive dashboards
 Collaborate with business users to understand requirements and translate them into effective visual analytics solutions
 Data Analysis & Support
 Respond to ad-hoc data requests from Sales, Operations, Finance, and Management teams with accurate and timely analysis
 Perform data validation, reconciliation, and quality assurance to ensure data integrity across all reporting systems
 Support business initiatives including churn forecasting, RGU (Revenue Generating Unit) analysis, customer segmentation, and A2P (Application-to-Person) traffic monitoring
 Provide technical support and documentation for data-related processes and systems
 Stakeholder Collaboration & Communication
 Work closely with Circle teams, Sales teams (SPO), Operations, Finance, Network, and Management to understand data requirements and deliver solutions
 Participate in requirements gathering sessions and provide technical expertise on data capabilities and limitations
 Document technical specifications, data lineage, and business logic for all developed solutions
 Train end-users on dashboard functionality and data interpretation when required
 Required Qualifications
 Education
 Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or related field
 Experience
 Minimum 2-3 years of experience in business intelligence, data analytics, or related role
 Proven experience in developing and maintaining data pipelines in a production environment
 Experience working with large datasets and complex data structures
 Experience in telecommunications or similar industry is preferred
 Technical Skills
 Required (Must Have)
 Advanced SQL proficiency: Expert-level ability to write complex SQL queries including joins, subqueries, window functions, CTEs (Common Table Expressions), and query optimization
 Database knowledge: Strong understanding of relational database concepts, data modeling, and database performance tuning
 Data transformation: Experience with ETL processes and data pipeline development
 Data analysis: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail
 Preferred (Highly Valued)
 Power BI: Proficiency in creating interactive dashboards, reports, and data visualizations using Microsoft Power BI
 DAX: Knowledge of DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for creating calculated columns and measures
 Experience with other BI tools such as Tableau, Qlik, or similar platforms
 Knowledge of Python or R for data analysis and automation
 Experience with cloud data platforms (e.g., Google BigQuery, AWS, Azure)
 Version control systems (Git) experience
 Key Competencies
 Technical Excellence: Demonstrated expertise in SQL and data manipulation with a commitment to writing efficient, maintainable code
 Attention to Detail: High degree of accuracy in data analysis and reporting with strong quality assurance mindset
 Time Management: Ability to manage multiple concurrent projects and meet strict SLA deadlines (T+1, EOM)
 Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills to interact effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders
 Problem-Solving: Analytical thinking and ability to troubleshoot data-related issues independently
 Adaptability: Flexibility to handle changing priorities and ad-hoc requests while maintaining quality standards
 Collaboration: Team player who can work effectively in a cross-functional environment
 Typical Work Examples
 Examples of work responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
 Creating and managing daily data pipelines for Postpaid and Retail KPI tracking with T+1 SLA
 Developing Power BI dashboards for Sales performance monitoring across multiple channels
 Building SQL scripts for Master Tracker (SSOT - Single Source of Truth) updates
 Maintaining monthly reporting datasets for closing processes and financial reconciliation
 Supporting FTTH (Home Broadband) analytics including installation, payment, and usage tracking
 Generating automated email reports for CVM (Customer Value Management) campaigns and cohort analysis
 Responding to ad-hoc data extraction requests from various business units
 Updating territorial hierarchy and outlet mapping data for sales operations
 Creating datasets for regulatory compliance reporting (e.g., MOCI quarterly reports)
 Developing forecasting models for churn prediction and RGU movement analysis
