Rex.zone is hiring a full-time, fully remote AI Data Annotation Specialist supporting training data creation for modern AI/ML systems. You will label and review text, images, audio, and video to improve LLM evaluation , RLHF feedback quality, and computer vision annotation accuracy while maintaining strong annotation guidelines compliance and training data quality.
 Key Responsibilities
 Annotate and review text datasets for NLP tasks such as classification and named entity recognition
 Perform RLHF tasks including preference ranking and rubric-based scoring
 Conduct prompt evaluation and QA evaluation to identify edge cases, label noise, and guideline gaps
 Label computer vision data (bounding boxes, polygons, keypoints, segmentation masks) and validate accuracy
 Apply content safety labeling and policy-based moderation labels for sensitive content categories
 Document decisions, propose improvements to annotation guidelines, and support calibration sessions
 Track quality metrics (disagreement rates, audit pass rates) and contribute to training data quality initiatives
 Required Qualifications
 Mid-Senior experience in AI data annotation, data labeling, or QA evaluation/data operations
 Familiarity with LLM evaluation concepts, RLHF workflows, and rubric-based scoring
 Strong written communication and ability to justify labeling decisions with clear rationale
 High attention to detail and consistent annotation guidelines compliance
 Comfort working remotely in a metrics-driven production environment with iterative feedback
 Compensation
 Hourly base pay range: $30–$50/hr (USD).
Remote Type: Remote (Miami-based search intent; eligible across the US).
 How To Apply
 Apply via Rex.zone and be prepared to summarize your experience across NLP labeling, computer vision annotation, RLHF, prompt evaluation, and QA evaluation.
