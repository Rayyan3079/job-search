Are you interested in international project management and helping people develop healthier ways of working with digital technologies? Join us to gain practical experience in project support, communication, and training. 
 The Media and Communication Management group at TU Ilmenau is looking for a motivated student assistant (HiWi) to support the Interreg Central Europe project Digi-B-Well – Enhancement of Capacities for Digitalisation and Digital Well-being. 
 Qualifications 
 Support the implementation of project activities.
 Support the planning, organisation, and delivery of training sessions, including preparing materials and assisting participants. Depending on your interests and skills, you may also facilitate parts of the sessions.
 Draft newsletters, news updates, and summaries of project findings for different audiences.
 Contribute to project website updates and social media content as needed.
 Profile 
 Enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s programme in media and communication science or a related field.
 Interested in project management, communication, and digital well-being.
 Fluent in written and spoken German and English.
 Willing to engage with participants and help deliver training; previous training experience is welcome but not required.
 Organised, reliable, proactive, and comfortable working both independently and as part of a team.
 What we offer 
 A student assistant position with 40 working hours per month.
 Flexible working hours arranged in agreement with the project team.
 A friendly, supportive working environment with opportunities to learn and develop your skills.
 Practical experience in an international project focused on digital well-being.
