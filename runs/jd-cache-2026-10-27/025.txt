We are one of the world’s leading companies in the fields of orthopedics, arthroscopy, sports medicine, and orthobiologics. True to our mission “Helping Surgeons Treat Their Patients Better,” we have been developing innovative products and surgical techniques for over 40 years. Arthrex was founded in 1981 in Munich and is now headquartered in Naples, Florida (USA), with additional subsidiaries in 21 countries. Our corporate culture is defined by cross-border collaboration and the exchange of ideas among colleagues from diverse national backgrounds.
The expansion into new business areas and the continuous development of our product portfolio contribute to our steady growth and ensure that we remain at the forefront of high-quality product innovation. Our employees play a key role in this success through their dedication and commitment.
In return, we offer a motivating and appreciative work environment.
 Your Responsibilities
 You analyze user feedback on our globally used AI-powered product knowledge database, with a particular focus on identifying the causes of negative feedback.
 You identify and document bugs and other issues and coordinate with Product Management and the Development team to address them.
 You support the testing of new features and verify whether reported issues have been resolved through bug fixes.
 You prepare feedback, test results, and other data in a structured way to support the further development of the product.
 You support the team in day-to-day operations, for example with analyses, documentation, and presentations.
 Your Profile
 You are currently studying Business Administration, Business Information Systems, Digital Business, Biomedical Engineering, or a comparable degree program. Students from other academic backgrounds are also welcome.
 You are interested in digital products and the practical application of AI.
 You have an analytical mindset and enjoy systematically identifying the root causes of problems.
 You work carefully, reliably, and independently.
 You are fluent in English, both written and spoken.

 A wide range of internal and external training and development opportunities, including digital learning platforms
 Comprehensive health and well-being benefits, including our cooperation with EGYM Wellpass, massage services, occupational health management, and internal health initiatives
 Additional benefits such as a subsidy for the Deutschlandticket and access to Corporate Benefits
