Your role
Key responsibilities in your new role
 Lifelong learning: Acquire knowledge on plasma etching processing, AI-data readiness and development workflows
 Data is everything: Access and analyze current databases, identify data gaps and develop a cloud database for missing recipe/measurement data
 Shape the future: Develop a scalable OutSystems web application to document experiments, extending the capabilities of the existing documentation
 Take responsibility: Develop and test automated workflows and digital solutions in the context of Unit Process Development
 Focus on the future: Create and enhance internal tools, applications, and digital solutions using technologies such as C# or Python to collect, cleanse, and analyze measurement data
 Convince with words: Document your methods, implementation, benchmark results, and limitations, and present your ideas
 Your profile
 Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed
 Study field: You are a top-performing student currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program in Computer Science, Data Science, or Artificial Intelligence
 Skills:
 You have proven (2+ years) experience in programming, with practical knowledge of machine learning frameworks and data analysis tools
 Familiarity with software development, scripting, and database technologies
 Experience working with data pipelines, and data integration concepts is advantageous
 Interests: You have a strong ambition for computer systems, programming and machine learning
 Experience:
 You are experienced in programming and data analysis, with a strong foundation in statistics and mathematics
 You are capable of critically assessing your own results
 Experience in machine learning is a bonus
 Personality: Proactive, self-driven and science interested. You have very good communication skills, capable of explaining complex concepts to non-expert and can organize your work in a very structured way
 Language skills: You have fluent English skills, both written and spoken, German would be a plus
