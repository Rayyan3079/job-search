 Working Student for Software Support (m/f/d) 
 Your tasks
 As part of the project team, you support SW Support projects by contributing to the analysis of the current state and collaborating with the project team and internal stakeholders on the software support concept
 You contribute to the further development of the internal project management tool in Jira and support the development of routines for automating data extractions
 You contribute to the creation of advanced reporting based on the automated data extractions
 You participate in building the Knowledge Base by helping to document and describe processes and project status in the department's knowledge database
 You contribute to establishing periodic reporting in the sense of visual management
 Your qualifications
 You are studying Computer Engineering, Software Development, Computer Science or a comparable field
 You bring experience with data analysis
 You have very good MS Office skills (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) as well as proficient skills in development with the tools Jira and Confluence
 You ideally have initial knowledge of project management
 You are familiar with concepts of software support
 You work reliably and in a structured manner
 Very good German skills and very good English skills (written and spoken) round off your profile
