With a company culture rooted in collaboration, expertise and innovation, we aim to promote progress and inspire our clients, employees, investors and communities to achieve their greatest potential. Our work is the catalyst that helps others achieve their goals. In short, We Enable Possibility℠.
Have you ever wondered how Actuaries evaluate risk and measure performance at insurance companies? The Actuarial Operations Team at Arch is a dynamic and growing team. We are expanding our reporting capabilities to better support the actuarial department, with strong opportunities for innovation and adoption of modern technologies.
The BI Analyst partners with Actuaries to identify reporting priorities driven by actuarial models. In this role, you will leverage business intelligence and analytical expertise to develop new reports and enhance existing reporting solutions.
 Job Responsibilities
 Collaborate with actuarial teams to design and deliver actionable reporting solutions
 Develop and enhance reports using Power BI and Microsoft Power Platform tools for data analysis and visualization, and maintain comprehensive semantic models within Power BI
 Build, configure, and maintain automated testing workflows within cloud environments
 Create and maintain scalable CI/CD pipelines for reporting solutions, leveraging Git-based workflows
 Perform regular validation of report outputs in partnership with QA teams
 Produce and maintain clear documentation for internal use and UAT
 Partner with data engineering teams to improve reporting data models and architecture
 Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders; implement reporting standards and align practices with governance and compliance requirements in partnership with IT teams
 Technical Skills
 Required Skills/Experience 
 2+ years of experience developing reports in Power BI
 5+ years of experience building reports and dashboards using visualization tools (e.g., Power BI, QlikView, Tableau)
 Experience supporting analytics or actuarial functions, preferably within an insurance environment
 Ability to independently troubleshoot and resolve technical challenges
 Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving capabilities
 Commitment to continuous learning and exploration of emerging tools and technologies
 Ability to leverage data to drive business insights and decision-making
 Familiarity with SQL, Snowflake, or Python is a plus
 Familiarity with Streamlit or Sigma is a plus
 EDUCATION
 Bachelor’s degree in statistics, mathematics, information technology, computer science, business analytics, data analytics, or equivalent work experience
