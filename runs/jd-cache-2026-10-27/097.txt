 At TYTAN , we aim to set a global standard in autonomous air defense with AI-driven, cost-efficient systems that counter unmanned aerial threats at scale. We protect people. We protect critical infrastructure. We protect European sovereignty.
 We are looking for working students supporting the core of our product development: The design – development – testing – improvement loop. You will have the chance to support data collection from our interceptors at testing sites, analyze the data post flight, present the data to key engineering stakeholders and derive improvement initiatives. In addition, you will be able to support continuous improvement of our data analytics toolchain and of our test database.
 Your Roadmap to Make an Impact 
 Data analytics 
 Join our flight and ground testing teams and support data gathering in the field seeing TYTAN products in action
 Analyze the collected data points using our analytics toolchain
 Derive improvement initiatives that, e.g., support development of our onboard AI system
 Analytics toolchain 
 Get to know and use the TYTAN toolchain for running data analytics and for training and improving our AI system
 Support continuous improvement of the analytics toolchain, e.g., by developing scripts and improving visualization
 Test database 
 Support maintenance of TYTAN’s database on flight testing data
 Identify any needs for additional data collection and develop respective test points
 Job requirements 
 3rd year in Bachelor’s studies or 1st/2nd year in Master’s studies in Physics, Computer Science, or Mathematics
 Experience in using PYTHON and tools for data analytics
 Strong analytical capabilities – numbers and data-driven mindset
 Fluency in both German and English is required
