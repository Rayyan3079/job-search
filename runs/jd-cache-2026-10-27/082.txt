Job Description
 What you'll bring
 Student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences
 Preferred fields of study: Data Science, Business & Data Science, Digital Business, or a related field
 Computer skills / Programming languages: Microsoft 365 (Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint); practical experience with AI tools (e.g. Claude)
 Language skills: Fluent in German and English (written and verbal)
 Soft skills: Structured and reliable; able to work independently; strong communicator; commitment to quality and accuracy
 Other important skills/experience: Solid understanding of data analysis concepts and digital business contexts
 Working from Walldorf office is required
 What You'll Build
 We are looking for a motivated working student to support our team in data-driven decision making, governance reporting, business intelligence initiatives, and the responsible use of AI-assisted analytics. This role offers unique insights into how strategic business objectives, operational governance, and legal requirements are managed in highly-regulated and dynamic business environments.
 What You Will Do
 Support data collection, structuring, and analysis for ongoing projects 
 Build and maintain dashboards and reports using standard data tools 
 Apply AI-supported tools to increase efficiency in research and analysis tasks 
 Assist in the preparation of presentations and decision-relevant materials 
 Contribute to the documentation and quality assurance of data-driven workflows 
 Where you belong 
 The SAP GS2 Governance Office drives compliance, governance and strategic projects, knowledge management, and cross-functional coordination.
You will be part of a small, high-impact team where your technical and analytical contributions are valued. We value rigour, clarity, and the ability to turn complex problems into simple, usable outputs.
 Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages. 
 This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.
