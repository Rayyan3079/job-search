CYBEX is a company that puts tomorrow’s people at the heart of its brand.  Your seat to fill: 
 Support the Customs & Trade Compliance team in steering and further developing customs and foreign trade–related processes 
 Contribute to the tariff classification of products (HS/TARIC system) 
 Independently take ownership of and manage selected customs and foreign trade–related sub-areas 
 Prepare and analyze customs-related KPIs and create reports and status overviews 
 Support operational import activities, in particular:
 Review of import declarations 
 Documentation and archiving 
 Support projects within Customs & Trade Compliance (e.g. process optimization, digitalization, compliance requirements) 
 Monitor import transactions and duties to ensure customs compliance and accuracy 
 Coordinate with internal stakeholders and external service providers (e.g. customs brokers) 
 Support the creation and maintenance of internal work instructions and policies 
 Your stroll to CYBEX:
 You are currently studying for a bachelor's or master's degree in business administration, business engineering or comparable courses of study 
 You have a good understanding of procurement processes and are eager to learn more 
 You have very good written and spoken English skills 
 You are a team player, detail-oriented and have an affinity for numbers 
 You have very good knowledge of Microsoft Office (especially Excel)
