Role Overview
 Rex.zone is hiring a Remote AI Data Annotation Specialist (open to US-based candidates, including Detroit) to support end-to-end LLM training pipelines and model evaluation datasets. You will apply detailed rubrics and labeling guidelines to improve training data quality and model reliability.
 What You Will Do
 Label and review text, image, and multimodal datasets to meet training data quality thresholds
 Execute RLHF-style preference ranking and rubric-based evaluations of model outputs
 Perform prompt evaluation and QA evaluation (calibration, audit sampling, consistency checks)
 Annotate NLP tasks such as named entity recognition, taxonomy mapping, and document classification
 Support computer vision annotation (bounding boxes, polygons/segmentation, keypoints, attributes)
 Complete content safety labeling using policy-based decision rules
 Document edge cases, resolve disagreements, and improve annotation guidelines compliance
 Required Qualifications
 Mid-Senior experience in AI data annotation, data labeling operations, or model evaluation
 Ability to follow complex rubrics and provide consistent judgments
 Familiarity with NLP concepts (e.g., NER, classification) and comfort with CV annotation concepts
 Strong written communication, attention to detail, and reliable remote work habits
 Work Model
 Remote, FULL_TIME. Work is performed online with asynchronous coordination; periodic calibration sessions may occur.
 Compensation
 Hourly base pay range: $30–$50 per hour (may vary by project complexity and evaluation scope).
