Schaeffler is a dynamic global technology company and its success has been a result of its entrepreneurial spirit and long history of private ownership. Does that sound interesting to you? As a partner to all of the major automobile manufacturers, as well as key players in the aerospace and industrial sectors, we offer you many development opportunities.
General information
Sales Excellence transforms data into clear and actionable insights that enable smarter and more profitable decisions. We develop intelligent tools and harmonized processes that simplify daily work and boost sales performance. By leveraging deep customer insights and building strong technical partnerships, we identify new opportunities that drive sustainable and profitable growth.
A basic requirement for an internship at Schaeffler is proof of enrollment at the time of the internship. This position is available as soon as possible for a duration of 6 months.
 Your Key Responsibilities
 Support the development and implementation of innovative AI use cases within Sales Excellence
 Collect, preprocess, and normalize large datasets to serve as the foundation for machine learning models
 Maintain and enhance our Sales Power BI dashboard to improve data visualization and accessibility
 Create insightful data analyses and visualizations that drive data-informed decision making
 Assist in automating data workflows to increase efficiency across sales processes
 Prepare technical documentation, reports, and presentations for internal stakeholders
 Your Qualifications
 Studies in Data Science, (Business) Informatics, (Business) Engineering, or a comparable field of study
 Very good verbal and written skills in German as well as good skills in English
 Proficient in MS Office applications
 Experience with data visualization tools, particularly Power BI, including dashboard creation and maintenance to communicate insights effectively
 Proficiency in data preprocessing skills such as data cleaning, feature engineering, handling missing values, and normalization
 Skills in scripting or programming languages (e.g., Python, SQL, R) to automate data workflows and streamline processes
 Strong foundation in machine learning concepts, including supervised learning, classification algorithms, model evaluation, and validation techniques
 Familiarity with model deployment and automation concepts is a plus to facilitate integration into business processes
 Independent, structured, and responsible working style combined with a strong willingness to learn
 Strong teamwork and communication skills
