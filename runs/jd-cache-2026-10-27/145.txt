Job-ID: MER000492O
 Tasks
 We are part of the MB.OS (Mercedes-Benz Operating System ) Developer Journey and develop the PMT (Product Methods & Tools) Solution, the central platform that provides vehicle engineers at Mercedes-Benz with the processes, methods, and tools they need for their daily work. 
 Our goal is to replace complex development workflows with lean, tool-supported processes that make vehicle engineering more efficient. 
 We are looking for someone who understands AI (Artificial Intelligence), not just uses it.
 These challenges await You:
 Develop and integrate AI (Artificial Intelligence) components (LLM agents, RAG pipelines, API integrations) into existing platforms
 Develop software using TypeScript in modern technology stacks (NestJS, Vue 3), including testing and code reviews
 Take ownership of end-to-end feature development, from technical design through deployment
 Contribute to architecture and technology decisions as a full member of the team 
 You'll work directly in the product team, not in a sandbox — your code ships to production. 
 We expect you to reason about your decisions, not just prompt your way through them.
 The activity can begin from December 2026.
 Qualifications
 Studies in the field of Computer Science, Business Informatics, Data Science, or a comparable degree program
 Proficient in German and English, both written and spoken
 Engagement and ability to work in a team
 Analytical mindset and structured, strategic approach to work
 Solid TypeScript skills – You know what You're doing, with or without an AI (Artificial Intelligence) assistant
 Familiarity with at least one Artificial Intelligence (AI) framework or Application Programming Interface (API) (e.g., OpenAI API, LangChain, Hugging Face)
 Understanding of software engineering fundamentals, including testing, clean code, and Git workflows
