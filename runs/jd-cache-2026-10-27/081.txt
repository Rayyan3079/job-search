Overview
 Rex.zone is hiring for full-time, fully remote data annotation specialists supporting AI training data for large language models and computer vision systems. You will perform data labeling, RLHF evaluation, prompt evaluation, and QA evaluation to improve training data quality and model performance across production LLM training pipelines.
 Key Responsibilities
 Annotate multi-modal datasets (text, image, and structured records) following detailed guidelines and rubrics
 Complete NLP and computer vision annotation tasks (e.g., NER, classification, extraction, bounding boxes/polygons) and content safety labeling
 Perform RLHF preference ranking, pairwise comparisons, and rubric-based scoring; document clear rationales
 Run QA evaluation passes, resolve edge cases, and flag ambiguous prompts/items for review
 Collaborate asynchronously with project leads, reviewers, and calibration workflows to improve consistency
 Required Qualifications
 Mid-to-senior experience in data annotation/data labeling operations with measurable quality outcomes
 Ability to apply detailed rubrics consistently and write clear QA rationales
 Familiarity with RLHF concepts, prompt evaluation, and LLM evaluation signals
 Strong English comprehension and professional writing for guideline interpretation and issue reporting
 Quality & Measurement
 Measured via guideline compliance, audit pass rate, calibration accuracy, and inter-annotator consistency
 Feedback provided through reviewer notes, audits, and periodic calibration sessions
 How To Apply
 Apply through Rex.zone and complete the qualification and calibration steps to be matched to active remote production projects.
