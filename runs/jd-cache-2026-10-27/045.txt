 Job Description
 Job Summary:
We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical BI Developer to join our team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining business intelligence solutions (primarily in EPIC Cogito platform and Microsoft Fabric/Power BI), that turn data into actionable insights. You will work closely with stakeholders to understand business requirements and translate them into technical specifications and data visualizations.
 Key Responsibilities
 Design and develop BI solutions using tools like EPIC Cogito and Power BI
 Collaborate with business analysts, data engineers, and end-users to gather requirements and deliver insightful dashboards and reports.
 Optimize and troubleshoot BI tools, reports, and queries for performance and usability.
 Help design data models and data marts to support analytics needs.
 Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and security in reporting solutions.
 Provide support and training to end-users on BI tools and dashboards.
 Stay up to date with BI trends, best practices, and new technologies.
 Create custom extracts using SQL to support ad hoc and specific mandated requirements.
 Testing BI tools, dashboards, reports and extracts.
 Required Qualifications
 Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, a related field, or equivalent experience.
 2+ years of experience in BI development or data analytics.
 Proficiency with BI tools (e.g EPIC Cogito, Power BI).
 Strong SQL skills (e.g., SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL).
 Understanding of data governance, data quality, and security practices.
 Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
 Certification in EPIC Clarity Data Model and EPIC Caboodle Data Model
 Preferred Qualifications
 Epic Cogito
 Experience using Epic’s reporting tools and reporting databases
 Experience creating Workbench report, editing Dashboards, editing extensions, editing SlicerDicer
 Microsoft
 Experience with Fabric or Power BI
 Other
 Familiarity with scripting languages (e.g., Python, R) for data analysis.
