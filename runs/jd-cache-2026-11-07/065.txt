 Create the documentation and presentation for management
 Develop analysis and reporting capabilities; monitor performance and quality control plans to identify improvements
 Be adept at queries, report writing and presenting findings

 Participate in the design, implementation and interpretation of experiments/trials for continuous refinement of ongoing strategies
 Responsible for data quality and timely delivery of reports that make common business sense
 Participate in Analytics & Reporting trainings to evangelize data across the organization
 Be able to work autonomously without supervision
