 Your Role
 As a Finance Intern in our Commercial Sales organization within Process Solutions EMEA, you will support financial analysis, sales forecasting, reporting, and business insights. Working closely with experienced Finance colleagues and cross-functional stakeholders, you will gain practical experience in Commercial Finance and Finance Business Partnering in an international corporate environment.
 Your Key Responsibilities
 Support the Commercial Finance Team in providing financial insights
 Analyze sales performance and development, including price and volume effects, as well as assess their implications on profitability
 Prepare and maintain regular management reports, dashboards, and KPI analyses
 Support the Sales organization during forecasting, target setting, and financial planning cycles
 Compare actual performance with targets, forecasts, and prior-year results, and identify key drivers and deviations
 Prepare ad-hoc analyses and business cases to support commercial decisions and strategic initiatives
 Help translate complex financial and commercial data into clear, actionable insights and recommendations
 Support the continuous improvement and automation of reporting and analytical processes
 Contribute to Finance projects
 Your Profile
 Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Business Administration, Economics, Accounting, or a related field
 Strong analytical and quantitative skills and a strong interest in the Life Science or Healthcare industry
 Excellent Excel skills; experience with Power BI, or other reporting/analytics tools is a plus
 Ability to structure complex data and communicate key findings in a clear and concise manner
 Strong business acumen and an interest in understanding the commercial drivers behind financial performance
 Proactive, independent, and solution-oriented working style
 Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
 Comfortable working in an international and cross-functional environment
 Fluent in English; German or French is a plus
