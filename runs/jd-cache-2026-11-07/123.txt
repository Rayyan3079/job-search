Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're studying Business Informatics, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable analytical program. You're drawn to data, AI, and finance — and ready to contribute from day one.
 A solid grasp of AI concepts — including Large Language Models and machine learning — from coursework or self-directed learning
 Hands-on experience with AI tools such as Copilot or ChatGPT for data analysis and workflow automation
 Proficiency in Excel for structured data work and reporting, as well as PowerPoint
 Foundational finance knowledge — Controlling, Accounting, or Financial Reporting — from your studies or practical experience
 A customer-oriented mindset and the ability to communicate findings clearly to different stakeholders
 Fluency in German and English is required for this role
 You are currently enrolled and will remain a student for at least one more year
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems — whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools — including agentic AI — to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one. This role requires partial office presence in Walldorf.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work on real projects that directly support our Customer Advisory team's governance work and AI readiness. Furthermore, you'll assist the management and team with in their day-to-day work. Your contributions will have a visible impact across Finance & Spend.
 Create data visualisations and dashboards for management reporting
 Develop AI-powered tools and workflows to automate governance and compliance processes
 Contribute to cross-functional projects on Finance Analytics, Governance, and Compliance
 Document workflows and project outcomes to support knowledge sharing
 Administrational support to keep the team running
 Where you belong
 You'll be part of the Customer Advisory Finance & Spend Domains team in Middle & Eastern Europe — a group at the intersection of advisory expertise and digital transformation. The team is responsible for the business development of the SAP Finance & Spend portfolio and for explaining these solutions to our prospective customers. You'll work closely with experienced Customer Advisors and Solution Experts in a hybrid setup, with on-site presence in Walldorf. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen — a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programs, and peers.
