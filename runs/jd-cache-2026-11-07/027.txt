StellDirVor is an independent consulting, trade and technology company specializing in immersive technologies for healthcare. We support organizations in implementing digital innovations — Virtual, Augmented and Mixed Reality as well as AI — particularly for learning, training and real-time assistance in clinical and care environments. By combining technology and healthcare, we help optimize processes, improve knowledge transfer and enhance quality of care.
 Supported by a Germany (ZIM) and Taiwanese (GIPIP) partnership grant, we are currently developing ARAIAS — an AR- and AI-based hands-free training and assistance system for chronic wound care. Using AR smart glasses, the system enables 3D capture, AI-supported analysis, and standardized remote expertise and documentation directly at the point of care.
 As our ML Engineer (Computer Vision & ML Ops), you will build the AI capabilities at the core of ARAIAS: evaluating and fine-tuning computer vision models on smart glasses data, running the ML operations behind them, and applying generative AI to create synthetic medical images for education. We're at an early stage — moving from concept to working prototype — so the near-term focus is on validating models and building toward a proof of concept we can test in real education and clinical settings.
 As part of a small team, you will own the full data lifecycle end to end — from sourcing, consent and annotation to preparation — alongside model development and deployment.
 We welcome candidates at different experience levels. What matters most is a solid ML foundation and genuine awareness of the medical imaging domain.
 Notes:
 Due to the nature of the project, we can only consider candidates who are already residing in Germany.
 Tasks 
 Select and benchmark pre-trained models and/or validated architectures for medical image analysis and adapt them to domain-specific data, ensuring robust performance under real-world conditions
 Identify and adapt suitable generative AI models to create realistic synthetic medical images for education and training
 Evaluate and fine-tune small language models (SLMs) for voice interaction — speech-to-text, intent understanding and text-to-speech — optimized for low-latency or on-device use with smart glasses
 Research and assess state-of-the-art ML models and emerging trends, including Explainable AI (XAI), cybersecurity for ML, and deployment and distribution modes (edge vs. cloud, federated learning, model compression)
 Plan and execute the ML operations pipeline — data gathering, annotation, preparation, training, testing and deployment
 Propose a data collection strategy — research suitable public and clinical data sources, recommend patient consent and other administrative requirements for ML training (e.g., data protection agreements, ethics approval), and define collection and annotation protocols with clinical experts, including labeling guidelines and quality checks
 Identify and validate factors that affect model performance — image conditions, dataset bias, patient diversity, data privacy and regulatory requirements
 Define test strategies and metrics, and validate model outputs against clinician assessments
 Perform build vs. buy analyses for models, tools and services, including cost comparison
 Estimate compute and training costs and propose budgets for model training and deployment
 Choose and set up training environments flexibly — local hardware or cloud — based on cost, data privacy and performance
 Make pragmatic technical decisions appropriate for the current prototyping stage and document results clearly
 Maintain clear, traceable documentation of data sources, models, experiments and design decisions to support audits and quality reviews
 Evaluate and integrate additional sensors where feasible (e.g., depth, LiDAR, thermal, multispectral) and fuse them with RGB input
 Requirements 
 Must-Have 
 Degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Biomedical Engineering, Mathematics or a related field — or equivalent practical background
 Strong Python skills and hands-on use of PyTorch, OpenCV or TensorFlow
 Solid understanding of computer vision — classification, object detection and image segmentation, 3D Reconstruction and Multi-View Geometry
 Hands-on knowledge of transfer learning — adapting pre-trained models and fine-tuning them on custom datasets
 Working knowledge of the full ML lifecycle — data preparation, training, evaluation and deployment
 Basic knowledge of generative models (diffusion models or GANs) and common open-source libraries
 Comfortable training models both locally (GPU workstation) and on cloud platforms
 Able to estimate ML compute costs and compare build vs. buy options to support budget decisions
 Awareness of medical imaging challenges — limited data, label variability between clinicians and class imbalance
 Awareness of healthcare data privacy and ethics (e.g., GDPR, patient consent, anonymization) and why explainability and bias matter in clinical AI
 Able to read research papers and apply them pragmatically
 Self-directed and pragmatic — able to take initiative and own problems end-to-end in a lean team
 Fluent in English — German is a bonus
 Strong Plus 
 Projects or coursework in medical imaging or clinical AI
 Familiarity with semi-supervised learning — leveraging limited or partially labeled data
 Experience with 3D data processing — depth maps, point clouds or mesh reconstruction
 Exposure to multi-modal learning — combining RGB and depth or LiDAR data
 Edge AI or wearable deployment (e.g., TensorFlow Lite, ONNX, model quantization)
 Knowledge of explainability methods (e.g., Grad-CAM, SHAP) or adversarial robustness
 Familiarity with MLOps tools (e.g., MLflow, Weights & Biases, DVC, Docker)
 Awareness of medical device regulation for AI software (e.g., EU MDR, IEC 62304)
 Familiarity with small language models (SLMs) and speech pipelines (e.g., Whisper, on-device speech recognition and text-to-speech)
