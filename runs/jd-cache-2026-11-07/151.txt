Introduction
 Raisin is the world's leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing . Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.
 Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.
 Team 
 The Deposits Integration Operations team serves as the operational gatekeeper, configuration expert, and bridge between Product, Engineering, and Deposit-taking Banks (DtBs). The team is responsible for the end-to-end operational integration of banking partners, ensuring secure, automated, and audit-compliant deposit lifecycle management across the marketplace.
 Your Responsibilities
 Support the processing of interest rate change requests from partner banks across all designated platforms.
 Assist in the creation, maintenance, and technical setup of partner bank profiles within the company's Content Management System (CMS).
 Draft and maintain formal deposit documentation to ensure compliance and accuracy of information provided to customers.
 Conduct regular quality assurance testing, data validation, and daily monitoring of operational workflows to safeguard data integrity.
 Execute end-to-end operational updates, ensuring all system changes are fully validated, documented, and published in a timely manner.
 Your Profile
 You are currently an enrolled student in a university program, preferably in Business, Finance, Information Technology, or a related field.
 You possess an extremely high level of precision and attention to detail, which is essential for managing critical interest rate changes and product attributes.
 You have a strong technical affinity and are comfortable working with complex software environments and tools like Jira and Slack.
 You are fluent in English with strong communication skills.
 Ideally, you have some experience with Content Management Systems (e.g., Contentstack) or Business Intelligence tools like Looker.
 Basic knowledge of or an interest in learning Jinja2 templating for dynamic document creation is a plus.
 Proficiency in other European languages (e.g., German, Spanish, or Dutch) and a general understanding of banking products are considered an advantage.
