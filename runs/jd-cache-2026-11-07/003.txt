NTT DATA is a trusted global innovator of business and technology services, helping clients innovate, optimize, and transform for success. We strive to hire exceptional, innovative, and passionate individuals who want to grow with us. If you want to be part of an inclusive, adaptable, and forward-thinking organization, apply now.
 Deine Mission
 Key Responsibilities 
 Own and execute the Banking AI go-to-market strategy across the DACH region.
 Build trusted relationships with C-level executives and senior stakeholders at banks and banking service providers.
 Identify, qualify, and shape AI opportunities, from lighthouse engagements to AI-native transformation programs.
 Translate banking challenges into compelling AI business cases, value propositions, and transformation roadmaps.
 Partner with Client Partners, Sales, and Industry Leaders to originate and close AI engagements.
 Lead executive-level presales, proposal positioning, and strategic deal reviews.
 Coordinate the Banking AI GTM team across solution consulting, offering development, marketing, content, and analyst relations.
 Develop new offerings and commercial models, including agentic AI, Service-as-Software, co-innovation, and AI-enabled services.
 Collaborate with AI, cloud, data, and banking technology partners to strengthen solutions and routes to market.
 Represent the organization as an AI thought leader at conferences, analyst briefings, and executive engagements.
 Drive pipeline and revenue performance while embedding responsible AI, security, privacy, governance, model risk, and regulatory requirements.
 Operate effectively within a complex, international, and matrixed organization.
 Basic Qualifications 
 12+ years of experience in consulting, professional services, technology, or industry roles, including progressive leadership responsibility.
 5+ years leading AI, data, analytics, or digital transformation initiatives.
 2+ years of practical experience with Generative AI, large language models, AI agents, or GenAI-enabled applications.
 5+ years of banking industry experience through client delivery, account ownership, or solution leadership, ideally in the DACH region.
 Experience leading or supporting presales, solution shaping, proposals, and executive deal reviews.
 Fluency in German and English and experience working with cross-cultural teams across DACH and Europe.
 Preferred Skills 
 Proven experience developing and executing Banking GTM strategies in the DACH region.
 Strong understanding of Generative AI, agentic AI, machine learning, NLP, banking value chains, and core processes.
 Familiarity with Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, and banking technology ecosystems.
 Track record of commercializing advanced technology solutions and driving growth in banking accounts.
 Strong executive presence and the ability to influence C-suite stakeholders.
 Experience with responsible AI, AI governance, data privacy, regulatory compliance, and model risk management in banking.
 Benefits: 
 Flexible working hours with a flexitime arrangement; business travel time is considered working time
 30 days of annual leave, plus December 24 and December 31 as additional non-working days
 The option to take a sabbatical of up to one year
 Remote work and mobile working from other EU countries
 Attractive benefits and compensation optimisation programmes, including an occupational pension scheme, JobRad bike leasing, a public transport pass, exclusive discounts through Corporate Benefits, and much more
 Comprehensive health and preventive care benefits, including extended continued remuneration in the event of illness, occupational health services, worldwide accident insurance covering both work-related and leisure-time accidents, and family support services
