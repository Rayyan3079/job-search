What’s the Opportunity 
 We’re looking for a Data Engineer II to help us turn the vast amounts of data generated by Ireland’s leading online marketplaces - Daft.ie, DoneDeal.ie and Adverts.ie - into useful insight for our users and employees.
 In this role, you will build processes to ingest, store and transform the data so that it can be used for reporting and insights, to power features on our websites including predictive models.
 How you’ll Create with Impact 
 Developing data pipelines and associated data transformations that support data production and consumption throughout the organisation
 Ensuring our data is trusted by our data consumers, colleagues and partners and that we provide timely and appropriate data to support the business
 Driving data modeling activities to simplify raw data into consumable data sets for end-users
 Building and maintaining feature pipelines and training/scoring workflows for predictive models in production
 Supporting the platform maintenance activities to ensure ongoing smooth operation
 Collaborating with Daft and DoneDeal application development teams to provide the data needed for some data features for end users on our websites
 Working with modern AI tooling such as Amazon Bedrock to power AI use cases on Daft and DoneDeal; and internal tooling for data self-serve
 What you’ll need to Play your Part 
 A collaborative spirit and growth mindset
 At least 2 years proven data engineering experience, with some exposure to machine learning in production
 Hands-on experience with SQL (DDL, DML) and Python in your role(s). (Most of our production systems are Java based, however the data team uses Python)
 Experience with relational databases (Redshift, Postgres, MySQL).
 Experience working on large-scale data warehousing and analytics projects, built on highly available, distributed systems
 Experience working on large-scale data warehousing and analytics projects
 Advantage if you have experience with 
 Building or productionising machine learning workflows in Python (e.g. scikit-learn, XGBoost) and a solid grasp of how to evaluate model performance
 Cloud technologies. (We use AWS technologies such as Redshift, Lambda, Athena, S3, Kinesis, Glue, Bedrock and Sagemaker)
 ML platforms and MLOps tooling (e.g. SageMaker, feature stores, model monitoring)
