 Becoming part of Mercedes-Benz means finding your individual role and workspace to unleash your talents to the fullest. It means becoming your best self in a global automotive company that has the goal to build the most desirable cars in the world. Empowered by visionary colleagues who share the same pioneering spirit. Together for excellence.  
 Our team is currently in a growth phase and will consist of up to 15 members across our international locations. We are looking for highly motivated individuals who support each other and bring a collaborative spirit. Our core values are trust, an open feedback culture, clear communication, and knowledge sharing.
 Our expertise lies in testing AUTOSAR base software and component software as well as in automating these tests within a continuous testing pipeline. We verify software on HiL systems and on our own test benches, covering everything from measurement and test execution to automated regression testing.
 As a working student in our team, you will play an active role in the following areas:
 Performing measurements on HiL systems using Gliwa (e.g. runtime and resource analysis of AUTOSAR software)
 Executing tests on our testbenches and analyzing the results
 Supporting the development of our test automation, including the test execution framework (CAPL, Python)
 Creating and maintaining test configurations for base software and component software tests
 Supporting the definition and creation of ASPICE-compatible processes
 Supporting development teams in applying ASPICE-compliant processes
 The activity can begin in mid-January 2027.
 Qualifications
 Enrolled student in computer science, electrical engineering or a comparable technical field
 Proficient in German and English, both written and spoken
 Good programming skills in Python, C or C++; experience with CAPL is a strong advantage
 Knowledge of AUTOSAR and automotive networks (Ethernet, FlexRay, CAN(-FD), LIN)
 Knowledge of Vector tools (CANoe, CANalyzer, DIVA) and of measurement tools such as Gliwa is an advantage
 Experience with HiL systems or test benches is an advantage
 Knowledge of JIRA, Confluence or equivalent is an advantage
 Professional experience in the automotive industry is an advantage
 Good knowledge of MS Office
 Good communication skills
 Proactive and well-organized
 Ability to think logically and analytically
