Role and tasks
 Automate and enhance data analysis tools through the development of Excel/VBA solutions and the use of Python or other programming languages to optimize processes

 Collaborate with business departments to gather requirements, document solutions, and support testing, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement
 Profile And Skills
 Enrolled student in Computer Science, Mathematics, Finance or a related field, with a strong interest in data analytics, business intelligence and reporting
 Initial programming experience (e.g. SQL, SAS, Python or VBA) and confident use of Excel
 Basic understanding of data processing concepts and a structured, analytical way of working
 Independent, detail-oriented team player with strong communication skills and a high willingness to learn
 Fluent German and English skills, both written and spoken
