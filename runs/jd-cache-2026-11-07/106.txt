Do you enjoy working with data, developing dashboards, and experimenting with new AI technologies? In our department, which works closely with our production facilities, you’ll help make data transparent and develop digital solutions. You'll be able to independently contribute and implement your own ideas - from analysis all the way to a working prototype.
 Tasks
 Design and development of interactive Power BI dashboards for visualizing KPIs and business data
 Preparation, analysis, and integration of data from various data sources
 Gaining practical skills with a focus on further developing and optimizing existing reports, dashboards, and analytics solutions
 Analyzing data to identify trends, anomalies, and correlations
 Testing forecasting and predictive analytics approaches
 Identifying specific AI use cases based on our existing data, applications, and business processes
 Prototyping initial AI solutions and evaluating their potential added value
 Independently working on smaller data and AI projects and contributing your own ideas
 Preparing and presenting results to various stakeholders
 Participating in meetings and workshops
 Requirements
 At the time of the internship, you must be in at least your third semester of a degree program in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Data Science, Industrial Engineering, Business Administration, or a comparable field of study
 Proof of enrollment for the entire duration of the internship (current certificate of enrollment and a written proof of mandatory internship must be included with the application) or an internship between the bachelor’s and master’s programs (Gap Year)
 Analytical thinking skills and an interest in data, BI tools, and digital solutions
 Basic knowledge of data preparation and data analysis
 Interest in AI, generative AI, and AI agents
 Initial experience with Power BI, forecasting, machine learning, or image analysis is a plus
 Enthusiasm for new technologies and for developing your own ideas and use cases
 A structured, independent, and solution-oriented approach to work
 Ability to take initiative, as well as strong communication and teamwork skills
 Excellent German and English language skills
