 About us At Admetrics, we are dedicated to leading a transformation in e-commerce marketing through science and technology. Our SaaS products provide evidence-based solutions that empower marketing teams to scale operations with efficiency and profitability. Leveraging our deep industry expertise, we offer tools that enable rapid, data-driven decision making. 
 As a focused and highly specialized team, we utilize state of the art and in house developed technologies to convert complex data sets into clear, actionable insights, giving our clients a competitive edge to outlearn and outperform their competitors. 
 Your role As a Junior Data Analyst, you will help support, optimize and design our data transformation process. We are looking for a technically strong and solution-oriented analyst. Your work will mostly focus around building and optimizing data modelling architecture and helping answer customer questions around their data. 
 You will work closely alongside our analytics, engineering and customer success teams to transform data into insights through our products. 
 Key Responsibilities: 
 Develop, debug, maintain and optimize data models in dbt (Data build tool), focusing on performance, stability and scalability. 
 Work with customer success to help answer client questions. 
 Collaborate with product and engineering teams to align pipelines and analytics needs according to business requirements. 
 Requirements: 
 Strong command of SQL modelling with dbt. 
 Familiarity with python in context of data modelling, wrangling and analysis is a plus. 
 Experience working with data warehouses such as Clickhouse or Google BigQuery. 
 Familiarity with data modeling, metric design, and scalable reporting architectures. 
 Proven ability to translate product requirements into data models with a focus on performance, efficiency and scalability. 
 Experience working with version control tools and understanding of CI/CD pipelines. 
 Keen eye for testing and debugging data models. 
 Strong communication skills. 
 Nice to Have: 
 Familiarity with data warehouses (ClickHouse, BigQuery, Snowflake). 
 Experience with ecommerce platforms (Shopify, Shopware, WooCommerce, Magento, JTL etc.). 
 Knowledge of advanced modelling techniques, such as marketing mix modelling 
 Knowledge of Bayesian Statistics 
 Experience working in a SaaS startup environment.
