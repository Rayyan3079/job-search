 Team language: German 
 Contract type:  Working Student (60-80 hours per month), preferred start date as soon as possible, for at least 12 months 
 What this role is about 
 You support the implementation and optimization of digital display advertising campaigns on PAYBACK.de and in the PAYBACK App. You are involved throughout the entire campaign lifecycle, from preparation and coordination to execution and performance analysis, gaining hands-on experience in digital campaign and ad management. 
 Your day-to-day work 
 You prepare campaigns for PAYBACK.de and the PAYBACK App, coordinate their implementation, and collaborate closely with internal stakeholders.
 You monitor ongoing campaigns and advertising assets, optimize performance, and support the management of the ad server.
 You analyze campaign results, evaluate performance data, and help derive meaningful insights and recommendations.
 You take ownership of your own projects, such as maintaining and further developing our best practices, and actively contribute your ideas.
 This role is a good fit for you if… 
 You are currently studying Business Administration, Marketing, or a comparable field and will remain enrolled as a student for at least another 12 months.
 You have gained initial practical experience in digital marketing or have already familiarized yourself with digital advertising and campaign formats.
 You are confident working with Excel and can analyze numbers and campaign performance data in a structured way.
 You work independently, in a structured and reliable manner, and can communicate effectively in German with a variety of internal stakeholders.
 Conditions & benefits 
 Working On-Site: This position is fully based at our Munich office. Enjoy direct collaboration, spontaneous exchange of ideas, and being part of a strong team environment every day
 Flexibility:  Vacation depending on your model (24 days/year for internships, 12 days/year as a working student) 
 Food & drinks: Mostly free, healthy and varied meals, daily fresh fruit and popcorn, plus coffee and tea 
 Health & fitness:  Free access to our in-house gym, health weeks, massages, and physiotherapy on-site 
 Culture:  Team events, student lunches, and an open “come as you are” mentality 
 Development: Individual internal trainings and various development opportunities 
 Mobility:  Mobility allowance equivalent to the Deutschlandticket, central location, and subway right outside 
 Compensation 
 Mandatory internship: €1,000 / month 
 Voluntary internship: €2,224 / month 
 Working student (Bachelor): €17 / hour 
 Working student (Master):  €20 / hour 
 Why PAYBACK? 
 PAYBACK is Germany’s leading loyalty program and one of the largest data-driven marketing platforms worldwide. Our solutions reach millions of people every day. 
 With over 1,000 colleagues at our Munich office, we develop all technical solutions in-house and combine data, technology, and marketing to create measurable impact. You can expect interdisciplinary teams, real ownership, and an open, innovation-driven culture. 
 The PAYBACK GROUP values diversity – come as you are.
