 Main Tasks & Responsibilities 
 Maintain and develop Microsoft Fabric and Power BI solutions within a large-scale Data Analytics environment.
 Develop and enhance reporting and analytics solutions, addressing new business requirements across multiple business areas.
 Design and implement data models, including star schema modelling.
 Develop and maintain Power BI reports and dashboards using DAX and Power Query (M).
 Work with Microsoft Fabric components, including Lakehouse, Warehouse, Dataflows Gen2, Data Pipelines and Notebooks.
 Manage Power BI Service functionalities, including workspaces, deployment pipelines, data gateways, incremental refresh and row-level security.

 Discuss business requirements, define KPIs and translate them into effective technical solutions.
 Work across multiple projects, applying a consulting mindset focused on value generation.
 Collaborate with business stakeholders and technical teams in an international environment.

 Strong SQL skills and experience consuming data from cloud data platforms.
