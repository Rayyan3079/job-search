Aufgaben 
 Welcome to Allianz!
 Allianz places the customer and their needs at the centre and has created the role of the Chief Customer Officer (CCO) to underline this. As the CCO team, we are the active and independent voice of our customers: we bring together a wide range of data sources, test products and processes and learn from feedback to drive genuine customer orientation.
 As a Working Student you will support our Text Analytics and Generative AI projects and learn how AI applications are built and tested responsibly in a large company.
 We stand for diversity and welcome you to our team regardless of your age, gender, religion, nationality, social and ethnic background, as well as sexual orientation and identity. We look forward to receiving your application!
 What you can expect as a Working Student in Generative AI & Data Analytics (m/f/d)
 Contribute to our Text Analytics and Generative AI projects - from data preparation and analysis through model and prompt evaluation to the development of new features.
 Make AI applications more reliable and safe: detect and measure hallucinations, check answers against source information, define boundaries for tool usage and test applications in controlled environments.
 Together with the team, develop and evaluate prototypes for internal AI assistants and agent workflows using large language models (LLMs), APIs, tool calling and orchestration.
 Follow developments in Generative AI, evaluate promising open-source tools and techniques, and turn selected ideas into experiments or prototypes for our projects.
 Profil
 What you bring as a Working Student in Generative AI & Data Analytics (m/f/d)
 Currently enrolled in a Master's programme in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics or a related field.
 Solid Python skills; familiarity with Git, SQL and agent harnesses is a plus.
 A good understanding of Data Science or Machine Learning fundamentals and first practical experience with AI projects; experience with Generative AI, Natural Language Processing, LLM APIs or agent-based applications is an advantage.
 Good communication skills and the ability to explain your work in English; German is a plus.
 Openness to creative and innovative solutions as well as enjoyment in working with forward-looking technologies such as artificial intelligence or our internal Allianz GPT.
