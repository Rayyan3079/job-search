We Are Era is a European community economy company focused on community-led relevance. We decode what moves people through data and translate those insights into culturally resonant storytelling – across platforms, formats and markets.
With offices in Amsterdam, Antwerp, Berlin, Cologne, Münster, Paris and Stockholm, our international team creates stories with impact and relevance, tailored to communities across all platforms.
It is no coincidence that We Are Era sees itself as the shortest route to every community and subculture. Our projects range from TV productions and fictional series to branded formats on platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, Twitch, and TikTok. We support and guide creators on their journey toward building their own personal brands. Our international team of 400 employees speaks every language – from mainstream to subculture, from the Fridays for Future movement to K-pop culture.
 Job Description
 As a Working Student in Social Insights & Analytics, you will support our team in turning social media data, digital trends, and audience behaviors into meaningful insights for our business and clients.
You will work at the intersection of data, social media, and the creator economy, contributing to research, analysis, and reporting across various projects. Alongside experienced colleagues, you will gain hands-on experience with data-driven storytelling, explore new analytical approaches, and discover how AI can enhance the way we generate insights.
 Support the Social Insights team in monitoring and analyzing social media conversations, digital trends, and audience behaviors
 Collect, structure, and analyze social media data to identify relevant trends, patterns, and actionable insights
 Assist in creating reports, dashboards, and presentations that translate social media data into clear and meaningful insights
 Conduct research on emerging trends, creator economy developments, influencer marketing, and social media platform dynamics
 Support the preparation of data-driven insights for client pitches, campaigns, and internal stakeholders
 Collaborate with colleagues across marketing, PR, sales, and data teams on ongoing research and analysis projects
 Explore and test Generative AI tools and new analytical approaches to improve research, reporting, and insight generation
 Take ownership of smaller research and analysis tasks with guidance from experienced team members
 Qualifications
 Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Marketing, Business Analytics, Data Science, Media Studies, Business Administration, or a related field
 Strong analytical mindset with an interest in social media trends, digital audiences, and the creator economy
 Initial experience in data analysis, digital research, or social media analytics through university projects, internships, or personal projects is a plus
 Good knowledge of Excel or Google Sheets; familiarity with data visualization tools such as Power BI or Tableau is an advantage
 Curiosity about Generative AI and its applications in research and analytics
 Structured, proactive, and detail-oriented working style, with strong communication skills and a willingness to learn
 Very good English communication skills, both written and spoken (minimum C1); German language skills are a plus
