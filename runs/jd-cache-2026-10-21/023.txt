 Global Project Operations Manager

 Key Responsibilities
 The work model for the role is:
 You Will Be Mainly Accountable For
 Owning global LVS data collection and ensuring accurate, timely acquisition from multiple sources.
 Analyzing operational and project-execution processes to improve data availability, accuracy, and reporting standards.
 Benchmarking KPIs and analyzing drivers of operational performance across LVS and ABB.
 Developing, maintaining, and continuously improving Power BI dashboards and automated reporting solutions.
 Providing management with decision-oriented insights on trends, risks, variations, and recommended actions.
 Supporting operational reporting, the annual budget process, and SAP improvement or transformation projects.
 Driving deployment and adoption of standardized reporting solutions across LVS locations.
 Coordinating with factories, process owners, data owners, and analytics teams to resolve data-quality issues.
 Qualifications For The Role
 Bachelor's degree in Data Analytics or a related field; proven data-analysis experience, preferably in a manufacturing or operations environment
 Knowledge of Power BI and Microsoft Office; dashboard and automated-reporting development; data validation and quality controls; KPI benchmarking; operational and project-execution analysis; basic financial and budget knowledge.
 SAP SD, MM, or PS experience is desirable
 Fluent English skills, both written and spoken
 Strong communication and collaboration across cross-functional, international teams
 Ability to work independently and drive data-based decisions
 Performance-oriented, proactive, and solution-focused mindset
 Forward-looking approach and willingness to challenge the status quo
 High safety, integrity, and compliance standards
 Ability to work transparently and effectively under time pressure
