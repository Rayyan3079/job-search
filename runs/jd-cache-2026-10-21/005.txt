We are looking for a motivated Working Student to join us on two connected missions: keeping our product documentation clear, accurate, and up to date while helping us build the next generation of our internal AI-assisted dashboards. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience in technical writing, data analysis, and modern BI tooling alongside your studies.
 Sounds interesting? We’d love to hear from you!
 Your Role 
 Maintain and enhance product documentation, including help center articles, user guides and internal knowledge base content
 Work closely with Product, Support and Video Production teams to keep documentation accurate, clear and easy to find
 Support the migration of our BI dashboards from Tableau to a new, self-hosted, AI-assisted dashboard solution
 Help design, build and test new dashboard views and reports and validate them against existing Tableau reports
 Research and help evaluate self-hosted BI tools and AI-assisted development approaches
 Document the new dashboard setup and processes for internal stakeholders
 Support both work streams day to day and pick up ad hoc tasks with the wider team
 Your Profile 
 Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Science, Technical Communication, or a related field
 Interest in technical writing / documentation and/or data & BI topics
 Proficient in SQL
 A structured, detail-oriented and reliable working style
 Comfortable learning and working with new tools, including AI-assisted tools
 Good communication skills and enjoyment of cross-team collaboration
 Strong written & spoken English; German is a plus
 Nice to have 
 Experience with Tableau or another BI/reporting tool
 Familiarity with self-hosted BI tools (e.g. Apache Airflow, Metabase, Apache Superset, Grafana)
 Experience with documentation tools (e.g. Confluence, Markdown, Git)
 Basic scripting knowledge (e.g. Python)
 Interest in AI-assisted development tools
