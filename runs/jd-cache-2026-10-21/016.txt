We provide the E.ON Group’s regional business units with access to wholesale energy markets and manage the associated opportunities and risks. Our expertise spans portfolio strategies, risk management and data-driven services. Working across several European countries, we connect regional business needs with European energy markets.

 Your Responsibilities
 You help to further develop our central Counterparty Risk Management System
 Support the digital transformation of our reporting and analytics landscape using technologies such as Power BI, Python, REST APIs, and AI solutions
 You analyze, prepare, and visualize data to support risk management and business decision-making
 Improve data quality, consistency and automation through modern analytics and AI driven approaches
 You support data management, process improvements, and documentation activities
 Your Profile
 You are currently enrolled in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Data Science, Applied Mathematics, Engineering, Economics, Natural Sciences with a strong IT background and basic understanding of, or academic exposure to, risk management
 Good IT skills and a genuine enthusiasm for data preparation, processing and visualization belong to your profile (MS Excel, Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI)
 Experience with databases (Snowflake) and REST APIs is a plus
 Familiarity with Microsoft Fabric or Power Automate, AI and cloud environments would be an advantage (Microsoft Azure, LLMs)
 Strong analytical skills and a solution-oriented mindset belong to your highly cooperative profile
 You have fluent and professional communication skills in written and spoken English, German is a plus
