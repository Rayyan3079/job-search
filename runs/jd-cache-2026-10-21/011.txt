About the Role 
 Join us as a working student and help build an internal data platform for analyzing scientific publications in robotics and AI research. The platform brings together openly available information about publications and turns it into a clear, reliable picture of how the research field is developing.
 Your work will give our team a solid data foundation for understanding research trends, emerging topics, and where new momentum is building. You will design and develop software tools that collect, validate, store and visualize this information, and you will see your work used by colleagues in their daily work.
 What You Will Do 
 Build and maintain an internal research data platform 
 Design, implement and maintain an internal application for collecting, processing, validating, storing and visualizing publication data
 Develop components for automated data acquisition from open sources such as scholarly APIs, repositories and websites
 Implement backend services and data pipelines for reliable processing and integration
 Contribute to frontend components for accessing, managing and exploring the data
 Use AI-assisted development practices to build and extend the platform efficiently
 Ensure data quality and automation 
 Implement automated and AI-supported validation, cleaning and quality assurance for the collected data
 Support the analysis of publication content, for example through text extraction or classification
 Keep the data consistent, traceable and usable across the whole system
 Visualize and report 
 Develop dashboards and visualizations that make research trends and KPIs easy to explore
 Improve usability and functionality of the application together with its users
 Help to continuously enhance the platform
 What You Bring 
 Currently enrolled in Computer Science, Data Science, Software Engineering, Statistics, Robotics or a related field
 Good knowledge of Python and web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
 Knowledge of SQL and databases
 Experience with APIs, web scraping and workflow automation
 Experience with AI-based software development, such as working with AI coding assistants and integrating AI models into applications
 Experience with Git and Docker
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with high attention to detail and data quality
 Structured, independent working style and good communication skills
 Interest in robotics and AI as a research field, and the ability to read and understand scientific publications
 Full professional proficiency in English
 Nice to Have 
 Familiarity with the robotics and AI research ecosystem
 Familiarity with scholarly data sources or publication management tools such as JabRef
 Experience with text processing or LLM-based data extraction
 Experience building dashboards or data visualizations
 Fluent German

 About us 
 Agile Robots SE is an international high-tech company based in Munich, Germany with a production site in Kaufbeuren and more than 3500 employees worldwide.
