 Job Description
 As our Working Student Markets Analytics, you will support the Shopping & Markets Analytics team in helping business stakeholders make data-driven decisions across European markets. You will contribute to reporting, analysis, and dashboard maintenance activities while gaining hands-on experience in data analytics within a leading e-commerce company.
Working closely with analysts and business stakeholders, you will assist in transforming data into actionable insights that support sales performance monitoring and operational decision-making.
 Your Tasks Include
 Support recurring and ad-hoc analyses using SQL, Excel and BI tools 
 Create, maintain and validate dashboards, reports and data extracts 
 Analyze sales and customer performance across markets, channels and segments 
 Perform data quality checks and translate analysis into clear insights and visualizations for business stakeholders 
 Support analytical projects and business initiatives across Central Sales and Regional Markets 
 Qualifications
 Currently enrolled as a student with at least 6 months of studies remaining (working towards a degreein Business Administration, Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Analytics, or a related field)
 Some experience with data analysis through studies, internships, personal projects, or student work 
 Basic SQL and good Excel skills; Tableau, Power BI or similar tools are a plus 
 Strong analytical mindset, attention to detail and interest in e-commerce, sales and customer analytics 
 Good communication skills and good command of English; German is a plus 
 Python knowledge is a plus, but not required 
 Available for working minimum 20 hours a week for at least 6 months
