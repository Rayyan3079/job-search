 Support in supplier and category-related activities, including supplier onboarding and master data maintenance.

 Maintain documentation quality, assist with contract overviews, and ensure the structured filing of supplier documents.
 Prepare reports, presentations, and concise management summaries.
 Support supplier performance, compliance, and risk-management activities.
 Contribute to procurement optimization, standardization, and digitalization projects while collaborating with Product Management, Operations, Finance, Legal, and other internal stakeholders.
 Support by making high level PowerPoint presentations
 What makes you the perfect fit:
 Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Industrial Engineering, Economics, Logistics, or a related field.
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with confidence in working with data.
 Good command of Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint.
 Structured, reliable, and self-driven way of working.
 Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively in an international team.
