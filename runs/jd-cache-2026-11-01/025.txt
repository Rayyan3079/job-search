Universal Music is the market leader in the music business. The world’s most famous and most successful record labels belong to us. We’ve been writing music history for over 100 years representing legendary musicians across all genres. Our accomplishments are driven by our love for music, extraordinary artists and our ambitious team.
Beats & Bytes (B3S) is our tech organization and is comprised of a network of product teams. To be agile and innovative, we live and foster psychological safety, constructive nonconformity and diversity of thought! Empowerment and community are part of our culture. As the technical backbone we generate added value for Universal Music by solving our users’ problems. The B3S Team forms the digital foundation for our business and actively contributes to the competitive advantage of Universal Music.
Here’s your chance to join! Effective as of now , we’re looking for a working student (all genders) Digital Product Management to join us within our Beats and Bytes department in the eCommerce team at our headquarters in Berlin.
 This Is What You Can Learn Here
 You can grow into the field of product management
 You learn about user-centric product management
 You will learn how quality assurance and testing fit into the product lifecycle - from feature development to delivery and user acceptance testing (UAT)
 You will develop communication and documentation skills to ensure clear alignment between technical and non-technical teams
 You will gain experience in identifying, reproducing, and documenting issues, and understand how Quality Assurance contributes to continuous improvement in digital products
 You will gain exposure to sales, supply chain, and finance processes in eCommerce and learn how the systems supporting these processes connect and interact.
 You will learn the B3S way – working in a creative and supportive cross-functional team that connects product, engineering, and business operations
 What You Will Be Doing
 You will work closely with the Product Managers of the eCommerce Team
 You will be responsible for some of the product management tasks like writing user stories
 You will work in product trios with product management, design, research, insights and engineering
 You provide first-level product support for internal teams and help resolve issues in collaboration with engineering
 Assist in user acceptance testing (UAT) and coordinate feedback loops with stakeholders
 Document testing outcomes, feature behavior, and user feedback for product improvements will also be in your field of work
 You maintain and improve our product documentation and support guides
 What else would you like to learn? Let’s discuss your interests – a lot is possible with our team! 
 We Would Like To Meet You If
 You are enrolled in a full-time degree programme in product management or another relevant field, with more than 12 months remaining in your studies.
 You think strategically and unconventionally, quickly connecting disparate information to uncover insights opportunities and actionable outcomes
 You have an interest in product development, testing, and technical support
 You are detail-oriented, organized, and enjoy solving problems
 You are proficient in English (German skills are a plus)
 You have a proactive, team-oriented mindset and are eager to learn
 Basic knowledge of eCommerce systems
 You are excited to work in a fast-paced environment and collaborate across teams
