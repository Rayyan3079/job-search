Location 
Oberhaching (near Munich), on-site
 About us 
 PFASuiki destroys PFAS, the “forever chemicals”, in contaminated water instead of just moving them elsewhere. We are a deep-tech startup with our lab and office in Oberhaching, south of Munich.
 We are currently developing a system for treating PFAS-contaminated water. We are looking for a mechanical engineering student to work with us on its design and build.
 Your tasks 
 You will work alongside our engineers. The main focus is practical work on the pilot plant.
 Carry out the hands-on build and mechanical assembly of our system
 Develop 3D models and technical drawings in SolidWorks to support the build
 Your profile 
 Enrolled in mechanical engineering or a related degree
 Experience with SolidWorks or a comparable CAD tool
 Practical workshop or assembly experience, from university, a student team, an internship or your own projects
 Able to read and create technical drawings
 A careful, safe way of working with tools and equipment
 Available on-site in Oberhaching for 20 h/week as a working student or 40 h/week as an intern
 Good English; German is a plus
 What we offer 
 Real hardware: you help build the plant that takes our technology out of the lab
 Day-to-day work with experienced engineers, who supervise and support you
 A deep-tech team working on an environmental problem to save the world
 On-site in Oberhaching, near Furth station on the S3 line
 How to apply 
 Apply through this listing with your CV and a cover letter that describes technical projects you have worked on.
