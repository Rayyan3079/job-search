About The Job
 Louis Vuitton is seeking a dynamic, analytical, and detail-oriented student or recent graduate to join our German Supply Chain team as a Supply Chain Business Analyst Intern (f/m/d).
 In this role, you will work closely with the Supply Chain and Retail Operations teams to analyze order management, inventory flows, optimize stock distribution across our store network, and support strategic supply chain projects. 
 This is an exceptional opportunity to gain hands-on experience in luxury retail operations and develop deep analytical expertise within a global Maison.
 Job responsibilities
 Data Analysis & Reporting: Monitor, analyze, and report on key supply chain performance indicators (KPIs), including inventory levels, stock turnover, lead times, and sell-through rates.
 Inventory Optimization: Assist in managing product replenishment and stock balancing across all Louis Vuitton retail locations in Germany & Austria to maximize product availability and minimize out-of-stocks.
 Launch Support: Support the supply chain team & stores in analyzing historical sales data, seasonal trends, and upcoming product launches to refine demand forecasts.
 Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Retail Team, Merchandising, Supply Chain & Stores to align stock strategies with commercial objectives.
 Process Improvement: Identify inefficiencies in current supply chain processes and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives, automation projects, and reporting enhancements.
 Ad-Hoc Analysis: Prepare presentations, ad-hoc reports, and strategic insights for management meetings and operational reviews.
 Profile
 Education: Currently enrolled in or a recent graduate of a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business Administration, Engineering, Economics, or a related field.
 Experience: Previous internship or project experience in supply chain, retail operations, data analysis, or consulting is an advantage.
 Technical Skills:
 Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel (VLOOKUP, pivot tables, data modeling).
 Familiarity with ERP systems (e.g., SAP) and business intelligence tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) is a strong plus.
 Analytical Mindset: Strong quantitative skills with a sharp eye for detail and the ability to translate complex data sets into actionable business insights.
 Soft Skills: Excellent communication and interpersonal skills; proactive, adaptable, and able to thrive in a fast-paced luxury retail environment.
 Languages: Professional fluency in English and German (both written and spoken) is mandatory. 
 Additional information
 What We Offer
 A comprehensive onboarding process and continuous mentoring from experienced supply chain professionals.
 Exposure to top-tier luxury retail operations and a multicultural corporate environment.
 Employee benefits and competitive internship compensation in accordance with German regulations.
