WHAT THIS JOB IS:
 Many of the processes we fix for clients in Germany end in SAP: purchase orders, invoices, cost center postings. When a request from our workflow does not arrive there correctly, someone has to find out why. We are hiring an SAP Support Specialist in Germany for that. Full-time and remote anywhere in Germany. The Chicago team is online from your afternoon.
 YOUR PART IN IT: Investigate why records from our workflows fail or arrive incomplete in SAP
 Check IDocs, interface logs and master data with client SAP teams
 Fix mapping and master data problems or hand them over with a clear description
 Test interface changes before go-live
 Document recurring errors and their fixes
 REQUIREMENTS: A degree in business informatics or economics, IT training, or comparable experience
 First experience with SAP as a key user, in support or in a project (internships count)
 Basic understanding of purchasing or accounting processes
 Knowledge of SAP MM or FI (bonus)
 Experience with S/4HANA (bonus)
 WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU:
 On the benefits side: open-ended full-time contract, remote in Germany; hours you can arrange around your day; thirty days of annual leave, public holidays on top; ResolveOps pays into a company pension; training and certification budget every year; and laptop and equipment for your desk at home.
 ResolveOps fixes the processes a business runs on, from the request form to the report at the end. Every assignment is agreed in writing first and finishes with operating instructions.
