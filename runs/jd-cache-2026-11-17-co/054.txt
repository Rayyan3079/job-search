Continental  is a leading tire manufacturer and industry specialist. Founded in 1871, the company generated sales of €19.7 billion in 2025 and currently employs around 76,000 people in 54 countries and markets.
 Tire solutions from the  Tires group sector  make mobility safer, smarter, and more sustainable. Its premium portfolio encompasses car, truck, bus, two-wheel, and specialty tires as well as smart solutions and services for fleets and tire retailers. Continental has been delivering top performance for more than 150 years and is one of the world’s largest tire manufacturers. In fiscal 2025, the Tires group sector generated sales of 13.8 billion euros. Continental's tire division currently employs around 53,000 people worldwide and has 19 production and 16 development sites.
Join us in shaping the future of Production and Industrial Engineering 
 At our Industrial Engineering department in Hannover-Stöcken, we create sustainable value by driving efficiency, organizational and digital transformation in Production. And we want you to be part of that journey. We are looking for a motivated Working Student to support our Communication and Training department , who wants to turn ideas into impact and support us through this transformation.
 In this role, you will play a key part in developing training programs for our Production and Industrial Engineering Tires community. You will work collaboratively with cross-functional teams to create effective communication materials and facilitate training initiatives that enhance operational excellence and employee development. This position offers valuable hands-on experience in a dynamic industrial environment while contributing meaningfully to our organization's success.
 The position is available for up to 20 hours per week , starting as soon as possible, ideally for a period of 12 months .
 Your tasks: 
 Support internal communication and community management activities within the global Industrial Engineering Tires (IET) network.
 Create and maintain communication materials and channels (e.g. Viva Engage, SharePoint, presentations, videos, newsletters).
 Assist in the planning, promotion, and execution of internal events, conventions, training sessions, and workshops.
 Support trainers in the development of training concepts, materials, and documentation.
 Collaborate with production and industrial engineering teams to identify training needs and improve learning initiatives.
 Contribute to internal marketing and communication strategies while ensuring compliance with corporate communication standards.
 Support projects related to operational excellence, continuous improvement, and lean management.
 Provide administrative and organizational support for communication and training activities.
Currently enrolled in Business Administration, Marketing, Communications, Media Design, or a related field
 Demonstrated experience in content creation across multiple formats, including written documentation, digital communications, and presentation materials
 Proven capability in designing professional visual materials, including posters, infographics, and video editing
 Prior experience in training development, facilitation, or educational content creation is of advantage
 Initial experience and a strong interest in community management is preferred
 Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including SharePoint, Copilot, and PowerApps
 Experience with Adobe Creative Suite (InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop) is desirable
 Fluent German and English language skills, written and spoken
 Strong networking and interpersonal skills with the ability to establish and maintain professional relationships across organizational teams
 Exceptional attention to detail with the ability to manage complex technical information accurately and professionally
