Learning nonstop. Innovation on tap. As a Working Student, Central Controlling , you'll support the Finance Insights Team with the cost center and intercompany work that keeps Delivery Hero's numbers accurate. You'll learn how controlling works at a global listed company and own real tasks from day one.
 Be part of the team ensuring Delivery Hero's financial stability and growth. In the Finance & Investor Relations Team, you'll provide the insights and analysis that guide our business decisions, helping us meet our financial goals and earn investor confidence. From reporting to strategic planning, your work will have a direct impact on Delivery Hero's ability to innovate, expand, and deliver outstanding experiences to our customers.
 Process and manage internal tickets on cost center adjustments and maintenance.
 Help resolve intercompany recharge differences.
 Support the preparation of internal monthly financial reports and ad hoc financial analyses, using data from standard reporting tools.
 Act as a point of contact for cost center owners, giving clear and timely answers to standard inquiries.
 Keep the internal intranet page current, with accurate materials and documentation.
 Document existing controlling workflows to streamline onboarding and keep the department consistent.
What you need to be successful 
 Current enrolment in a university degree program, ideally a Master's in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration or a related field.
 Availability to work up to 20 hours per week during the semester.
 MS Office skills, especially Excel, and an affinity for finance tools.
 Interest in processes and finance.
 Organised and well-structured, able to switch between diverse tasks in a fast-paced environment.
 Good communication skills in English.
 Nice to have 
 Experience with any reporting tool.
