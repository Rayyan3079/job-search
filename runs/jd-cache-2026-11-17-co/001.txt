Join our Product Development team as a Working Student (x|w|m) and contribute to the development of innovative cell line, media and testing solutions. In doing so, you will support our teams in focusing on what matters most: better health for more people.
The position is based at our site in Ulm, Germany.
You will become part of a team of dedicated colleagues who look forward to shaping the future together with you.
In this role, you will benefit in two ways: You will gain valuable practical experience while earning money alongside your studies. With 15 to 20 working hours per week, you will have enough flexibility to continue focusing on your studies. 
 Grow With Us – Your Responsibilities 
 Support assigned laboratory activities and defined work packages in cell culture development by applying theoretical knowledge and established methods, processes, and instructions
 Assist with routine cell culture experiments involving CHO and HEK cells
 Maintain documentation, records, and experimental data by ensuring information is accurate, complete, and updated in a timely manner
 Support data evaluation and reporting activities to provide a reliable basis for operational decision-making
 Assist with organizational and administrative tasks that contribute to efficient day-to-day laboratory operations
 Support the team during weekends and holidays as required
 What Will Convince Us 
 Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Pharmaceutical Biotechnology, Life Sciences, or a related field.
 Initial experience with sterile laboratory work or cell culture techniques is an advantage.
 Ability to organize assignments and work reliably and accurately.
 Willingness to learn and adapt to changing priorities
 Fluent in English

 Welcoming Culture: Mutual support, teamspirit and international collaboration; communities on numerous topics, such as coaching, agile working and business women network
 Mobility Benefits: Good connections to public transportation as well as free parking,
