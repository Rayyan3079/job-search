 Job Description: 
 Shape the future of computing with Intel. 
 Are you passionate about low-level software, compiler technologies, and high-performance computing? Join Intel's Data Center Group (DCG) as a System Software Engineering Undergraduate Intern and gain hands-on experience working on technologies that power the world's digital infrastructure.
 In this role, you'll collaborate with experienced engineers on the development and optimization of system software, contributing directly to Intel's next-generation platforms and solutions. This is an excellent opportunity to apply your academic knowledge to real-world challenges while building valuable industry experience.
 What You'll Do:
 Support the design, development, and validation of the Intel Graphics Compiler 
 Work with cross-functional teams to optimize software performance across Intel platforms
 Contribute to system software and reference platform development
 Participate in hands-on engineering projects involving firmware, drivers, operating systems, and middleware
 Apply knowledge gained through coursework and personal projects to solve real technical challenges
 Learn from industry experts while exploring future career opportunities within Intel
 Qualifications:

 Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field with 0-1 years of experience gained through internships, academic projects, coursework, or practical training, or

 Strong programming skills in C/C++ 
 Basic understanding of Operating Systems , Computer Architecture , or related areas
 Excellent analytical, mathematical, and problem-solving skills
 Preferred Qualifications 
 Experience with LLVM 
 Knowledge of OpenCL , CUDA , or SYCL 
 Interest in compiler technologies, performance optimization, or systems programming
