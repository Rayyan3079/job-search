About The Role
 As renewable energy reshapes the global power system, batteries are taking center stage. Making them safe, reliable, and bankable at scale is a defining challenge of the energy transition. At TWAICE, our software turns battery data into insights - so decisions are made with confidence, not guesswork.
We're accelerating the transition into a new energy era and we're looking for those who want to move fast with us.
As a Working Student | Battery & Data Analytics (m/f/d), you'll work hands-on alongside experienced data and engineering professionals, right at the intersection of data and real-world impact. You'll help evaluate and improve the performance and safety of battery energy storage systems used around the globe - all while getting real exposure to live operational data and the technology powering our platform.
Working hours are flexible (especially around exam periods). Ideally, you're available around 20 hours per week and able to come into our central Munich HQ on a regular basis.
 Your daily business will involve 
 Bringing new projects onto our platform
 Connecting data from operational battery storage sites to the TWAICE cloud
 Building digital twins of BESS in our data model
 Running data quality checks so the data our analyses rely on is solid
 Analyzing real-world battery systems on demand
 Making sense of power and data flows in battery energy storage systems
 Running big-data analyses, using AI tools and techniques, to evaluate the performance and safety of BESS around the globe
 Shaping our analytics
 Contributing to the development of TWAICE’s algorithms and data pipelines
 If you’re interested, joining customer meetings to see how your analyses make a difference
 Your profile
 Studying engineering, physics, data science, or a related field
 Basic understanding of batteries and a passion for renewables
 Confident using Python to clean, transform, and analyze data
 Curious, proactive, and hands-on when solving problems
 Bonus: experience with BESS, Databricks, SQL, or AWS
 Don’t tick every box? Apply anyway. We know great people don’t always match every line of a job ad, and we care more about your curiosity and drive than a perfect checklist. We welcome applicants of all backgrounds and look forward to hearing from you.
