Mode of Employment: Fixed Term / Part-Time; (15-20 hours / week)
Are you passionate about robotics and AI? Join us as a Working Student Simulation for Physical AI (f/m/d) in Garching and help shape the future of intelligent machines by working hands-on with cutting-edge simulation tools, innovative prototypes, and visionary research projects!
Connect, learn, and grow with our Working Student Network – your space to exchange ideas, build meaningful relationships, and navigate your Siemens journey.
 You'll make an impact by
 You evaluate state-of-the-art methods, tools, and technologies in robotics, simulation, and physical AI
 You support the development of robotics demonstrators and prototypes for research and innovation use cases
 You work with robotic software stacks and simulation frameworks such as ROS/ROS2, NVIDIA Isaac Sim/Lab, MuJoCo, or related tools
 You contribute to the setup and improvement of simulation environments and robot development workflows
 You help prepare presentations and summarize technical findings for internal and external stakeholders
 You collaborate with cross-functional teams to test and validate new concepts in robotics and automation
 You support the exploration of agentic workflows and AI-driven approaches for robotic systems
 This Is How You'll Win Us Over 
 Education: You are a student in robotics, mechatronics, computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, (applied) mathematics, or a similar field
 Experience and Skills:
 You have solid programming skills in Python and ideally also in C#, C, or C++
 You have first practical experience with robotic software stacks such as ROS/ROS2, NVIDIA Isaac Sim/Lab, MuJoCo, NVIDIA Newton, Gemini Robotics, or similar platforms
 You are interested in robotics simulation, robot control, and AI-based robotics workflows
 You are familiar with design and simulation tools and ideally with reduced-order modeling concepts
 Ways of Working: You enjoy evaluating technical approaches, comparing methods, and presenting your findings in a structured way; You are motivated, curious, and eager to work on real-world robotics challenges
 Languages: Fluent English and/or German round off your profile
