 What You'll Do
 As a working student on the Record-To-Report Agile Product team, you'll be integral in supporting the team’s dynamic and innovative IT delivery processes. Your main responsibilities will include:
 Assist with Agile Transformation: Support the Product Owner and Agile Coach in implementing agile methodologies and practices, helping the team to adapt and thrive in a flexible working environment.
 Coordinate Innovative Deliveries: Aid in the organization and execution of transformation projects, including initiatives that leverage AI to enhance processes and outcomes. Ensure alignment with strategic objectives and contribute to the development of cutting-edge solutions.
 Facilitate Daily Operations: Help team members in managing day-to-day tasks, ensuring the smooth operation of our Run & Secure the Business efforts. Provide administrative and logistical support to maintain high-quality service delivery.
 Enhance Documentation and Communication: Assist in creating, tagging, and organizing content for efficient retrieval and use by the team. Produce clear documentation and communication materials that keep team members informed and engaged.
 What You Bring
 You are a student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences, studying business administration, information technology, or a similar field.
 You have knowledge of corporate finance processes and ERP systems, especially SAP, along with data analytics skills and an understanding of software lifecycles. 
 You possess the ability to plan, organize, manage schedules, and have a basic understanding of Agile and DevOps methodologies. 
 You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills to assess situations and derive insights. 
 You are proficient in both written and verbal English and German for documentation, stakeholder interaction, and active listening. 
 You demonstrate strong teamwork and interpersonal skills, coupled with the ability to work independently and take initiative.
