Role Description
 We are still looking for the very Top Talent…and we would be delighted if you were to join our team!
 More in detail, UST is a multinational company based in North America, certified as a Top Employer and Great Place to Work company, with over 35,000 employees worldwide and presence in more than 35 countries. We are leaders in digital technology services, providing large-scale technological solutions to major companies.
 What we look for?
 We are looking for an experienced BI Developer with 3–5 years of experience working with Power BI, QlikView and Qlik Sense to join a team focused on dashboard support and reporting within a CIB environment .
The role combines hands-on BI development with application support, maintenance, data modelling and quality control , working primarily with existing dashboards and reporting solutions while also contributing to their evolution.
 Key Responsibilities
 Provide technical support for Power BI, QlikView and Qlik Sense dashboards and reporting solutions.
 Support key users and troubleshoot issues related to existing BI applications.
 Maintain and enhance existing dashboards, reports and data models.
 Participate in new developments and evolutionary changes to BI solutions.
 Work with data structures and data loading logic.
 Analyse and optimize existing data models to improve performance and maintainability.
 Perform quality control and functional validation of BI developments.
 Ensure the reliability and consistency of dashboards and reporting solutions.
 Collaborate with technical and business teams within the CIB environment. Mandatory requirements:
 3–5 years of professional experience in Business Intelligence / BI development.
 Strong hands-on experience with Power BI.
 Strong hands-on experience with QlikView.
 Strong hands-on experience with Qlik Sense.
 Experience in Data Modeling.
 Good understanding of Data Structures.
 Experience with Data Loading Logic.
 Knowledge of Model Optimization and performance improvement.
 Experience developing and maintaining dashboards and reporting solutions.
 Experience in Application Support & Maintenance.
 Experience performing Quality Control of BI developments.
 Nice To Have
 Previous experience in CIB / Corporate & Investment Banking.
 Experience within the banking sector.
 Previous exposure to regulated environments.
