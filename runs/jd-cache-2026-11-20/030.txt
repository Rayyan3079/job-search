Ready to take your Data Engineering skills to the next level?
At Baltic Amadeus , we're growing our Data & Analytics team and looking for a Data Engineer who enjoys working with data, solving technical challenges, and building reliable data solutions.
You'll contribute to real-world projects across finance, insurance, telecom, and other industries, working with technologies such as Microsoft Fabric, Azure, and Databricks.
 Why join us? 
 Diverse projects – Work on different data engineering challenges, from modernizing existing Data Warehouses to building new cloud-based Lakehouse solutions. 
 Modern technologies – Gain hands-on experience with Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory, Databricks, Spark, and other tools used in modern data platforms. 
 Learn from experienced engineers – Collaborate with skilled Data Engineers, BI Developers, and Architects who share knowledge and support your technical growth. 
 Your ideas matter – Contribute to technical discussions, suggest improvements, and gradually take on more responsibility as your expertise grows. 
 What You Will Do
 Develop and maintain data pipelines using SQL, Python/PySpark, and cloud-based data engineering tools. 
 Build and modernize Lakehouse and Data Warehouse solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Azure, and Databricks. 
 Integrate data from different source systems, including databases, APIs, and files. 
 Implement data transformations, quality checks, and performance optimizations. 
 Collaborate with other Data Engineers, BI Developers, Architects, and business stakeholders to deliver reliable data solutions. 
 What We Expect From You
 2+ years of experience in Data Engineering, ETL/ELT development, or a similar role. 
 Hands-on experience with SQL and developing ETL/ELT pipelines. Familiarity with Python would be an advantage. 
 Understanding of Data Warehouse and Lakehouse concepts, including data modeling. 
 Familiarity with Spark/PySpark or cloud data platforms such as Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory, or Databricks would be an advantage. 
 Willingness to learn, solve problems, and develop your technical skills. 
 Proficiency in Lithuanian and English (B2+).
