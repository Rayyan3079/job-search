As part of the Network Performance and Controlling team in the heart of Hamburg you will work closely with the controlling and operations units to analyse, automate and improve data‑driven processes that underpin the global liner service network and partnership settlements, enabling sound business decisions for multi‑billion‑USD ship deployment.
 What You'll Do 
 Analyse business processes, data flows and system requirements related to network operations and partnership settlements
 Identify and implement improvements for data quality, processing efficiency and automation
 Support cross‑functional initiatives to integrate and enhance data‑process elements for accurate settlement
 Design, test and optimise data interfaces, workflows and transformation logic across Controlling and Operations
 Develop reusable Python scripts for data extraction, consolidation, validation and analysis of complex datasets
 Create and maintain data sets for reconciliation, data‑flow documentation and process visualisations
 Contribute to the digital transformation and automation of Network & Cooperations processes and systems
 Participate in ad‑hoc projects, including training and system implementation support
 What Makes You A Fit 
 Enrolled in a Master’s program (business studies, business informatics, mathematics, engineering, natural sciences or comparable)
 Strong analytical skills with a high affinity for numbers and IT
 Proficiency in Python; experience with Qlik, Alteryx or similar data‑visualisation/ETL tools
 Ability to develop and implement new concepts and automation solutions
 Proactive, solution‑oriented work style with a strong analytical mindset
 Excellent communication skills; able to present complex issues in a recipient‑oriented manner
 Empathic, reliable and motivated team player
 Fluent in written and spoken English
