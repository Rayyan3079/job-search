Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're a Master's student in a data or engineering field, with strong Java foundations and a curiosity for large-scale data systems.
 Enrolment as a Master's student in Computer Science, Data Science, Data Engineering, Applied Mathematics, or a related field
 Strong Java skills and solid working knowledge of SQL
 Familiarity with Apache Parquet, Arrow, or Iceberg is a genuine plus; Python is welcome
 Experience with data pipeline tools such as DuckDB, Spark, or dbt is an advantage
 A customer-oriented mindset - you consider how your technical decisions affect the teams and systems that depend on your work
 German at C1 or above (written and spoken) is required for this role; English at B2 or above is also needed
 A hybrid working model applies, with some time on-site at our Walldorf or Markdorf office
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems - whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools - including agentic AI - to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 Your first contribution is a technical Proof of Concept evaluating Dremio within SAP's Distributed Energy Resources platform - a system handling high-volume time series data from millions of smart meters. Your findings will shape real architecture decisions.
 Design and run benchmarks for Apache Arrow-based read and compute performance
 Compare locally stored data against federated external sources using Dremio
 Implement write pipelines using Apache Iceberg as the target table format
 Evaluate results and document findings for the architecture review
 Present conclusions to the DER team and contribute to technology stack decisions
 Where you belong
 You'll join the Distributed Energy Resources (DER) team - part of the Industry AI Unit for Energy and Natural Resources - based in Walldorf and Markdorf. The team helps utilities navigate the energy transition, connecting smart meter data, prosumer devices, and commercial agreements at scale. The Energy Data Management component you'll contribute to sits at the core of this - where data reliability, throughput, and consistency that the rest of the product depends on are built and maintained.
When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen - a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers. If you enjoy working close to real data problems — and want your work to matter beyond the PoC itself — this is a good place to learn.
