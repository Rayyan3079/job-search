The Opportunity
We’re looking for a foundational Machine Learning Engineer to join our team in Porto and build the evaluation foundation behind the next generation of AI-native productivity at Superhuman, specifically our agent platform, where multiple AI agents collaborate to solve complex user problems. You’ll own how we define, measure, and improve quality. You’ll build the eval datasets, the infrastructure around them, and the metrics that tell us whether our models and agents are actually getting better for users.
This is a high-autonomy, high-impact role on a foundational team that moves fast from idea to production. As Superhuman Docs’ collaborative workspaces evolve so users can solve arbitrarily complex tasks with agents, rigorous measurement will be what lets us ship with confidence.
 In this role, you will:
 Build high-quality evaluation datasets: Design, source, and curate golden sets, labeled data, and synthetic data for agent behaviors, search ranking, and proactive suggestions. Define annotation guidelines, manage labeling workflows, and ensure datasets are representative, versioned, and trustworthy.
 Build the eval infrastructure: Create scalable, reproducible pipelines for running offline evaluations, regression testing, and benchmarking across models, prompts, and agent configurations. Make it easy for every team to run evals and trust the results.
 Measure and improve quality: Define metrics that reflect real user value, from classic ML metrics (precision/recall, NDCG, calibration) to LLM-as-judge and human-eval approaches. Validate that automated metrics correlate with user outcomes, and use the results to drive model and product decisions.
 Own classic ML systems: Develop and deploy ML models for core product experiences, including search ranking and proactive suggestions that anticipate user needs, with evaluation built in from day one.
 Support the agent platform: Partner with the team building orchestration, routing, and planning to define success criteria and quality gates for multi-agent workflows.
 Close the loop with production: Connect offline evals to online experiments and real-world signals, so we catch regressions early and learn from what users actually experience.
 Ship fast: Move quickly from idea to impact, with no red tape or endless approvals, on a platform used by millions.
 Qualifications
 5+ years of industry experience as a machine learning engineer or applied research scientist.
 Strong foundation in classic ML and NLP, including experimental design, statistics, and metric design, plus working knowledge of modern LLM approaches.
 Hands-on experience building evaluation datasets and the tooling and infrastructure to run evals at scale (data pipelines, labeling workflows, benchmarking frameworks).
 Experience using evaluation results to measure and improve model or product quality, whether for ranking, classification, or LLM-based systems.
 Strong Python and data engineering skills, with experience in reproducible, production-grade ML pipelines.
 Deep care about the end-user experience and a drive to ensure high quality.
 Ability to work independently with minimal guidance, manage priorities across multiple projects, and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams in a fast-paced, results-driven environment.
 Nice to have: experience with LLM-as-judge, agent or workflow evaluation, or search/ranking systems; MS/PhD in Computer Science or a related field.
