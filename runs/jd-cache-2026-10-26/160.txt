 Tasks
 In our department Infotainment Device Integration, we are responsible for the seamless integration of consumer devices such as smartphones, headphones, and wearables into the vehicle infotainment system. This includes the development, validation, and continuous improvement of technologies such as Bluetooth, Apple CarPlay, and Google Android Auto. We are looking for you as a Working Student Data Analytics for Infotainment Systems for this exciting and challenging area.
 You will master these tasks:
 Analyze quality, usage, and diagnostic data from customer vehicles in the field
 Identify product issues, usage challenges, and opportunities to improve the customer experience
 Gain insights from various data sources, including vehicle diagnostics, customer complaints, and user behavior data
 Apply modern AI and machine learning approaches to automate data analysis and pattern recognition
 Develop automated processes for filtering, categorizing, and processing large datasets
 Correlate identified issue patterns with existing ticketing systems and development workflows, such as Jira
 Create insightful dashboards, reports, and visualizations to support data-driven decision-making
 Support quality improvement initiatives by transforming data into actionable recommendations
 What we offer:
 Hands-on experience in cutting-edge automotive infotainment development
 Opportunity to work on real customer-facing quality and user experience topics
 Exposure to large-scale data analytics and AI applications
 Close collaboration with experienced engineers and data analysts
 Flexible start date from November 2026 onwards
 Insights into innovative connected mobility technologies
 An open and collaborative working environment with opportunities for personal and professional development
 Shape the future of connected mobility with us and help create outstanding customer experiences.
 The activity can begin from mid November 2026.
 Qualifications
 Degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a comparable field of study
 Fluent German and English language skills, both written and spoken
 Commitment and ability to work effectively in a team
 Analytical mindset and strategic way of working
 Strong interest in data analytics, artificial intelligence, and software-driven products
 Basic understanding of technical systems and enthusiasm for automotive infotainment technologies
 Ideally, first experience with Python, data analytics, business intelligence, or machine learning
 Independent and structured way of working
 Good communication skills and a proactive attitude
