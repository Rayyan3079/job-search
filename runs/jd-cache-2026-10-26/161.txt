Experteer offers senior executives the opportunity to actively develop their careers. The premium career partner allows recruiters to discreetly contact senior professionals ready for a career change. Candidates can access more than 1,000,000 carefully curated executive positions and also stay informed on relevant career topics and valuable insights.
 Experteer, based in Munich, Germany, has been operating since 2006 and connects more than 6 million candidates worldwide with exclusive job offers and renowned headhunters every day.
 Do you enjoy working in an open and international environment? Then join our dynamic team.
 Your responsibilities
 Depending on your interests, experience, and learning pace, you can quickly grow and take ownership of one or more of the following tasks:
 Become familiar with our technology stack and learn everything about the high-level job market and our job classification framework.
 Research and select the most high-level jobs and classify them according to our key criteria.
 Collaborate with the product and engineering teams to improve internal data management tools that you will be working with.
 Perform regular data quality checks on job, company, and salary data and document results.
 Analyze company and salary data to support our classification framework.
 Increase our data pipeline by supporting the roll-out and scaling of our core crawling technology.
 Identify recurring data issues, investigate root causes together with Product/Engineering, and provide clear examples and suggestions to improve our processes and tools.
 Your profile
 Student halfway through your bachelor's or just started your master's.
 Availability for at least 10 months, working 15-20 hours per week.
 Be proactive, work with minimum supervision, show initiative.
 Excellent command of English is essential. A second language (German, Dutch, French, Italian, or Spanish) is desirable.
 Tech savvy, advanced skills/knowledge of the internet, its tools, and technologies.
 Analytical thinking, qualitative and quantitative analysis skills. 
 Willingness to work in a hybrid setup and to come to our Munich office on a regular basis.
 Our offer
 A highly talented, dynamic, and multinational team
 The chance to work on exciting and challenging projects either independently or as part of a team
 A creative working atmosphere and flat hierarchies
 Flexible working times, hybrid work model, and an office in the heart of Munich.
