About us:
 Quantum Systems specialises in the development, design, and production of small Unmanned Aerial Systems (sUAS). The company’s range of electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) sUAS are built to maximize range and versatility and to provide operators with a seamless user experience. By integrating cutting-edge software capabilities, like edge computing and real-time AI-powered data processing, Quantum Systems is building next-generation UAS for clients in defence, security, public sectors.
 What is your Day to Day Mission:
 As a Working Student in AI Automation, you will help turn promising opportunities for automations into working solutions that make a measurable difference. Together with our Process & Automation Manager and Procurement team, you will develop practical MVPs that reduce repetitive work and create more time for strategic procurement activities.
 You will take ownership of a portfolio of AI automation use cases, from assessing and prioritizing opportunities to rapidly developing and validating MVPs. Beyond individual use cases, you will help build a scalable solution for implementing, expanding and maintaining AI automations across the organization. You will own the solutions you create throughout their lifecycle, from initial concept to reliable day-to-day operation.
 Evaluate and prioritize AI automation use cases based on impact, feasibility and user needs
 Translate procurement processes and pain points into practical solution concepts
 Build MVPs for AI agents and workflow automations with integrations into the ERP Test solutions with end users and improve them based on feedback
 Document process logic, results and realized efficiency gains
 Prepare successful MVPs for scaling, integration or handover
 Explore new AI capabilities and identify further automation opportunities
 What you bring to the team:
 You are currently studying business informatics, computer science, data science, engineering, business administration or a comparable subject 
 You have a strong interest in generative AI, AI agents and process automation
 You bring initial practical experience with programming, automation or low-code tools
 You enjoy understanding processes and turning ideas into simple, working solutions
 You approach problems analytically and are comfortable experimenting with new technologies
 You bring a continuous improvement mindset and are confident challenging the status quo
 You work independently, communicate openly and actively seek feedback
 Experience with APIs, Python, workflow tools or ERP systems such as NetSuite is an advantage
 You communicate confidently in English; German is an advantage
