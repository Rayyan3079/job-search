Your Task Would you like to find out whether the apprenticeship as an IT system management clerk is something for you? With us, you can try out just that! As one of Europe's biggest telecommunications brands, we open the doors to our world for you. During your career orientation internship, we will show you how diverse this job is — from advising and supporting our customers to planning IT systems to organizing and implementing exciting IT projects. This is what you can expect during your career orientation internship: An internship buddy who accompanies you throughout the internship, shows you everyday life and is always there to answer your questions Exciting insights into training as an IT system management clerk and into everyday working life An individual process that is based on your interests Personal tips for your further orientation and application phase Our internship app with interactive content and learning opportunities Your Profile We focus on you! Your internship is your chance to try out new things, ask questions and find out what you enjoy — without any previous knowledge. We let you take a look behind the scenes, give you exciting insights and are by your side as you discover your strengths. To take a look behind the scenes with us, you should bring the following: You are still in school and are aiming for a secondary school diploma, secondary school diploma, high school diploma or a comparable degree Are you interested in computer technology and IT topics — or simply want to try out whether you enjoy working with IT systems You enjoy working in a team and are open to learning new things You would like to see how we at Telekom plan, implement and ensure that they are used optimally Benefits We'll take you into the world of Magenta Telekom , show you how we work and share current projects with you. The best part: Our employees, trainees or dual students take you into their everyday lives, share their experiences with you and give you real insights — you might even discover your future career in the process. Students can complete these types of internships with us for professional orientation: Compulsory internship in general education schools (no minimum age, max. internship duration: 1-3 weeks) Compulsory school internship vocational school (no minimum age, max. internship duration: 3 months) Voluntary orientation internship during school holidays (minimum age: 15 years, max. internship duration: 4 weeks) Important: Please indicate the desired period in your application and — if it is a mandatory internship — upload the proof directly. Application period: To ensure that your internship with us is not only good, but really good, we need a bit of advance notice. In this way, we can work together to find the right area of employment for you and prepare everything optimally. Therefore, apply at least 6 months and at least 2 months before your desired start date. Please note: Whether the internship can actually take place always depends on whether a suitable area of employment is available in the desired period of time. Our career orientation internship is aimed exclusively at students. Are you looking for an internship for students? Then find out more here. We look forward to receiving your application — regardless of whether you already know exactly where you want to go or are simply curious.
