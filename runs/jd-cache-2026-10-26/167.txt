Beschreibung We are currently looking for a Working Student to join the Data & Analytics Team! The position will be based in our Munich office. About the Job As a Working Student in our Data & Analytics team, you will work across Reporting & Analytics, Data Services, and the Data Office, helping ensure that decisions across the company are based on reliable data. This role offers the opportunity to gain practical insights into asset management and capital markets while developing experience in reporting, data analytics, and data management within an international organization. You will also learn how to use AI tools meaningfully and responsibly in your day-to-day work while receiving support and guidance from experienced team members. What you do • Prepare customized proposal documents on our investment services in response to client inquiries • Calculate common financial metrics and present portfolio structures in a clear and concise manner • Identify opportunities to simplify and improve existing processes • Support Data Governance activities to ensure clear data ownership and consistent data management practices • Collaborate with teams across different functions, prepare reports and workshops, and contribute your own ideas and recommendations. What you bring • Currently pursuing a degree in Economics, Mathematics, Finance, or a related field, with at least one year remaining until graduation • Basic knowledge of capital markets and a strong affinity for data and reporting • Confident user of Microsoft Excel • Very good written and spoken German and English skills • Open to using AI tools such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, or Claude, with an ethical and responsible approach to their use • Able to work approximately 20 hours per week across three days, including two days in the Munich office and one day from home • Residential address in or near Munich and enrolled at a university in Munich or the surrounding area • Not at the very beginning of your studies • Ability to use Microsoft Copilot, with a strong commitment to ethical and responsible AI practices.
