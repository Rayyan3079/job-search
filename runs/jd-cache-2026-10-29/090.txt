Job Title
 Business Intelligence (BI) Developer
 Summary
 We are looking for a talented Business Intelligence (BI) Developer to join our team and help transform data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions. Working closely with business and technical stakeholders, you will develop reliable and user-friendly BI solutions, improve how data is integrated and accessed, and help turn complex information into clear, actionable insights.
 Key Responsibilities
 Design, develop and maintain dashboards and reports using Power BI and Qlik
 Translate business requirements into effective BI and reporting solutions
 Develop and optimize SQL queries, stored procedures and database structures
 Create Python scripts for data transformation, automation and integration
 Build and maintain ETL processes, ensuring data accuracy and consistency
 Collaborate with business and technical stakeholders to understand reporting and analytical needs
 Ensure the quality, reliability and performance of reporting solutions
 Contribute to improvements in data architecture, BI standards and best practices
 Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and automate data and reporting processes
 About The Ideal Candidate
 Strong experience with SQL and relational databases such as SQL Server, Oracle or PostgreSQL
 Hands-on experience with Power BI, including DAX, Power Query and data modelling
 Experience with QlikView and/or Qlik Sense, including scripting, data loading and dashboard development
 Good knowledge of Python for data manipulation and automation
 Experience with ETL and data integration processes
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
 Ability to translate business needs into effective technical solutions
 Comfortable collaborating with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
 Experience with cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS or Google Cloud Platform is a plus
 Knowledge of data warehousing concepts is an advantage
 Experience with Git or other version-control tools is a plus
