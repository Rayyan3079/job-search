 Tasks
 Your tasks?
 As a Working Student in Sales Data Architecture & Agentic AI (m/f/d/x), you will support the team in analyzing and harmonizing global sales and master data across different markets and systems.
 You will contribute to the development of data models, semantic models, and ontologies to create a unified understanding of sales data.
 You will also support the design and development of innovative talk-to-data solutions and AI agents.
 You will analyze existing data structures and explore relationships between different business terms and data.
 You will document data structures, business terms, and their relationships, presenting complex information in a clear and understandable way.
 In addition, you will work closely with Data Architects, Data Engineers, and AI Engineers and support the development of innovative data and AI solutions.
