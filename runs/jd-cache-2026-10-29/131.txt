How You Can Contribute To Our Shared Success
 Join the Value Engineering team at One Data and help customers understand the business value of our software for them. Our team covers presales and the initial phase of W2V projects, our paid presales engagements. Working alongside experienced Value Engineers, you help translate customer requirements into relevant demos, first results, and quantifiable value, for example in SAP BW migration and data product management. Interested? Then we are looking for you to grow with us as a Value Analyst (f/m/d) - Working Student and become part of our team!
 These tasks are waiting for you in our team
 You help prepare and participate in customer meetings and discovery workshops, documenting business and technical requirements and identifying and quantifying relevant use cases.
 You prepare demo data and support the setup, testing and presentation of customer demos with One Data; to do this, you help develop an Agentic AI framework for demo building.
 You help to carry out complex analyses with the help of our One Data software and other tools. 
 You support data analyses using One Data, SQL or Python within Datasphere, Databricks, and/or Snowflake to assess data quality and the technical feasibility of customer use cases.
 You help build value arguments and business cases that make potential benefits tangible, such as time savings, reduced manual effort or improved data quality.
 You turn findings into clear customer presentations and help maintain reusable demos, templates and documentation together with Sales and our technical teams.
 Your Data - What We Are Looking Forward To
 You are highly motivated to work in presales at the intersection of large enterprises, AI and data.
 You have relevant experience or are currently studying a subject relevant to the position, e.g., computer science, business informatics, business administration, natural sciences, mathematics.
 You are equally enthusiastic about presales and AI- or data-inspired work and are excited about solving problems with an analytical mindset.
 You have already gained some experience with Python and have knowledge of databases and SQL.
 Our team is the most important thing we have, so collaboration, learning from each other and communication should be important to you. 
 Fluent German and a good command of English are required.

 About Us
 One Data is a German software company leading the category of Data Product Lifecycle Management. Its standard software solution is the Data Product Space, that is a collaborative software platform where business and data teams come together to define, build, and scale trusted, AI-ready data products.
Founded in 2013 by Dr. Andreas Böhm in Passau, Germany, One Data enables organizations to transform fragmented data landscapes into structured, governed, and reusable data assets that deliver measurable business value. With deep expertise in data lifecycle management, the company helps enterprises accelerate time-to-insight, reduce costs, and lay the foundation for responsible AI adoption.
Our steadily growing team of around 150 talented individuals from more than 20 nations works in Passau, Munich, Frankfurt and Berlin. Our colleagues are our top priority – our success is built on their know-how, creativity and commitment. Our working environment is based on mutual respect, authenticity and commitment. We give our One Datas the freedom to express their creativity and they receive our full support for their development – so that they can maximize their potential with us.
