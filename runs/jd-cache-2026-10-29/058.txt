NVIDIA is looking for a Software Engineering Intern to join a team that builds infrastructure and agentic AI services for teams across the company. Our job is to remove other teams' bottlenecks: we build and run core internal services that power large geo-distributed infrastructure, and a growing share of what we ship is driven by AI agents. As an intern, you will work alongside senior engineers to design, build, and ship production systems – from backend services that run automated workflows at massive scale to AI agents that take routine work off engineers' plates. This is an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience with agentic AI and modern infrastructure while contributing to real products used by teams across NVIDIA.
 What You'll Be Doing
 Work with senior engineers to design, build, and improve backend services and APIs for internal platforms that run automated workflows at massive scale
 Build AI agents and agentic workflows that connect LLMs to tools and internal services, and integrate them into production systems
 Help make agents reliable and safe in production – from access control and guardrails to evaluating agent quality
 Assist in building data pipelines and GPU job orchestration for synthetic data generation and model evaluation at scale
 Implement and improve observability (tracing, metrics, logging) for distributed services and agents
 Participate in code reviews, testing, and debugging, and apply software engineering best practices in a production environment
 Work in a product-minded way: talk to internal users, prototype quickly, collect feedback, and iterate
 What We Need To See
 Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
 Strong programming fundamentals in at least one of: Go (preferred), Python, or C#
 Solid understanding of data structures and algorithms
 Basic knowledge of software design patterns and API design concepts
 Hands-on experience building with LLMs – for example, a project, prototype, or coursework that uses LLM APIs
 Familiarity with version control systems (Git)
 Understanding of database concepts and SQL
 Strong problem-solving skills and eagerness to learn new technologies
 Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in a team environment
 Ways To Stand Out From The Crowd
 Experience building AI agents or LLM-powered applications: tool/function calling, MCP, RAG, or agent frameworks (e.g., LangGraph, NVIDIA NeMo Agent Toolkit)
 Experience with distributed systems concepts (REST APIs, microservices architecture) and containerization (Docker)
 Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure), GPU workloads, and infrastructure automation
 Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, DevOps practices, observability tools (OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana), and orchestration platforms (Kubernetes)
 Experience with database technologies (MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch)
