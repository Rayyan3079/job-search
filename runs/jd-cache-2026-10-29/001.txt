 General Application - School Internship (m/f/d)
 With us, you’ll be right where the digital and analog worlds come together because we manufacture microchips. Our chips make it possible, for example, to unlock your smartphone using your fingerprint or help sensors make cars smarter and more convenient. 
 During a school internship at X-FAB, you can gain first-hand experience and find out whether an apprenticeship or degree program at X-FAB could be the right path for you. 
 Your Benefits: 
 An experienced team that looks forward to welcoming you 
 A tram stop right outside our door 
 Insights into a large, international industrial company 
 Valuable experience to help you explore future apprenticeship and study opportunities 
 Possible Internship Areas: 
 IT
 Mechatronics
 Microtechnology / Microchip Manufacturing
 Electronics / Facility Management
 Logistics
 Sales & Marketing
 Engineering
 Your Profile: 
 You are at least 13 years old
 You are completing the internship as part of your school education
 You are motivated and eager to learn
 Please upload your complete application documents to the system: 
 Cover letter, including your preferred internship period and department
 CV
 Most recent school report
 School internship agreement, if available
