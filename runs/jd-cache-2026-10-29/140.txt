Your role
Bring your curiosity to work as our next Technical Data Analytics Intern. You will be part of our Engineering & Technical Support team reporting to our Service Development Engineer. The internship is limited to a maximum duration of six months.
In this role, your mission is to turn data from connected industrial vacuum systems into actionable insights and support the development of scalable digital service solutions. You will contribute directly to how we create high‑quality products and solutions for our customers.
Your day-to-day
 Analyze data from connected industrial vacuum systems and identify relevant patterns and insights
 Develop and improve analytics solutions using Python, SQL and Databricks
 Create benchmarking and prioritization models to support data-driven decision-making
 Ensure data quality and validate analytical results
 Collaborate closely with engineers and technical experts to understand technical requirements and translate them into analytical approaches
 Document analytical methods, results and recommendations in a clear and structured way
 Your Skills And Experience
 Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering or a related technical field
 First practical experience with programming, data analytics or software projects
 Good knowledge of Python and basic knowledge of SQLStrong interest in Industrial IoT, digital services and data-driven decision-making
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skillsStructured, independent and detail-oriented approach to work
 Good communication skills and a collaborative mindset
 Professional working proficiency in English; German language skills are a plus
 We encourage you to apply even if you don't meet every single requirement. We value diverse experiences and perspectives and are excited to see what you bring to the role.
