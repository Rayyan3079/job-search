 Position : Associate IT Expert – Hardware Supply Chain Management & Logistics
 Location : Munich, Germany
 Type : Full-time/60% Remote
 Project Overview
 We are seeking an Associate IT Expert to support Order Management activities within a Hardware Supply Chain Management & Logistics environment.
The role involves coordinating, processing, and monitoring orders for IT hardware components throughout the entire operational logistics chain. The successful candidate will ensure efficient order execution, accurate documentation, timely coordination with internal teams and external logistics partners, and continuous improvement of order management processes.
 Tasks And Responsibilities
 Create, process, monitor, and track orders for IT hardware components.
 Plan and coordinate incoming orders on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis.
 Manage and coordinate logistical activities across the entire operational supply chain.
 Ensure accurate processing and tracking of transport orders and material movements.
 Maintain and archive order-related documentation for IT hardware rollouts, rollbacks, and relocations.
 Prepare operational reports on order volumes, processing status, and order fulfillment.
 Monitor order progress, identify discrepancies, and support timely problem resolution.
 Coordinate with internal departments, customers, and external logistics partners via telephone and other communication channels.
 Handle logistics-related inquiries, complaints, and order discrepancies.
 Perform plausibility checks and support the resolution of logistical issues.
 Support warehouse inventory counts, stock reconciliations, and clarification of inventory discrepancies.
 Identify and recommend continuous improvement measures for order management processes, tools, and workflows.
 Ensure accurate data maintenance and compliance with established operational procedures.
