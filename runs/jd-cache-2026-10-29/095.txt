 Main Responsibilities 
 Design, develop and maintain the Data Warehouse architecture;
 develop data loading flows from source databases to the DWH;
 interact with the different internal product areas to gather and analyze requirements;
 develop Business Intelligence solutions within a Microsoft environment;
 develop and optimize queries and procedures using SQL/T-SQL;

 Requirements 
 Knowledge of Microsoft SQL Server, preferably from version 2016 onwards, and of the T-SQL language;
 experience in developing and optimizing queries and procedures, with knowledge of Performance Tuning techniques;
 knowledge of SSIS, SSAS and SSRS;
 knowledge of Power BI, Tabular Models, MDX and DAX;
 knowledge of Python.
