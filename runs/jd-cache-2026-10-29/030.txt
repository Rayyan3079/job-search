Your mission
 As a Junior CRM Consultant Working Student, you will support the delivery of HubSpot-powered CRM solutions while gaining hands-on experience in client projects, CRM implementation, and digital transformation. Working alongside experienced consultants and technical specialists, you’ll learn how businesses use CRM platforms to improve marketing, sales, and customer service operations.
 This role is ideal for a curious and motivated student looking to develop practical consulting and CRM skills in a collaborative, fast-paced agency environment.
 The Role 
 As part of the CRM team, you will contribute to both client delivery and internal project support while building your technical and consulting capabilities.
 What You’ll Be Doing
 CRM Delivery & Solution Support
 Support consultants during client discovery sessions by taking notes, documenting business requirements, and identifying key challenges
 Assist with the creation of dashboards, reports, presentations, and basic CRM workflows
 Help configure CRM properties, forms, and simple automations within HubSpot
 Participate in testing and quality assurance activities for CRM implementations
 Observe and support data mapping and integration planning sessions
 Work alongside Project Managers and Consultants to understand project delivery processes and timelines
 HubSpot & Platform Learning
 Develop foundational knowledge of HubSpot CRM and related tools
 Complete HubSpot Academy certifications as part of your onboarding and development journey
 Learn how CRM systems support marketing, sales, and customer success processes
 Support the identification of underused CRM features and opportunities for optimisation
 Stay up to date with new HubSpot features and CRM best practices
 Collaboration & Client Experience
 Support the team with onboarding coordination, documentation, and client enablement materials
 Assist in preparing training notes, guides, or internal knowledge-sharing content
 Collaborate closely with consultants, specialists, and project teams across different client engagements
 Contribute ideas, ask questions, and actively participate in team learning initiatives
 Support retrospective and improvement discussions by gathering project insights and feedback
 AI & Innovation
 Explore how AI tools can support CRM workflows, documentation, and operational efficiency
 Use AI responsibly to improve productivity and learning outcomes
 Stay curious about emerging technologies and digital trends
 The Application Process
 Apply providing your CV, a covering letter explaining why you are the right fit for the role and your personality type summary from 16personalities.com 
 Screening we will review all applications and contact you to advise if your application has been shortlisted
 Meet with HR we'll schedule a 30 minute call to get to know you; talk about culture, working location, and see if we are a mutually good match.
 Technical interview with hiring manager and another member of the leadership team
 Offer to the right candidate
 Please note: Avidly is a remote-first business and so all interviews will be held via Google Meet. Should you live close to one of our offices then it could be possible to schedule an onsite, in-person meeting (dependant on location of hiring manager).
 Your profile
 This role is for a Working Student setup. The successful candidate will need to be based in Germany and be fluent in German & English (both written and spoken). 
 What We’re Looking For
 Currently enrolled at a German university (Bachelor’s or Master’s programme)
 Available to work approximately 20 hours per week during term time
 Strong interest in CRM systems, digital transformation, consulting, or marketing technology
 Excellent communication and organisational skills
 Analytical mindset with strong attention to detail
 Proactive, eager to learn, and comfortable asking questions
 Previous exposure to HubSpot, CRM platforms, or digital tools is advantageous but not required
 What You’ll Gain
 Hands-on experience working on real client CRM projects
 Structured learning and mentoring from experienced CRM consultants
 Exposure to HubSpot implementation and consultancy practices
 Opportunity to build certifications and practical consulting skills
 Flexible working arrangements compatible with university studies
 A collaborative and supportive team environment focused on growth and development
 Why us?
 At Avidly, you will join an international company where HR plays an important role in supporting growth, culture, and employee experience. This role offers hands-on exposure to a broad HR scope and is ideal for someone who wants to grow into a strong HR operations professional.
 You will have the opportunity to learn from experienced colleagues, build practical HR expertise, and contribute to meaningful people work across multiple countries.
