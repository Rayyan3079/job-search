 Tasks
 Your tasks?
 As a working student (m/f/d/x), you will support our team in day-to-day operations, particularly in preparing quotations, project controlling, and billing. This will give you comprehensive insights into our operational processes.
 You will actively contribute to cross-functional financial processes, such as monthly and annual closings, internal allocations, and calculations.
 In this role, you will make a significant contribution to optimizing our processes by supporting strategic and conceptual projects aimed at transforming finance and controlling processes.
 You will support the digitalization of processes in Finance and Controlling and assist in preparing analyses and evaluations of the financial performance of projects, as well as presentations and decision-making materials for management.
 In addition, you will support the further development of controlling tools and methods and contribute to achieving optimal results and efficient workflows through your collaboration within the team and across broader initiatives.
 Qualifications
 Your profile?
 Ready to get started?  You are an enrolled student (m/f/d/x) in Business Administration, Economics or a related field, ideally with a focus on Finance, Controlling, Accounting, or a comparable area of study.
 Ideally, you already have basic knowledge and initial practical experience in Finance and Controlling and have gained some experience with ERP systems.
 You are also confident in using MS Office, particularly Excel.
 You have strong analytical skills, an affinity for numbers, an independent and structured way of working, and enthusiasm for transformation topics in the finance area.
 With your high level of motivation, willingness to learn, initiative, sense of responsibility, and strong communication skills, you are a great fit for our team.
 You are keen to work regularly on-site in Stuttgart with your colleagues.
 Good written and spoken German and English skills complete your profile.
