Job Requirements
 Do you want to help create the future of healthcare? Our name, Siemens Healthineers, was selected to honor our people who dedicate their energy and passion to this cause. It reflects their pioneering spirit combined with our long history of engineering in the ever-evolving healthcare industry.
We offer you a flexible and dynamic environment with opportunities to go beyond your comfort zone in order to grow personally and professionally. Sounds interesting?
 Then come and join our global team as Working Student (f/m/d) to support us in the field of Agentic AI Tool Engineering.
 Choose the best place for your work – Within the scope of this position, it is possible, in consultation with your manager, to work mobile (within Germany) up to an average volume of 60% of the respective working hours.
This position is part-time with 15h/week.
Job location: Hartmannstr. 16, 91052 Erlangen
 Your Tasks And Responsibilities
 You will work with us to develop AI agents and AI-based tools which support the development of interoperability features of Siemens Healthineers products
 You support the implementation and maintenance of a specialized agentic engineering harness for the tool development and research the newest agentic software engineering developments and educate the team about the capabilities
 You help us build prototypes using large language models and generative AI technologies
 You collaborate with the team to develop retrieval-augmented generation solutions based on internal knowledge sources and integrate AI services and data sources via APIs
 You support the testing, evaluation, and documentation of AI solutions
 You contribute to the introduction of new AI technologies and development standards and will also work with the team to help manage source code and project documentation using GitHub and comparable platforms
 To find out more about the specific business, have a look at: https://www.healthcare.siemens.de/products-services.*
 Your Qualifications And Experience
 You are currently pursuing a university degree in Medical Informatics, Bioinformatics, Computer Science, or a comparable qualification relevant to the responsibilities outlined above
 You have experience with AI coding agents and with GitHub, preferably GitHub Copilot with VS Code
 You have worked with agentic software engineering frameworks like OpenSpec, Spec Kit, or BMAD
 You have good knowledge with developing AI agents, AI-based applications, or machine learning solutions, as well as a basic understanding of large language models, embeddings, and vector databases
 Ideally, you are familiar with retrieval-augmented generation and semantic search
 You are skilled in Python, SQL, or comparable programming languages, software architecture concepts, and cyber security risks of AI agents is an advantage
 A basic understanding of APIs, JSON, healthcare interoperability standards like IHE, DICOM, FHIR, and the integration of different systems and data sources is an advantage
 Your Attributes And Skills
 You have very good English skills and ideally good German proficierncy
 Teamwork is one of your strengths, you can drive projects forward independently, and you have a structured, analytical way of thinking
 You work carefully, have high standards for the quality of your results and are a fast learner 
 Are you curios for exploring the agentic software engineering landscape? Then we look forward to your application!
