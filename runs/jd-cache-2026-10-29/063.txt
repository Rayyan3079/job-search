Job Requirements
 Do you want to help create the future of healthcare? Our name, Siemens Healthineers, was selected to honor our people who dedicate their energy and passion to this cause. It reflects their pioneering spirit combined with our long history of engineering in the ever-evolving healthcare industry.
We offer you a flexible and dynamic environment with opportunities to go beyond your comfort zone in order to grow personally and professionally. Sounds interesting?
 Then come and join our global team as Working Student (f/m/d) to support us in the field of AI agents for automated test case generation.
 This position is part-time with 20h/week.
Job location: Roentgenstr. 19-21, 95478 Kemnath
 Your Tasks And Responsibilities
 You support the development and further improvement of an AI agent for the automated generation of test cases from requirements and relevant system data
 You help with the analysis and evaluation of innovative technologies as well as conducting feasibility studies and proofs of concept
 You assist with connecting and integrating ALM/PLM tools for requirements and test management
 You help in building Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions as well as using ontologies and knowledge graphs
 You contribute to the development of prototypes and near-production solutions in the field of generative AI and large language models
 You support the preparation, structuring, and quality assurance of requirements and test cases, as well as documenting results and presenting solution approaches and prototypes
 There’s also the possibility to work on this topic as part of a master’s thesis
 To find out more about the specific business, have a look at: https://www.healthcare.siemens.de/products-services
 Your Qualifications And Experience
 You are currently studying a technical subject, preferably computer science, business informatics, electrical engineering, or a similar field
 Some practical experience in software development, ideally with Python, is important
 You are very interested in the field of Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and Large Language Models 
 Ideally, you already have knowledge of AI frameworks like LangChain or similar frameworks and experience with ALM/PLM systems as well as requirements and test management
 Knowledge of RAG, vector databases, ontologies, or knowledge graphs is a plus
 Experience with Docker, REST APIs, Git, SQL, and cloud technologies like Microsoft Azure is a plus too
 Your Attributes And Skills
 You have very good German and good English skills, both spoken and written
 You enjoy trying out new technologies and developing innovative solutions and quickly get up to speed on new topics
 Teamwork is one of your strengths, you can drive projects forward independently, and you have a structured, analytical way of thinking
 You work carefully and have high standards for the quality of your results
 Do you have up to 20 hours per week available and ideally want to support us long-term? Then we look forward to your application!
