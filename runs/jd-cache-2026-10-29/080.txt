 About the Position:
 We’re looking for a Database Developer with strong Oracle SQL and PL/SQL development skills to join our software and technology team.
In this role, you will develop, enhance and maintain database driven applications and integrations that support our business systems and operational processes.
You will work alongside software developers and business stakeholders to translate requirements into reliable technical solutions, troubleshoot complex issues, improve database performance and support the ongoing evolution of our applications. This is an opportunity for a hands on developer who enjoys solving problems, working with complex data and building practical solutions that connect databases, applications and business processes.
 Specific Responsibilities:
 Design, develop, test and maintain Oracle SQL queries, PL/SQL packages, stored procedures, functions, triggers and other database objects.
 Build and enhance database driven application functionality, business logic and data processing workflows.
 Develop and maintain database integrations with internal applications, external systems and services, ensuring reliable and accurate data exchange.
 Analyze queries and execution plans, identify bottlenecks, and improve the performance, scalability and maintainability of database solutions.
 Investigate application and data related issues, identify root causes, and implement effective fixes and preventative improvements.
 Implement appropriate validation, error handling and transaction management to ensure data consistency and reliability.
 Work with developers, analysts and business stakeholders to understand requirements, evaluate solutions and deliver enhancements that meet business needs.
 Participate in code reviews, testing, documentation, version control and release processes to maintain high quality, maintainable code.
 Required Knowledge, Skills & Abilities:
 3 to 5 or more years of professional experience in database development or a closely related software development role.
 Oracle certification in SQL, PL/SQL or database development.
 Strong hands on experience with Oracle SQL and PL/SQL, including the development and maintenance of complex queries, stored procedures, functions and packages.
 Solid understanding of relational databases, data modelling concepts, transactions, indexing and query optimization.
 Experience developing and maintaining application functionality that interacts with relational databases.
 Experience integrating databases with applications, APIs, services or other data systems.
 Demonstrated ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues and develop effective, maintainable solutions.
 Familiarity with software development fundamentals, including source control, testing, debugging and technical documentation.
 Strong analytical and problem solving skills, with the ability to manage priorities and work collaboratively.
 Effective written and verbal communication skills.
 Required Qualifications:
 Scripting or automation using Python, PowerShell or similar languages.
 Automated deployments, CI/CD pipelines or database change management.
 Supporting enterprise business applications or data intensive operational systems.
