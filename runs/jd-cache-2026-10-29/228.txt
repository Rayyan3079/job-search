 This is your new job:
 Data analysis on product final test yield
 Automation of data crunching on final test yield from AWACS and EXTENSIO platforms covering Far East assembly sites production line data
 Yield estimation on wafer level based on historical data and correlation of typical MOSFETs test parameters
 Support test specification update and test program release
 Data crunching on financial figures (e.g. wafer costs, die free package cost) of released products and future developments
 This is you:
 Student in electrical engineering or physics or similar with software programming skills.
 Basic Knowledge of semiconductor/MOSFET technology is a plus.
Fluent german or english speaker.
