NTT DATA is a trusted global innovator of business and technology services, helping clients innovate, optimize, and transform for success.  Deine Mission
 Key Responsibilities 
 Lead strategic AI pursuits from opportunity development through negotiation and closure.
 Create deal plans, win strategies, stakeholder maps, competitive positioning, business cases, and close plans.
 Lead client discussions on AI-enabled value creation, automation, productivity, and measurable outcomes.
 Structure outcome-based, value-sharing, gainshare, risk-sharing, and MVP-to-scale commercial models.
 Coordinate multidisciplinary pursuit teams and ensure alignment across solution, delivery, pricing, governance, and risk.
 Build trusted relationships with C-level executives and senior business, technology, finance, procurement, and operations stakeholders.
 Collaborate with technology partners to strengthen propositions and routes to market.
 Mentor junior sellers and develop reusable pursuit and commercial assets.
 Ensure responsible AI, security, privacy, governance, compliance, and human oversight are embedded in opportunities.
 Success Measures 
 Closed-won AI deals exceeding $25 million in total contract value.
 Win rate and qualified AI pipeline generated or influenced.
 Successful value-based, outcome-based, or risk-sharing commercial models.
 Strong pursuit quality, executive alignment, forecast accuracy, margin performance, and team orchestration.
 Basic Qualifications 
 10+ years in enterprise, large-deal, strategic account, consulting, professional services, or technology transformation sales.
 5+ years selling or shaping AI, data, analytics, cloud, automation, managed services, or digital transformation.
 3+ years leading complex, multi-stakeholder pursuits, ideally exceeding $25 million.
 Strong knowledge of enterprise AI, Generative AI, AI agents, data readiness, adoption, responsible AI, and business value.
 Experience with business cases, pricing, negotiations, and value- or outcome-based commercial models.
 Proven C-level engagement and strong commercial acumen across finance, legal, procurement, contracting, and delivery.
 Fluency in German and English.
 Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
 Preferred Skills 
 Experience with sales governance, price-to-win, proposals, and executive deal reviews.
 Relevant industry experience in financial services, insurance, manufacturing, automotive, healthcare, life sciences, or the public sector.
 Familiarity with leading AI, cloud, data, and technology ecosystem partners.
 Understanding of Generative AI, agentic AI, data platforms, cloud, automation, and AI-enabled managed services.
 Strong executive presence, communication, negotiation, stakeholder management, resilience, and ownership.
 Willingness to travel as required.
 Benefits 
 Flexible working hours with a flexitime arrangement; business travel time is considered working time
 30 days of annual leave, plus December 24 and December 31 as additional non-working days
 The option to take a sabbatical of up to one year
 Remote work and mobile working from other EU countries
 Attractive benefits and compensation optimisation programmes, including an occupational pension scheme, JobRad bike leasing, a public transport pass, exclusive discounts through Corporate Benefits, and much more
