Job Title: Data Analyst
 Experience: 1–2 years
 About the Role 
 We're looking for a Data Analyst to join the data=[lp\-m` team. You'll work closely with stakeholders across the business to turn data into actionable insights — writing SQL, building dashboards, and helping teams make data-informed decisions. This is a great role for someone early in their career who wants to build strong analytical fundamentals.
 Responsibilities 
 Write SQL queries and build data models that power dashboards used across the organization
 Design and maintain reports and dashboards using BI tools (Tableau, Data Studio, or Power BI)
 Optimize slow-running or high-compute queries for better performance and cost efficiency
 Perform data analysis in collaboration with different stakeholders
 Meet with stakeholders across geographies to identify problems that can be solved with data and gather requirements
 Ensure data accuracy and consistency across reports and dashboards
 Document report logic and data definitions for team reference
 Requirements 
 Non-Negotiable (Must-Have — disqualify if absent) 
 Strong SQL skills — must be able to write and reason through joins, aggregations, window functions unaided (this is core to the role, not learnable on the job in this timeframe)
 Strong communication skills — will be speaking directly with technical and non-technical stakeholders across different countries; no room to compromise here, since this is often where junior analysts fail even if technically capable
 Requirement-gathering ability — comfortable asking clarifying questions and translating a vague business ask into a data problem
 Negotiable (Nice-to-Have — don't disqualify, use for tie-breaking) 
 Ability to optimize slow/high-compute queries — a strong plus signal (shows they understand data structures, not just syntax) but not a baseline expectation for 1–2 years experience
 Hands-on BI tool experience (Tableau, Data Studio, or Power BI — any one is fine, but must have built real dashboards, not just "familiar with")
 Python skills — good to have, but can be picked up on the job if SQL and analytical thinking are strong
 Which specific BI tool — proficiency in one is enough; don't require multi-tool experience
 Cloud data warehouse exposure (BigQuery, Redshift, Snowflake) — a plus, not required
 Exact years of experience — 1–2 is a guideline; a strong 8-month candidate with sharp fundamentals shouldn't be auto-rejected, and someone at 3 years shouldn't be disqualified either
 Knowledge about AI and experience is a plus. Personal projects with AI agents are encouraged.
