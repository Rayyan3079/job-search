Motivation of the Work 
 Turning today’s research into tomorrow’s applications, together. At ZEISS, we focus on user-centric innovation to transform ideas into cutting-edge solutions. The ZEISS Innovation Hub @ KIT brings together students, researchers, and industry professionals to advance AI, computer vision, and machine learning in application-oriented projects.
 Your Role
 We are looking for highly motivated students to develop AI-based solutions for real-world vision tasks, including agricultural solutions, from data collection, simulation-based data generation, and model development to deployment on target devices. This internship provides hands-on experience across the full ML pipeline, including vision foundation models, data preprocessing and curation, quantization-aware training, edge-device deployment, and performance evaluation.
 Develop an efficient and reproducible workflow for application-oriented machine learning solutions. 
 Be involved in sample preparation and data acquisition in the laboratory for full data ownership 
 Implement quantitative metrics to assess and optimize data preprocessing quality and guide relabeling efforts 
 Curate, structure, and version datasets for reproducible experiments 
 Build data preprocessing pipelines for AI and machine learning workflows 
 Generate and evaluate simulation-based or synthetic data 
 Run hyperparameter and model studies in a traceable environment. 
 Automate embedded-device tests and optimize accuracy and speed. 
 We Offer
 A dynamic and interdisciplinary environment 
 Hands-on experience with real-world image, sensor, and simulation-based data, foundation models, model quantization, and edge deployment 
 Learn how to set up a full machine learning pipeline 
 Close mentorship from experts in deep learning 
 An agile work environment 
 Your Profile
 Enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in computer science, mathematics, physics, AI, or related fields
 Strong programming skills in Python and PyTorch
 Solid understanding of software development principles
 Fundamental knowledge of data preprocessing steps for computer vision and vision foundation models
 Familiarity with AI/ML concepts (e.g., supervised learning, deep learning architectures) is a plus
 Experience with edge-device deployment and model quantization is a plus
 Creative, pragmatic, and self-motivated with strong analytical skills
 Capable of working independently as well as in a team-oriented environment 
 Excellent communication skills in English or German
 Passion for innovation, enthusiasm for new technologies, and motivation to contribute to interdisciplinary projects
