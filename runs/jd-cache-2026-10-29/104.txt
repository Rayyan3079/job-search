At Stark, we forge the future of European and national security through cutting-edge, AI-driven technology and unmatched engineering precision. As a premier defence technology company, our mission is clear: to equip NATO Allies and their partners with next-generation autonomous defence platforms and advanced AI-driven software built to perform in the world’s most demanding environments.
We believe that true security stems from relentless innovation. By combining elite engineering talent with agile technology and an AI-native operational culture, Stark turns complex defence challenges into field-tested, multi-domain solutions.
 WHY JOIN US
 The software and systems you work on directly protect personnel and defend European and national sovereignty.
 You will work alongside industry-leading minds on autonomous systems, advanced propulsion, and secure cyber platforms.
 We foster a fast-paced, collaborative environment where bold ideas are supported by serious R&D backing.
 About The Team
 STARK's Electronic Warfare team is a systems-focussed engineering group delivering software-defined, mass-scalable RF solutions for autonomous systems. Combining deep domain expertise in hardware board design, thermal/ structural packaging, real-time C++ signal processing, and resilient directional mesh networking, we rapidly build field advanced EW capabilities designed to survive and operate in contested spectrum environments.
 YOUR MISSION
 You will support the design, development, integration, and testing of advanced Electronic Warfare capabilities on STARK’s internal EW platforms, contributing to performance across complex and highly dynamic RF environments.
 What You Will Do At Stark
 Support development and integration of electronic and RF systems enabling Electronic Warfare capabilities
 Assist in designing and implementing hardware setups related to RF sensing and signal acquisition
 Support System integration tasks through mechanical design, and cable harness development
 Support testing, debugging, and validation of electronic systems to ensure stability and performance
 What You Bring To The Mission
 Strong engineering fundamentals at a conceptual and implementation level
 Basic understanding of electronics, circuits, and RF systems
 Strong interest in physics, RF systems, or signal behaviour
 Understanding of signals, systems, or electromagnetic fundamentals
 Experience In 3D printing and CAD tools
 Clear and structured communication skills for cross-functional collaboration
 NICE TO HAVE
 Electronic, Telecommunications or Mechatronics Engineering or related field, ideally with hands-on hardware experience
 RF spectrum, communications, or physics-related coursework
 Signal processing or mathematically driven system understanding
 Embedded systems or hardware-software integration exposure
