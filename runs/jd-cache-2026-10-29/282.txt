Who We Are
 BTT Health manufactures medical devices for bone health. It produces the Marodyne LiV device – a leading clinically validated low-intensity vibration device for bone density, muscle function and mobility; a mega market for osteoporosis patients which represents one of the TOP 10 diseases in the western world according to the WHO. Moreover, it manufactures and distributes Bone4ce/Melmak for bone regeneration, both with distribution partners in the US, Canada, Australia and Central Europe.
 In summer 2026, an investor group acquired the company. Now we're rebuilding BTT Health as a start-up with proven products: a sharper strategy, new markets and a broader portfolio. You'll work directly with our Head of Growth, one of the investors – and since we use AI to move fast as a small team, that's exactly where you come in.
 What You'll Do
 AI-Powered Workflows & Automation
 ● Spot the potential – find tasks in marketing, sales and admin that AI chatbots and agents can do faster and better
 ● Build it – set up AI workflows and automations (e.g. Power Automate, Zapier, HubSpot), from prototype to daily use
 ● Digitize sales – help shape our CRM, sales reporting and dashboards
 ● Enable the team – create prompt templates, quick guides and mini-trainings
 Sales & Business Development
 ● Partner support – help manage our distribution partners in the US, Canada, Australia and Central Europe
 ● New markets – research and qualify potential new distribution partners and markets
 ● Portfolio scouting – evaluate new products and technologies, from first research to a decision paper for management
 ● Deal prep – presentations, quotes, analyses and partner meetings
 Marketing & Market Analysis (strategic focus)
 ● Campaigns & content – for our website, LinkedIn, newsletters and professional audiences – AI-assisted from idea to launch
 ● Market intelligence – research markets, competitors, trends and growth opportunities
 ● Science to story – turn clinical study data into clear content for partners, clinicians and patients
 What You Bring
 ● Studies – Bachelor's or Master's student in business, industrial engineering, medtech, life sciences, informatics, healthcare management or similar
 ● Curiosity – real interest in medtech, medicine and technology – and in the business side
 ● AI-native – you use ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot & co. daily; Power Automate, Zapier or Python is a plus
 ● Structured thinking – analytical, well organized and confident in Excel and PowerPoint (old school, we know, but we use it and need it)
 ● Languages – fluent in English, German is a big plus but no requirement
 ● Start-up energy – hands-on, full of ideas, at home in a fast-paced start-up
 ● Availability – up to 20 hours/week (more during semester breaks) for min. 6 months
 What We Offer
 ● €1,500 gross per month – based on 20 hours/week, adjusted pro rata for fewer hours
 ● Start-up spirit with substance – help rebuild a medtech company with established products and partners
 ● Real ownership – your own projects from day one that shape the rebuild
 ● Direct line to the top – work with our Head of Growth; flat hierarchies, fast decisions
 ● AI playground – test new tools and put your ideas into practice right away
 ● Hybrid & flexible – hours around your lectures; Kissing is 30 min by direct train from Munich Hbf
 ● Real impact – products for bone health (Marodyne LiV vibration therapy, LIPUS) and the option to write your thesis with us
 How to Apply
 Ready to help us rebuild BTT Health? Just send your CV – that's all we need. A short motivation letter is welcome but optional. Please mention your earliest start date. Bonus: tell us in two or three sentences one skill that would definitely help you to excel at this job (e.g., medtech knowledge, technical skills..). Humans will read your application; we do not use AI in the hiring process.
