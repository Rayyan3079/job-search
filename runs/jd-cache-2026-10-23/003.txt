Your role
Key responsibilities in your new role
 Focus on the future: Support data analysis and reporting activities by collecting, cleaning, and analyzing datasets using Excel, SQL, and visualization tools such as Power BI or Tableau

 Keep up to date: Conduct research on AI trends and technologies and support the team in identifying opportunities to improve processes, analytics, and AI-driven solutions
 Your profile
 Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed
 Study Field: Currently enrolled in a degree with focus on Computer Science, Natural Sciences/Engineering, or a related field
 Skills: Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Office tools (especially Power Point, Excel); proficiency in data analysis and visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar software
 Experience: Experience with data manipulation and statistical analysis using tools like Excel and SQL
 Way of working: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills as well as strong communication and presentation skills

 Important information:
 Working part-time: The focus is on studies. That’s why working as a student employee during lecture period is limited to a maximum of 20 hours per week.
