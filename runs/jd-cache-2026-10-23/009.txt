Job Description
 What you bring 
 Availability: Able to work at least two hours per business day alongside the agreed weekly schedule. 
 Mindset: Strong initiative, proactive and goal-oriented working style, with affinity for structured work. 
 Enterprise Architecture: Understanding of EA frameworks such as TOGAF and architecture modelling. 
 LeanIX & Jira: Theoretical knowledge required; hands-on experience with either tool is a strong plus. 
 AI & Automation: Experience with AI agents, scripting, or automation pipelines is an advantage. 
 MS Office: Good working knowledge of Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Teams. 
 Languages: Very good English (written and verbal); German is a plus. 
 Education: Enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a related field. 
 What You´ll Build
 We are looking for a working student to join our team part-time (20 hours/week) for at least 12 months. You will support our Enterprise Architecture team in the following areas:
 Support the third-party software approval process as a quality gate, ensuring submissions meet required standards. 
 Maintain Enterprise Architecture data in LeanIX, collaborating with architects to ensure data quality and consistency. 
 Identify and resolve data inconsistencies across LeanIX and Jira to improve reliability of architecture information. 
 Develop proof-of-concept scripts and leverage AI skills and agents to automate the daily work of enterprise architects. 
 What You Can Expect
 Work closely with experienced enterprise architects in a warm, collaborative team environment. 
 Gain first-hand experience of the IT organization of a DAX-listed company. 
 Build practical knowledge of LeanIX, TOGAF, and Enterprise Architecture Management. 
 Explore AI applications in business processes: prompt engineering, AI agents, and workflow automation. 
 Where you belong 
 The Corporate Processes & Information Technology (CPIT) organization drives efficiency and streamlined operations. As part of CPIT, the Enterprise Architecture Group shapes the architecture of core IT solutions across SAP's lines-of-business - from Marketing and Sales to Financials and Services by applying advanced Enterprise Architecture methods.
