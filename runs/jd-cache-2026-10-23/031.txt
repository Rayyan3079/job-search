 Aufgaben 
 Your tasks?
 As a Bachelor's Thesis Student – User Feedback & Data Analytics (m/f/d/x), you will support the team in analyzing existing user research findings and evaluating user feedback.
 You will process and analyze existing quantitative user data to identify relevant insights.
 You will also support the AI-assisted analysis of the data, likely using Visual Studio Code.
 You will conduct statistical analyses and identify relevant patterns and correlations within the user data.
 Based on the analysis results, you will identify user needs, usage barriers, and potential areas for improvement.
 In addition, you will develop concrete recommendations for the user-centered further development and present your findings in a way that is tailored to the target audience.
