 Aufgaben 
 Your tasks?
 As a Working Student AI Research Engineer for SLM Optimization (m/f/d/x), you will support the team in developing, refining, and managing caching mechanisms for Small Language Models (SLMs).
 You will contribute to the training and further development of models for cache prediction.
 You will also support the configuration and management of machine learning environments in the cloud.
 You will work on the design and exploratory analysis of new caching strategies while contributing your own ideas.
 You will support the selection, development, and integration of the latest technologies as part of innovative research projects.
 In addition, you will collaborate with experienced developers from various disciplines in an agile product development team.
