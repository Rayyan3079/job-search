The Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS) conducts research to advance intelligent systems that perceive, learn, and interact. Through fundamental research across computational, physical, and social dimensions, we shape artificial intelligence and robotics to advance knowledge and benefit society.
We are looking for a
Student Assistant (HiWi) (f/m/div) for AI-Assisted 3D Segmentation of Photonic Nanostructures
 About Us
 We are seeking a motivated student research assistant to contribute to an interdisciplinary research project investigating the mechanisms of dynamic structural coloration in blue shark (Prionace glauca) skin and translating the underlying biological principles into adaptive biomimetic photonic systems.
The project combines fundamental research on biological photonic architectures with the development of a denticle-inspired platform comprising modular optical units capable of changing color in response to environmental stimuli. Understanding how the spatial arrangement of light-reflecting guanine crystals governs optical output is central to both research directions.
To investigate this relationship, we have acquired three-dimensional cryogenic focused ion beam (Cryo-FIB) microscopy datasets of shark dermal denticles maintained in three distinct color states: blue, green and orange. Computational simulations predict that increasing the spacing between guanine crystals shifts the reflected light towards longer wavelengths.
The student will contribute to the 3D segmentation of individual light reflective crystals, providing essential structural data to test this hypothesis and guide the development of bio-inspired, color-responsive photonic systems.

Roles & Responsibilities
The position offers hands-on experience in scientific image processing, AI-assisted segmentation and three-dimensional analysis of biological nanostructures.
Possible tasks include:
 Performing 3D segmentation of individual guanine crystals based on Cryo-FIB microscopy datasets using Amira software. 
 Applying and optimizing machine-learning-based, automated and semi-automated segmentation workflows. 
 Refining segmentation results manually where necessary to ensure accurate separation of individual crystals. 
 Ensuring a consistent segmentation approach across datasets representing the three color states (blue, green and orange). 
 Preparing segmented 3D datasets for quantitative analysis of crystal spacing and spatial organization, and for computational modelling of their optical response across different color states. 
 Documenting image-processing workflows and organizing datasets for reproducible analysis. 
 Training and guidance in Amira and the relevant segmentation methods will be provided!
