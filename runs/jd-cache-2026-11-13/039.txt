About The Role
 What if your everyday skills could help build the AI tools that millions of people rely on? We're looking for AI Training Specialists to complete structured tasks that teach AI systems to think more clearly, respond more accurately, and behave more reliably.
This is a fully remote, flexible contract role open to motivated individuals from any background. No prior AI or technical experience required — just sharp attention to detail and a willingness to follow clear guidelines.
 Organization: Alignerr
 Type: Hourly Contract
 Location: Remote
 Commitment: 10–40 hours/week
 What You'll Do
 Complete structured labeling, rating, and evaluation tasks that directly improve AI model performance
 Assess AI-generated content across text, image, and audio formats for quality, accuracy, and consistency
 Follow detailed rubrics and guidelines to ensure your evaluations meet project standards
 Flag errors, inconsistencies, or low-quality outputs using structured feedback tools
 Work independently and asynchronously — on your schedule, at your pace
 Who You Are
 Detail-oriented with a strong ability to follow instructions precisely and consistently
 Self-motivated and comfortable managing your own workload without supervision
 Confident using web-based tools and platforms
 Clear and effective written communicator in English
 Open to feedback and committed to maintaining high accuracy standards
 No prior AI, data, or technical experience required
 Nice to Have
 Experience in content moderation, data entry, research, or quality assurance
 Familiarity with AI tools or platforms as an end user
 Background in writing, editing, or critical analysis
 Why Join Us
 Work on cutting-edge AI projects in collaboration with leading research labs
 Fully remote and flexible — work from anywhere, on your own schedule
 Freelance autonomy with the structure of meaningful, task-based work
 Contribute to AI development that has a real, tangible impact on the world
 Potential for ongoing work and contract extension as new projects launch
 Join a global community of contributors helping to shape the next generation of AI
