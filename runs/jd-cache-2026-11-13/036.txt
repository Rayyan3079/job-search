Your role
Key responsibilities in your new role
 Automation: You develop Python-based automation, utilities, and data-processing solutions for chip design and integration workflows 
 Software Development: You design and enhance scalable software workflows, interfaces, and integrations between engineering tools and IT systems 
 AI & Innovation: You explore and prototype AI/ML and generative AI use cases, including log analysis, anomaly detection, knowledge retrieval, workflow assistance, and productivity automation 
 CI/CD: You build reliable CI/CD pipelines, automated testing frameworks, monitoring solutions, and technical documentation for internally developed tools 
 Data Analysis: You analyze workflow data and performance bottlenecks and translate findings into practical software improvements 
 Collaboration: You work closely with software, IT, and semiconductor-domain experts in an international engineering environment
 Your profile
 Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed
 Academic Background: You are currently studying Computer Science, Informatics, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a comparable software-focused field 
 Programming Skills: You demonstrate strong programming fundamentals and practical experience with Python 
 Software Engineering: You understand core software engineering concepts such as modular design, APIs, testing, debugging, data structures, and algorithms 
 Operating Systems: You work confidently in Linux/UNIX environments and use Git for collaborative software development 
 Problem Solving: You enjoy solving unfamiliar technical challenges, learning complex application domains, and translating user needs into maintainable software solutions 
 AI Interest: You have a strong interest in AI/ML, generative AI, data engineering, or intelligent automation, with hands-on project experience considered an advantage 
 Tools & Technologies: You bring knowledge of additional programming languages such as Java, C++, JavaScript/TypeScript, or Go and have basic experience with CI/CD tools and technologies such as GitLab CI, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or Docker 
 Languages: You communicate fluently in English, German language skills are a plus
 Please attach the following documents to your application:
 CV in English
 Certificate of enrollment at university
 Latest grades transcript (not older than 6 months)
 High school report
 Important information:
 Working part-time: The focus is on studies. That’s why working as a student employee during lecture period is limited to a maximum of 20 hours per week. 
 Proper students (according to the German law) are welcome: To work as a student employee with us, you must be enrolled at a university and not have completed all of your exams or modules for your degree programme. You must not be in a semester of leave. We look forward to welcoming you to our team for at least 6 months.
 You should live close to the site: For good collaboration, it is important to us that you can come to the office regularly to integrate to the team.
