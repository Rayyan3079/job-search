🚀 WE'RE HIRING | AI Data Annotation Specialist – Text & Audio 
 📍 Location: Dearborn, MI
 🏢 Work Mode: Hybrid
 🎓 Education: Bachelor's Degree Required
 Job Overview: 
 We are looking for an AI Data Annotation Specialist with experience in text and audio annotation to evaluate and improve the quality of conversational AI responses.
 The ideal candidate should have strong analytical skills, excellent English communication, and the ability to review chatbot conversations, identify response errors, and apply annotation guidelines consistently.
 Key Responsibilities: 
 Review chatbot conversations, AI-generated responses, retrieved content, and citations.
 Perform text and audio data annotation and labeling.
 Evaluate AI responses for accuracy, relevance, completeness, and correctness.
 Identify factual errors, unsupported claims, ambiguous responses, and policy concerns.
 Apply annotation guidelines, taxonomies, and quality standards.
 Document annotation decisions, rationales, and confidence assessments.
 Participate in quality reviews, calibration sessions, and training.
 Prepare weekly reports covering productivity, quality, and annotation progress.
 Recommend improvements to annotation guidelines and processes.
 Required Qualifications: 
 Previous experience in conversational AI data annotation (Text & Audio).
 Excellent English reading comprehension and written communication skills.
 Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
 Ability to identify factual errors and evaluate AI-generated responses.
 Experience following detailed annotation guidelines.
 Strong documentation and reporting skills.
 Bachelor's degree required.
 Preferred Qualifications: 
 Previous automotive industry experience.
 Experience working with organizations such as Amazon, Google, Apple, or Scale AI.
 Master's degree preferred.
