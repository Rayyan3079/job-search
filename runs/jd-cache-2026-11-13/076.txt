Cabka develops clever and transformative solutions for shipping goods around the world. We take plastic waste and transform it into reusable transport packaging. This is how we turn the used into the useful. Transformation is our power because we know that while resources are limited, possibilities are endless. Together with our customers, we transform entire supply chains and beyond, combining technology, innovation, and sustainability. To us, success means creating solutions that are optimal for our clients and for the planet. In Cabka we believe that every transformation starts with a group of passionate, dedicated, and innovative people. It is for them that we live a culture of inclusion, respect and trust that is strongly aligned with our values RESPECT, PASSION, CREATIVITY, ACCOUNTABILITY.
 Turning sustainability ambitions into measurable progress starts with the right data. As our Working Student Sustainability Reporting, you will help us turn data into transparency and support the progress of our sustainability initiatives. 
 Working Student Sustainability Reporting (f/m/d)
 based in Berlin
 Your responsibilities
 Sustainability Reporting: Support regular internal and external reporting on sustainability KPIs and contribute to the preparation of sustainability-related information.
 Data Collection & Analysis: Collect and analyze data for sustainability programs and support ad-hoc analyses of sustainability developments relevant to Cabka.
 Sustainability Systems: Maintain systems used to measure and track the progress of sustainability initiatives.
 Cross-Functional Support: Provide sustainability-related information to colleagues across different entities and support collaboration on sustainability topics.
 Administrative Support: Take on administrative tasks related to the team's sustainability activities.
 Your profile
 Studies: Currently enrolled at a German university, ideally in Business or Public Administration, Environmental Planning, Engineering or a related field, preferably at Master’s level with a completed Bachelor’s degree.
 Sustainability Knowledge: High interest in sustainability reporting in an international corporate environment; previous experience with ESRS, sustainability reporting, data analysis or regulatory publications is beneficial.
 Analytical & Project Skills: Advanced analytical skills, an outcome-oriented approach and good project management capabilities.Working Style: Independent, self-motivated and structured, with the ability to work both independently and as part of a team in a fast-paced environment.
 Communication & Collaboration: Strong communication skills, persistence and intercultural competence.
 Languages: Confident communication skills in both German and English; additional languages are a plus.
 Systems: Proficient in MS 365, particularly Excel and PowerPoint.
 What we offer
 Purpose & Impact: Get involved in a variety of sustainability projects and work on topics that connect sustainability with our business.
 Learning & Development: Take responsibility from the start, develop your skills and gain valuable practical experience alongside your studies.
 International Team: Work in a multicultural team and collaborate with colleagues across different functions and countries.
 Room for Ideas: Bring your own perspective and ideas and help shape how we approach sustainability topics.
 Modern Workplace: Enjoy our modern office right on Kurfürstendamm in the heart of Berlin, including our own barista.
 Benefits: Attractive compensation, company events and fresh fruit in the office.
