 YOUR NEW CAREER
 For our headquarters in Böblingen we are searching a working student (m/f/d). You become part of a global team developing distributed fiber optic sensing solutions that help optimize critical infrastructure, protect people, and safeguard the environment. Using fiber optic cables as continuous sensors, we combine advanced photonics, measurement technology, and modern software to deliver reliable real-time insights along pipelines, power cables, railways, and other assets. In this role, you contribute to solutions deployed worldwide, working in a collaborative, cross-disciplinary engineering environment that values technical rigor, long-term quality, and practical innovation.
 Your Responsibilities
 Careful and accurate annotation of complex data
 Management and processing of large datasets
 Improvement of annotation workflows (semi-supervised annotation, weak labeling, etc.)
 Your Profile
 Ideally, you bring the following:
 STEM background with knowledge of Machine Learning and Data Science
 Programming skills (Python, Bash, etc.)
 Ability to quickly understand and adapt to complex technical topics
 Ability to thrive in dynamic environments and switch between different tasks and contexts efficiently
 You are willing to work regularly on-site at our headquarters in Böblingen 
 We are looking for someone to start immediately, ideally for 20 hours per week 
 Interested? Then send your application—including the  high school diploma , bachelor’s degree certificate (if already available) and the current transcript or records to hr@apsensing.com .
