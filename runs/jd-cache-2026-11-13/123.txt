ProCredit. We are an international group of development-oriented commercial banks for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSME). We are active in Southeastern and Eastern Europe, South America and Germany. Our overarching goal is to combine a high developmental impact with economic success. This position is based in Frankfurt am Main at ProCredit Holding, the listed parent company of our banking group.
 For our Group Economic Analysis Team, we are seeking a motivated
 Working Student Economic Analysis (m/f/d) 
 Your role in our team: 
 The working student/intern will assist ProCredit Holding’s Economic Analysis team by:
 • Working with datasets, including macroeconomic, microeconomic data and high-frequency financial data
 • Contributing to the development, maintenance, and improvement of the team‘s data infrastructure and econometric models, including the management tools and dashboards.
 • Contributing to the team input into macroeconomic analysis by using data analysis, statistical, and econometric tools to help with empirical and quantitative oriented assessments
 • Updating regular internal reports and presentations, liaising with other departments of ProCredit Holding
 What we expect: 
 • Enrolled in a master’s or advanced bachelor’s degree program in economics, econometrics, finance or in a comparable degree program
 • First relevant experience working in data management, and econometric analysis (time series, panel data)
 • Proven experience with statistical and econometric software packages, such as Python, R, Stata, EViews and/or MATLAB for forecasting model development
 • Sound experience with data visualization tools such as Power BI, Tableau, and/or Python
 • Ability to manage and analyse diverse economic data sources such as macroeconomic, financial market, firm-level, credit, supervisory banking/accounting, high-frequency, panel, and big-data
 • Strong analytical skills and affinity for quantitative analysis
 • Strong command of MS Office products, especially Excel and PowerPoint
 • Fluent written and spoken English – that is our company language across the whole group; German is a plus but not a must
 • Interpersonal skills coupled with the ability and determination to communicate in a sincere and open manner, high degree of proactivity, and capability to work independently
 • A keen interest in Eastern and Southeastern Europe, where ProCredit predominantly operates
 • Ability to work meticulously, both independently and as part of a team
 What you can expect: 
 • An international environment as well as a pleasant and open working atmosphere
 • Constant learning in a sustainability-oriented group with exciting, responsible tasks
 • Flat hierarchies and short decision paths
 • Highly motivated, international teams with very good development opportunities
 • Great location in the Bockenheim district of Frankfurt
 • EGYM Wellpass (membership valid at a number of gyms throughout Germany as well as for online fitness courses)
 • Corporate Benefits program
