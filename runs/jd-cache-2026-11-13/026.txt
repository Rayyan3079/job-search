 What we check for in your CV A degree in business informatics, economics, mathematics, computer science or a related field
 A Power BI, Tableau or Qlik dashboard you built
 SQL: SELECT queries with joins and aggregations
 Excel, including pivot tables
 Not required, but useful DAX or Power Query
 The Microsoft PL-300 certification
 Work with a data warehouse
 Working at Grainset 30 vacation days
 Employer-funded company pension
 Training budget for courses and certifications
