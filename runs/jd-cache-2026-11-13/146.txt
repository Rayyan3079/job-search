About the role: 
 As a Working Student or Intern in Product Management - Air Domain, you will support our
 eight-person Product Management team at Quantum-Systems. Our scope covers Vector, Twister,
 Reliant, Droneport, RAT and cross-product projects.
 You will help the team keep roadmaps up to date, maintain clear product documentation and
 coordinate information across products. Your contribution will increase the team's capacity and help
 product managers focus on priorities and product decisions.
 You will work closely with Programs/Bid and Engineering, as well as Operations and Sales. The role
 is based in Gilching, with an office-based start and up to 20% remote work afterwards.
 What is your Day to Day Mission: 
 Support product managers in updating roadmaps, milestones and product status.
 Create, maintain and structure product documentation so information is accurate and easy to find.
 Gather inputs from product managers and stakeholders, and track open questions and follow-up
 actions.
 Prepare clear summaries, presentations and materials for product discussions.
 Support coordination across Vector, Twister, Reliant, Droneport, RAT and cross-product projects.
 Help improve everyday PM workflows and the consistency of shared information.
 What you bring to the team: 
 You are pursuing studies preferably in Engineering, Business Management or a related field, and bring a technical foundation or understanding.
 You have a strong interest in drone technology and want to learn how technical products are managed.
 You enjoy organizing information and turning complex topics into clear, structured documentation.
 You work reliably, pay attention to detail and follow through on agreed actions.
 You communicate openly, collaborate well across teams and take initiative while asking questions when needed.
 You can communicate effectively in English, both in writing and in conversation. German is a plus.
 Experience with Jira and Confluence is a plus.
 We welcome applications for either a working student position or an internship. Preferred start: as
 soon as possible. Minimum duration: six months. Weekly hours depend on the contract.

 About us: 
 Quantum Systems develops advanced autonomous systems for defense and security operations across multiple domains, powered by integrated software and AI. Quantum Systems is at the forefront of AI-powered aerial, ground, naval robotics, developing a family of smart systems that go far beyond traditional drones. Built on a foundation of in-house hardware, software and AI, our systems transform data into critical insights, empowering decision-making across security, defense and public safety, as well as for the commercial, industrial and GIS sectors. Our vertically integrated system with industry leading endurance, enable seamless interoperability and software interconnectivity. On-board AI powers multi-modality sensing, autonomous navigation, anti-jamming, swarm operations and more.
