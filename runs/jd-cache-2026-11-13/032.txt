Mode of Employment: Fixed Term / max. 20 hours/week
As a Working Student in Strategic Procurement (f/m/d) at our Erlangen location, you’ll dive into the world of monthly reporting, digitalization projects, and data management—ready to shape the future of procurement with us? Join our team and turn your ideas into real impact while gaining hands-on experience in a dynamic environment!
Connect, learn, and grow with our Working Student Network – your space to exchange ideas, build meaningful relationships, and navigate your Siemens journey.
 You'll make an impact by
 You support the monthly procurement reporting process, assisting with raw material and hedging calculations to ensure accurate and timely data delivery
 Building on this, you contribute to digitalization projects by helping to optimize workflows and manage procurement-related data efficiently
 Additionally, you assist the team with various organizational tasks, ensuring smooth operations and effective collaboration across the department
 This Is How You'll Win Us Over
 Education: You are currently enrolled in a relevant degree program such as Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Industrial Engineering or a comparable field
 Experience and Skills:
 You have basic knowledge of procurement processes and data management
 You are proficient in Microsoft Office, especially Excel
 You are interested in digitalization and support organizational tasks effectively
 Ways of Working: You demonstrate a structured, reliable, and proactive working style and enjoy working in a team
 Languages: Very good English skills are required
