We are looking for motivated students in the area of Cloud Applications for internship, thesis (Bachelor/Master) or as working student to support our software development team in Ulm.
You will participate in shaping our digital products we develop for various divisions within the Liebherr Group for which we provide production grade solutions like fleet management, control software for autonomous wheel loaders and apps for smart fridges.
 Your Responsibilities
 Implement software solutions in the area of cloud backend and infrastructure, contributing to various projects within our team
 Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to address challenges and develop innovative solutions
 Be creative and adaptable, ready to tackle different tasks and projects as they arise 
 Embrace agile practices and methodologies, actively participating in team meetings and sprint activities 
 Your Qualifications
 Currently studying Computer Science or a related field 
 First experiences in programming languages such as Java, C# or Go
 Basic knowledge in backend development (APIs, databases, etc.)
 Familiarity with cloud computing concepts and technologies, like MS Azure, AWS, GCP, containerization and Kubernetes 
 Motivated and enthusiastic about working in an agile team environment
 Profound communication skills in both German and English 
 Our offer
 As a student you will be able to gain deep insight into cutting-edge technologies in areas such as cloud development, distributed systems and connected machines/devices.
At the same time, you will enjoy a modern and comforting work environment with enthusiastic, highly supportive and open-minded colleagues in our Ulm office.
You will profit from those benefits:
 Flexible and hybrid working
 Attractive renumeration
 Space for creative working
 Employee benefits and discounts
