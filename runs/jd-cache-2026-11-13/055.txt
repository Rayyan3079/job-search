Who are we?
 SOLEVO is a leading distributor of chemicals and inputs for specific industrial and agricultural segments in Africa. SOLEVO assists African farmers and Industries succeed by providing them with a full range of fertilizers, seeds, crop protection products and industrial chemicals they need with a guaranteed quality and good value. With over 75 years at the frontier of economic growth and transformation across Africa, we deliver critical raw materials and expertise to the continent’s high GDP-contributing life sciences and industrials sectors.
What are we looking for?
We are seeking an experienced and talented Data Analyst to join our data and analytics team based in Belgrade, Serbia.
What do we expect from the candidate?
The ideal candidate will play a key role in transforming data into actionable insights that drive strategic decision-making. You will work closely with business analysts and business stakeholders to understand their reporting needs, build robust semantic models and dashboards , and support the team in implementing AI in BI and data-driven solutions. Your expertise in Power BI, data modelling, and storytelling with data will be crucial
 The Key Responsibilities
 Data Analysis & Reporting: Gather, understand, and translate business requirements into effective data models and visualizations in Power BI.
 Build and maintain Power BI semantic models: DAX, Power Query, relationships, RLS. Keep them fast and readable.
 Team Collaboration: Work together with business analysts teams and business stakeholders, ensuring reports meet business needs and objectives, delivering actionable insights.
 Data Quality & Validation: Ensure accuracy and reliability of data by validating against source systems and proactively identifying data issues.
 Handle day-to-day support and help users get the most out of their reports.
 Documentation & Knowledge Sharing: Create comprehensive documentation for data models, reports, and processes to support knowledge sharing and future development.
 Continuous Improvement: Support data governance, performance optimization, and contribute to the overall improvement of data practices within the organization.
