Job Description
 What you'll bring
 You're a student ready to contribute alongside your studies. You're curious, adapt quickly, and stay resilient as priorities evolve. You think creatively, communicate clearly, and collaborate well across teams. You're building your foundations in business and AI — and you take initiative to apply them. You bring emotional intelligence and cultural awareness to every interaction.
 Hands-on experience with AI tools, automation, or prototyping, or a strong drive to build these skills
 An interest in cloud infrastructure, technical architecture, or enterprise software
 The ability to analyze processes and identify opportunities for improvement
 Regular attendance at the office in Garching is required for this role
 Fluency in German and English is required for this role
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems, whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools, including agentic AI, to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You'll Build
 You'll work on real deliverables, not shadow tasks. Your contributions connect directly to your team's goals. You'll leave with a portfolio of SAP work you can build on. You'll solve real technical problems that improve how the SC Technical Architecture & Coordination team operates day-to-day.
 Build AI-assisted automations that reduce manual effort across team processes
 Develop dashboards that give the team visibility into metrics and project status
 Create reporting tools that support informed decision-making
 Identify and document process improvement opportunities
 Contribute to the team's shared knowledge base on tooling and automation
 Where you belong
 As a working student you will be part of SAP Next Gen, a global network of students and early professionals growing together. You'll access mentors, development programs, and peers building careers alongside you. The SC Technical Architecture & Coordination team brings together architects, Technical Product Managers, and project managers who lead the technical enablement of commercial products for SAP Sovereign Cloud. It's a technically strong environment where your contributions have real impact.
Additional Information
 Please apply initially with your resume in table form. In a later stage we will ask you to upload also school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships).
