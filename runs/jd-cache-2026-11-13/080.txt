Job Description
 What you’ll build
 You'll work on real deliverables, not shadow tasks. Your contributions connect directly to your team's goals. You'll leave with a portfolio of SAP work you can build on
In this role, you will support our team in implementing and maintaining information security measures based on BSI IT-Grundschutz. You will help provision and hand over VS-TOP laptops for secure mobile working. You will also support our Berlin team with guided tours and hands-on infrastructure tasks, including installing and configuring new hardware, adapting existing systems, and documenting technical changes.
 What You’ll Bring
 You're a student ready to contribute alongside your studies. You're curious, adapt quickly, and stay resilient as priorities evolve. You think creatively, communicate clearly, and collaborate well across teams. You're building your foundations in business and AI — and you take initiative to apply them. You bring emotional intelligence and cultural awareness to every interaction.
You are studying computer science, cybersecurity, or a related field and bring practical programming experience with Python. You are familiar with Linux, Git, Docker, and SQL databases. Through university or personal projects, you have gained initial experience in web security, vulnerability analysis, or capture-the-flag competitions. You have a basic understanding of information security management and frameworks such as BSI IT-Grundschutz and ISO 27001. Experience with machine learning, large language models, or building data pipelines for security analysis and alert enrichment is a plus. You enjoy turning technical requirements into working solutions, approach problems analytically, and collaborate well with others. Good written and spoken German and English complete your profile.
 Where you belong
 As a working student you will be part of SAP Next Gen, a global network of students and early professionals growing together. You'll access mentors, development programmes, and peers building careers alongside you. It's a place to start something real.
You will join SAP’s Secure IT Infrastructure team, supporting secure IT operations in Walldorf and Berlin.
