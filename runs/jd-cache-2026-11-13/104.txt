Your job at DKV Mobility? As part of a leading European B2B platform for on-the-road payment solutions, you will work in an exciting environment. Our tasks are defined by our Purpose: To drive the transition towards an efficient and sustainable future of mobility. We offer you numerous opportunities to help shape the mobility of tomorrow in a dynamic and agile environment. Our approximately 2,700 employees throughout Europe are united by a common passion: to give their best every day for our customers in more than 50 countries. True to our claim: You drive, we care. Become part of our diverse, dedicated and unique team!
What you can expect
 You support the Tech Office with strategic and cross-functional topics across the technology organization.
 Your key areas of focus include Post-Merger Integration (PMI), IT Sourcing, and Strategic Partnership Management.
 You support the technological integration of newly acquired companies and assist with the preparation and coordination of integration activities.
 You contribute to IT procurement initiatives, for example by conducting research, preparing supplier information, and comparing proposals.
 You support collaboration with strategic technology partners, prepare partner meetings, and contribute to reporting activities.
 You prepare information and analyses and support the creation of management presentations and decision-making materials.
 You collaborate with various tech teams as well as internal and external stakeholders.
 What makes you stand out
 You are currently enrolled in a degree program in business, engineering, computer science, mathematics, or a comparable field and have a strong academic record.
 You are interested in technology and eager to gain insights into PMI, IT Sourcing, and strategic technology partnerships.
 You have good knowledge of Microsoft 365, particularly Teams, PowerPoint, Excel, SharePoint, and Forms, and ideally have already worked with AI tools such as Microsoft Copilot.
 You work in a structured and independent manner and demonstrate initiative and strong analytical thinking.
 You enjoy collaborating with a variety of stakeholders and have strong communication and teamwork skills.
 Good German and English language skills complete your profile.
 We are looking forward to your application and to applicants who enrich our diverse culture! Regardless of age, gender, origin and sexual identity and orientation. Severely disabled applicants will be given preference if equally qualified. This position can be full-time or part-time (min. 30h/week). DKV Mobility is looking for the best talents and knows that these are not always available on a full-time basis. Many of our employees work part-time or in flexible working models. Please talk to us about the flexibility you need during the application process.
