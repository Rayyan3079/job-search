The Fraunhofer Institute for Open Communication Systems FOKUS is one of the largest ICT institutes within the Fraunhofer Society.
We work in an interdisciplinary manner, with colleagues from more than 30 countries, and we are pleased that an increasing number of women are helping us shape the digital future.
To strengthen our team "Software-based Networks" (NGNI)" we are looking for Student Assistants / Student Workers , starting at the earliest possible date.
 Be part of change
 Support for software development (e.g., Open6GCore, Open5GCore)
 Setup, integration and operation of test environments (5G/6G test networks, virtual networks, other software test environments)
 Research on mobile communication technologies
 Conducting experiments
 Contributing to publications or reports
 What You Contribute
 You are currently enrolled as a student for at least two more semesters in computer science, technical computer science, computer engineering, communications engineering, or a comparable program at a German university
 You have at least a basic knowledge of C and Rust
 You should have some experience working with Linux, Git, VMs/containers, as well as network protocols and mobile networks
 You have good to very good German and English language skills
 You'd like to help shape the future, are open to growth, and you are a teamplayer with good 
 You are available to work on-site at our institute in Berlin-Charlottenburg at least one day a week
 What We Offer
 Flexible working hours and the possibility of hybrid working (with at least one in-person day per week at the institute )
 Barrier-free building with bicycle storage and public transport connections right outside the door
 Discounts on numerous well-known providers through Corporate Benefits
 A practical, study-related activity in an innovative and modern working environment
 Support for your bachelor's or master's thesis
 The working hours are 60 - 80 per month. The remuneration is based on your progress in studies and ranges from € 18.06 to € 18.78 per hour. The contract is for one year. In principle, we are interested in a longer-term collaboration. Your workplace is located at Kaiserin-Augusta-Allee 31, 10589 Berlin.
