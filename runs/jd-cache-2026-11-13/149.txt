Introduction
 At the forefront of health technology for over 35 years, Munich-based Brainlab digitizes medical workflows, from diagnosis to therapy, to offer clinicians and patients better treatment possibilities. Our innovative digital ecosystem forms the basis for modern healthcare technology in 4000 hospitals in 120 countries.
 Our key to success is our creative, talented and hard-working team, which consists of around 2000 dedicated and inspiring individuals in 25 locations worldwide. To succeed in reaching our targets, we are seeking committed colleagues who can stand behind our core values curious, authentic and useful.
 Stellenbeschreibung 
 Would you like to gain practical work experience in an innovative environment? Then we look forward to receiving your online application for a student position!
 Alongside smaller day-to-day tasks, your main responsibilities may include:
 As part of our (embedded) software team in Munich, you will support the development of software for medical technology products.
 Developing and maintaining tools for software development and testing (e.g. in Rust, Python or C/C++)
 Building and extending simulations to support software development and verification
 Developing and automating software tests, including Hardware-in-the-Loop tests
 Supporting integration, debugging and analysis of embedded software
 Documenting tools, tests and results
 Qualifikationen
 Study of computer science, software engineering, electrical engineering, robotics or a related technical subject, ideally beginning your master studies
 Programming experience, ideally in at least one of Rust, Python or C/C++
 Familiarity with Linux, git and related command-line tools is a plus
 Hands-on mentality
 Analytical thinking, team and communication skills
 Conscientious, organized and independent work
 Good knowledge of English; German is a plus
