Connect, learn, and grow with our Working Student Network – your space to exchange ideas, build meaningful relationships, and navigate your Siemens journey.
 You'll make an impact by
 You support senior AI developers in designing, developing, and deploying generative AI-based solutions for software modernization and energy-efficient engineering
 Together with the team, you apply AI agents and agentic workflows to automate code analysis, refactoring, and optimization in existing software projects
 Building on this, you implement and evaluate agent-based solutions for continuous code improvement, including automated testing and requirements management
 Throughout, you document your results and processes to ensure knowledge transfer within the team
 To stay ahead, you continuously monitor advancements in generative AI research and contribute your insights and ideas to our projects
 This Is How You'll Win Us Over
 Education: You are currently enrolled in a master's program in Data Science, Computer Science with a focus on AI/ML, or a closely related field
 Experience and Skills:
 Strong understanding of core concepts in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning
 Proficiency in Python and familiarity with a C-family programming language such as C++, C#, or Java
 Experience with large language models (e.g., GPT, Llama) for code analysis, generation, and optimization
 Ways of Working: You demonstrate excellent analytical skills, attention to detail, and enjoy working collaboratively in a team
 Languages: You have very good written and oral English skills (C1); German is a plus
