THE BEST INTERNSHIP IN THEORY - AND IN PRACTICE.
 SHARE YOUR PASSION.
 World-leading technologies don’t make it into a BMW until they’ve undergone one of the most challenging journeys imaginable. It takes dynamic teams with outstanding technical skills to take them from the drawing board to the road. That’s why our experts will treat you as part of the team from day one, encourage you to bring your own ideas to the table – and give you the opportunity to really show what you can do.
Our team focuses on strengthening digital Self-Care and Self-Service solutions across international markets and customer channels. We aim to make customer support simpler and more accessible by combining data-driven optimization, digital solutions and AI to continuously improve the customer experience.
 What awaits you?
 You will support structured collaboration with our international markets to continuously improve Self-Care and Self-Service performance.
 In addition, you will contribute to tracking agreed measures and preparing analyses and materials for performance discussions and decision-making.
 You will support the consolidation and structuring of analytical data from different dashboards, channels and formats and contribute to the further development of analytics tools and dashboards.
 Furthermore, you will gain insights into AI-supported analytics and automation solutions that simplify data processing, identify relevant insights and make performance information more actionable.
 You will contribute to developing lean and scalable approaches for sharing performance data with markets while reducing complexity and manual effort.
 In addition, you will support the documentation and continuous improvement of internal processes and help identify opportunities for digitalization and automation.
 You will also contribute to digital collaboration and information-management solutions such as SharePoint and support organizational activities as our department continues to develop.
 What should you bring along?
 Current studies in Business Administration, Business Informatics, Data Analytics, Economics, Digital Business, Computer Science or a comparable field.
 Strong analytical and structured thinking combined with an interest in data, performance management and digital customer journeys.
 Ability to translate complex information and data into clear insights and visualizations.
 Strong interest in AI, analytics and automation; initial practical experience with AI-based tools is an advantage.
 Good knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint; experience with Power BI or comparable analytics tools is an advantage.
 Process-oriented mindset and interest in simplifying and improving existing processes and ways of working.
 Proactive and structured working style as well as good communication skills.
 Very good English skills and confidence working in an international and cross-functional environment.
 Are you passionate about data, AI and digital customer experiences and interested in helping make Self-Service solutions even more effective? Apply now and join us in shaping the future of digital customer assistance.
