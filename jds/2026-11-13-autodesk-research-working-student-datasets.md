 Position Overview
 The Research Engineering team at Autodesk Research is seeking a motivated and skilled Research Working Student (Intern) to join the team at our Bonn office.
Candidates based elsewhere may also be considered if they can commute to the Bonn office once or twice per week.
You will contribute to ongoing research on cognitive models grounded in experiential datasets. These models will help predict how people experience designed environments.
 Responsibilities
 Compensation will reflect your experience and skill set.
 Support the design and implementation of cognitive models for human simulation. 
 Aggregate, curate, and standardize trial-level datasets from environmental psychology and behavioral science, focusing on human responses to architectural, interior, and urban spaces (navigation, comfort, affect, memory, wayfinding, and social interaction).
 Identify and collect open-access datasets and published experiments related to environmental psychology and spatial experience.
 Annotate datasets with relevant metadata, such as demographics, context, and spatial features.
 Document the work through academic publications and/or internal communications.
 Minimum Qualifications
 Currently pursuing a master's or PhD degree in environmental psychology, cognitive science, data science, behavioral science, or a related field.
 Ability to learn new technologies quickly and apply them independently. 
 Ability to communicate effectively and work collaboratively. 
 Excellent written and verbal communication skills. 
 Fluency in English. Preferred Qualifications 
 Experience with cognitive architectures, such as Soar, and neuro-symbolic approaches. - Experience with data science tools and methods. - Experience with qualitative and quantitative research methods.
 Familiarity with programming languages such as Python.
