WELCOME TO BASF
 Welcome to the global management consultancy of BASF! We focus on strategic projects that drive the effective transformation of our businesses. Our teams work on a broad range of topics such as sustainability, organizational development, efficiency projects, M&A, and profitable growth through all types of innovation.
We put people first: We are proud of our strong team spirit, and we cherish diversity. Become part of a great international team where your hands-on approach and desire to leave an impact are truly valued. Together, let’s create new ideas and offer the best solutions to our customers.
What You Can Expect
 From 
Januar 2027 on you are part of our Management Consulting department for a period of 
3 - 6 months . The Management Consulting is a team that gives advice to the BASF group and its business units in the field of general management and is therefore actively supporting the change processes of BASF.
 As a full member of the team, you are involved in the preparation, coordination, and implementation of consulting projects for BASF management. 
 You gain exposure to a broad range of topics such as strategy, marketing, organizational design, business optimization, and transformation initiatives. 
 Furthermore, you support the Data Analytics team of our in-house consulting unit and work on data-driven and AI-supported solutions for consulting projects. 
 You realize sophisticated analyses and develop specific tools and applications that support our consultants in their projects, including the use of modern technologies and data-driven approaches. 
What You Offer
 currently enrolled in an advanced degree program in Business, Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, or a comparable field of study
 ideally first relevant work experience in an international business consultancy, analytics, or a similar environment
 knowledge of data management, data analysis, and data science; knowledge of data visualization tools (e.g., Power BI, Dash) is a plus
 proficiency in Python programming and first experience with frontend frameworks like Streamlit or similar technologies
 experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) or initial exposure to AI based applications (e.g., via projects, coursework, or practical use cases)
 structured and analytical way of working combined with a hands-on mindset and curiosity for new technologies
 fluent English skills; German is a plus
