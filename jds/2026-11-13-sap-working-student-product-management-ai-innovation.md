Job Description
 What you’ll bring 
 You are studying and want to understand how AI shapes real products, in practice, not just in theory.
 A genuine interest in AI, digital products, or product management 
 A proactive approach: you ask questions, explore ideas, and see things through 
 Strong ability to structure information and communicate findings clearly 
 Comfort working independently on defined topics 
 A customer-focused mindset, thinking about who uses a product and why 
 Fluency in English is required for this role 
 We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems, whatever stage of your career you're at.
In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools, including agentic AI, to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.
 What You’ll Build
 You will work alongside Product Managers on emerging AI topics, turning early ideas into concrete product concepts.
 Research AI trends and product experiences and translate findings into practical insights for the team 
 Explore AI features and use cases to understand user problems and expected value 
 Shape new AI use cases with Product Managers, from early idea to concept 
 Take ownership of well-scoped topics such as competitive analysis, feature exploration, or evaluation 
 Structure open questions, capture learnings, and follow up on next steps with stakeholders 
 Identify ways to contribute as your knowledge of our products and AI work grows 
 Where you belong 
 You will join an international Product Management team building digital experiences for SAP customers. The team works with engineering and design to shape product capabilities. AI is a growing focus of this work. You will learn how product decisions are made in practice and contribute your own ideas. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen, a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programs, and peers. Onsite presence in Walldorf is expected, during onboarding and in everyday work.
