What To Expect
 Tesla was founded in 2003 with the goal of accelerating the transition to sustainable energy and protecting the planet Earth. Through solar energy, batteries and electric vehicles, we are building a world of amazing abundance.
At Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg, we manufacture the Model Y, and starting in 2027, we’ll produce everything from battery cells to electric vehicles. This is unique in Europe. Our international team consists of over 12,000 employees from 130 countries. We hire talent at all skill levels - prior experience in the automotive industry is not required. Would you like to help write the next chapter in Tesla’s success story and work at one of Germany’s most modern workplaces? Then this is the right place for you!
 Location:  Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg 
 Duration:   6 Months  
 Time Type:  Full-Time 
 Start Date: December 
 The internship assignment : We are the Quality Data Lab, central to the data science and analytics efforts at Giga Berlin. You will be supporting our team in day-to-day business tasks by preparing presentations, workshops and coding task. You will also assist with the development of dashboards and ETL pipelines, code deployment, and be part of problem-solving efforts. Our fast-paced, multicultural environment provides opportunities for data science, analytics and engineering in the manufacturing domain, no matter where your passion lies.
 What You'll Do
 Getting an impression of how to build a Datalab and how to manage data projects
 Invole in coding and developing ETL pipelines so that you can further improve your coding skills
 Work with stakeholders to take a vague problem statement, refine the scope of the analysis, and use the results to drive informed decisions
 Creating meaningful visualizations and preparing presentations 
 Assisting in designing, the development and the deployment of data-driven solutions 
 You will work in close collaboration with other team members on complex problem solving and coding tasks
 What You'll Bring
 Strong proficiency in Python, SQL
 Experience writing software in a professional environment
 Skilled in building data visualizations and dashboards (Dash, plotly, matplotlib, panel…)
 Strong problem-solving skills to help refine problem statements and figure out how to solve them with the available data
 Verbal and written communication skills. Clearly documenting your work is key.
 Smart but humble, with a bias for action
 Able to work in a fast-paced environment while working on multiple tasks at hand
