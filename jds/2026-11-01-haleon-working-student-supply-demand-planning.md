We are looking for a motivated and analytical Working Student to support our Supply & Demand Planning team. You will gain hands-on experience in forecasting, inventory management, supply planning, and reporting while working with different business functions.
 Your Responsibilities
 Support demand forecasting and regular planning cycles
 Analyze sales, consumption, trends, seasonality, and promotions
 Monitor inventory, product availability, and potential supply risks
 Support reporting and preparation of planning analyses and presentations
 Maintain planning data and help improve data quality and processes
 Support ad hoc analyses and Supply & Demand projects
 Your Profile
 Currently studying Business, Supply Chain, Economics, Engineering, Data Analytics, or a related field
 Strong analytical skills and confidence working with numbers
 Good knowledge of Excel; Power BI experience is a plus
 Interest in Supply Chain and Demand Planning
 Structured, proactive, and reliable working style
 Good English skills; German is a plus
 What We Offer
 Hands-on experience across Supply & Demand Planning
 Exposure to forecasting, inventory, supply, and commercial topics
 Opportunity to take ownership and develop your analytical and planning skills
