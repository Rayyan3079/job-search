Some It Works. Some Changes What's Possible.
 SHARE YOUR PASSION.
 More than 90% of automotive innovations are based on electronics and software. That's why creative freedom and lateral thinking are so important in the pursuit of truly novel solutions. That’s why our experts will treat you as part of the team from day one, encourage you to bring your own ideas to the table – and give you the opportunity to really show what you can do.
We believe AI agents have the potential to fundamentally change the way engineering work is done. Our team explores practical applications of AI in engineering and develops solutions that integrate modern technologies into existing workflows, from initial prototypes to practical implementation.
 What awaits you?
 You support our team in identifying engineering workflows that can benefit from AI agents or automation.
 In addition, you contribute to the design and implementation of AI agents that interact with existing tools, APIs, and knowledge sources.
 You gain hands-on experience developing prototypes with Python, LLMs, RAG, tool calling, and agentic AI frameworks.
 Furthermore, you support the evaluation of AI solutions regarding reliability, traceability, and practical value.
 You contribute to documenting solutions and their integration into existing engineering workflows.
 What should you bring along?
 Bachelor's or Master's studies in computer science, AI, software engineering, data science, or a comparable field.
 Good proficiency in Python and experience with REST APIs.
 Interest in or first hands-on experience with LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and tool/function calling.
 Familiarity with GitHub and modern software development practices.
 Curious and hands-on mindset combined with an interest in turning emerging AI technologies into practical solutions.
 Are you passionate about AI and want to gain hands-on experience applying emerging technologies to real engineering challenges? Apply now and help us explore how AI agents can shape the engineering workflows of tomorrow!
